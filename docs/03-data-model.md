# Data model

> Source: generated from the approved consolidation plan (planning session of 2026-09-03; workflow `wf_8edc6cab-3ac`, 44 agents). The evidence behind every claim is under `knowledge/reviews/`. Legal statements carry one of four labels: PRIMARY, SECONDARY, REPO, UNVERIFIED (see `docs/11`).

## Data model

**Conventions on every table.** `id uuid` v7; `tenant_id uuid NOT NULL` with an RLS policy `tenant_id = current_setting('app.tenant_id')::uuid` and inclusion in every unique constraint and index; `created_at timestamptz`; `created_by_id uuid` (no FK) plus `created_by_name text` frozen. User-visible numbers come from `sequences(tenant_id, kind, next)`. Money is `amount_cents bigint` + `currency char(3)`. Legal artifacts also store frozen local-time text with the zone from `locations.timezone`. No soft delete on clinical or financial data; corrections are new rows. Append-only tables additionally carry BEFORE UPDATE/DELETE triggers that raise, and event/log tables carry `prev_hash` / `row_hash` chained per tenant.

**Entity groups** (the full column lists are in the synthesis and go into `docs/03-data-model.md`):

- **Tenancy and identity**: `tenants` → `locations`; `users` (admin rank, clinical role, encrypted MFA secret, hashed recovery codes, watermarks, hire date); `sessions` (device profile operatory | desk, idle deadline, absolute expiry, revoked_at, ip, ua); `role_templates` seeded from Precog; `user_entitlements` as an append-only grant log over Precog's 14 entitlements plus `edit_schedule`, `delete_appointment`, `edit_fee_schedule`, `export_data`, `break_glass`, `clear_variance`; `staff_credentials` with expiries; lifted `auth_throttle` and `password_reset_tokens`.
- **Patients and accounts**: `patients` (per-tenant MRN, encrypted SSN, restricted flag, `retention_until`); `patient_relationships`; `patient_alerts` in its own table so it cannot be collapsed; `guarantor_accounts` + `account_members` (a patient belongs to exactly one active account; every ledger row carries both account and patient); `consents` (decision agreed | declined | deferred | other_option, consenting party and relationship, interpreter, clinical vs marketing scope); medical-history snapshots, medications, allergies; `documents` (object key, SHA-256, virus scan, PHI flag, retention class, legal hold); `records_requests` (due at +10 working days); `retention_holds`; `destruction_log`.
- **Insurance**: carriers, plans with `coverage_rules jsonb`, `patient_coverage` with rank and effective dates, fee schedules and lines (maker-checker), append-only `eligibility_checks` with the frozen 271, `edi_enrollments`.
- **Schedule**: operatories, provider schedules, appointment types (color token + shape token + behavior flags), appointments with the state machine and supervision fields, append-only `appointment_events`, appointment procedures, recall rules and due list, waitlist.
- **Encounters and clinical record**: `encounters` (the spine; status open | signed | amended); `clinical_note_drafts` with OCC; `note_revisions` ring of 20; `clinical_notes_filed` (frozen markdown and audit report, ruleset version, three frozen identities, attestations, omission licences, byteaudit flag, amendment link); `note_amendments`; append-only `chart_events` with a human-set `historical` flag and `chart_current` materialized view; `procedures`; `cdt_codes` under the ADA licence; `perio_exams` → `perio_sites` + `perio_tooth`; treatment plans → phases → items (estimates only, with `rule_trace`); case presentations; prescriptions; anesthesia records → events; imaging studies → images → interpretations.
- **Ledger** (INSERT-only role, hash-chained): `ledger_entries` (kind charge | patient_payment | insurance_payment | adjustment | write_off | refund | transfer_out | transfer_in | reversal; `gl_bucket` patient_ar | ins_ar_primary | ins_ar_secondary | unapplied_credit | undeposited_funds; reason code required for adjustment, write-off, refund, transfer, reversal; frozen poster; reversal links set by trigger; `approval_request_id` required when the gate said needs_second; tender; unique idempotency key per tenant); `payment_allocations` (sum per payment ≤ payment credit and per charge ≤ charge, by trigger); `reason_codes` with `requires_approval_over_cents`; `estimates` never joined into a balance; views `account_balances` (patient due, pending insurance, unapplied, estimated write-off shown separately) and `ledger_explanations`; statements; deposits + lines; `day_closes` frozen in one transaction with a verifier check.
- **Bank**: `bank_accounts`; append-only `bank_transactions` with unique external id and source feed | statement; `reconciliation_runs` with `independent` computed (the closer held no posting or deposit-preparation activity that day) and `lag_days`; matches; variances with frozen clearer and decision link.
- **Claims**: `claims` (status draft | scrubbed | preflight_failed | queued | submitted | acknowledged | pended | paid | denied | appealed | closed | voided; `primary_claim_id` for secondaries; frozen 837), claim lines with CARC/RARC, append-only `claim_events`, `preflight_findings`, attachments, pre-authorizations, `era_files` → `era_payments` → `era_lines` with match status auto | manual | unmatched.
- **Controls** (INSERT-only role): `control_policies` (new row per change), `control_exceptions`, `approval_requests` (frozen evaluation, `CHECK requester_id <> second_approver_id`, decision reason required on decline), `approvals_log`, typed `controls_registry`, `sod_findings` with history, `control_findings`, `control_snapshots` stamped with scoring and rulebook versions, append-only `control_decisions` (remediate | compensate | accept_residual | monitor | insure; `residual_at_decision`; `review_by`), `digest_acks`, PHI-gated `tips`, `scoring_constants`.
- **Audit, events, compliance**: `domain_event` (monthly partitions; namespaced event types auth.* | ledger.* | claim.* | chart.* | control.* | export.* | disclosure.* | role.*; payload holds codes and ids only; `prev_hash` / `hash`; outbox `processed_at`); `phi_access_log` (monthly partitions; purpose enum; justification); `disclosures`; `audit_chain_checks` with Object Lock key; `business_associates` → `baas`; `integration_registry` where `enabled` requires a live BAA row; versioned `policies`; training completions; compliance logs; `incidents` with both TN and HIPAA deadlines; SRA questionnaires and responses; compliance tasks; retention policy; legal holds.
