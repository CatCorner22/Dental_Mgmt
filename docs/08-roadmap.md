# Roadmap

> Source: generated from the approved consolidation plan (planning session of 2026-09-03; workflow `wf_8edc6cab-3ac`, 44 agents). The evidence behind every claim is under `knowledge/reviews/`. Legal statements carry one of four labels: PRIMARY, SECONDARY, REPO, UNVERIFIED (see `docs/11`).

Durations are engineering estimates for a small team with heavy reuse; each phase ends on its exit criteria, not on a date. Sum of the midpoints: about 65 weeks to general availability; the risk register below says 18–24 months is the honest planning range once counsel review, vendor contracts, and pilot recruiting are included.

## Phase 0 — Trust foundation and repo surgery (8–10 weeks)

**Scope.** Seed the monorepo from the dental repo (`apps/pms`, `packages/clinical-core`, `controls-engine`, `db`, `verifier`). Tenants and locations; `tenant_id` + RLS on every table; three DB roles; `SET LOCAL` per transaction; drizzle-kit migrations with history and the shadow-DB CI check replacing `ddl.ts`; per-tenant sequences; UUIDv7. Lift the dental auth stack; add the sessions table, mandatory TOTP with recovery codes, the two-admin recovery ceremony, envelope-encrypted MFA secrets, `withGuard` + `requireAccess` + the CI route-glob test, Origin and Content-Type checks. `domain_event` outbox with hash chain, `phi_access_log`, disclosures, nightly chain verifier, daily chain head to Object Lock, byteaudit retargeted. BAA registry primitive (`integration_registry.enabled` requires a live BAA row). Port the Precog engines into `packages/controls-engine` with demo data replaced by `PracticeState`, the three math bugs fixed (timeline sign, counterfactual ignoring variables, beam search dropping the status quo), the `waive_dual` infinity leak fixed, a versioned constants table, golden and monotonicity tests, and the zero-app-import test. Container deploy (one region + cross-region backups), `pg-boss` worker, managed Postgres with BAA, S3 with BAA, status page, scheduled restore drill. CI gains eslint, `npm audit`, secret scanning, a coverage floor, the e2e probes and Postgres durability tests in the blocking job, axe-core accessibility checks on the Playwright probes, and version guards for `SCORING_VERSION` and `CONTROL_RULEBOOK_VERSION`. Delete gamify, store, Sparkle character content, Gauntlet UI, EDR seam, law-watch product, the precog shell, Grok auth, multiplayer, and the military skin; rotate the committed secret; do not inherit precog history. **In parallel, not on the critical path:** run the report's D.8 Phase 1 research (24–30 persona interviews weighted to office managers; ledger-reading probes on three incumbent ledgers) as a gate on the Phase 1 ledger UI design, with a named owner and a budget, and measure incumbent baselines during those visits (time from ready to filed, perio time and completion rate, checkout clicks, days to tie) so every later exit criterion has a measured baseline rather than an asserted number.

**Phase 0 non-code deliverables** (the critic found each missing): a first-party, PHI-free usage-metrics pipeline derived from `domain_event` and passed through the redactor, so the pilot buy gates are measurable without a third-party analytics vendor; a **legal pack** (vendor-form BAA, pilot agreement with the shadow-ledger enforcement disclosure, terms of service with the published exit and export terms, privacy policy, subprocessor notice, the RPC 5.7 opinion request, an operating entity separate from the law practice, cyber and technology E&O binding), because the Phase 1 pilot cannot lawfully receive PHI without the countersigned BAA and the trust page promises exit terms that do not yet exist; Curve Hero's report-export and API documentation and data-exit terms obtained from the design partner's contract; a **24-month budget and staffing plan** (clearinghouse per-claim and per-eligibility fees, attachment vendor, messaging, cloud, processor, SOC 2, penetration test, counsel, fractional security officer, insurance, CDT licence) against the proposed per-location price, with at least a second engineer and a fractional compliance officer by Phase 2, because the roadmap the risks section sizes at 18–24 months cannot be executed by one person and every compliance cadence item is unstaffed labor until then; a one-page go-to-market for the Phase 1 financial layer (target list of twenty practices, CPA and fraud-examiner referral partners, pricing sheet, demo tenant with synthetic data).

**Exit criteria.** Two tenants seeded; an RLS negative test proves a deliberately missing WHERE clause cannot leak rows; 100% of route handlers pass the guard-coverage test; deactivating a user kills the session on the next request; MFA enrollment is forced on first login; the audit chain verifies nightly and detects a planted tamper in test; a restore drill from last night's backup passes and writes its own audit row; `controls-engine` has zero app imports and every lifted scoring function has a golden test with monotonicity asserted one way; a connector with no BAA row is refused at the registry; production refuses to boot without verify-full `POSTGRES_URL`, KMS key, backup target, object storage, and the append role; no PHI held yet; **the vendor's own security risk analysis is complete and signed off, the pilot practice's BAA is countersigned, and the incident-response plan and revoke-all runbook are written and drilled once on staging**, because the first Phase 1 import (patient names plus balances) is PHI; the legal pack is executed or ready to execute, the budget and staffing plan is approved, and the usage-metrics pipeline is emitting redacted events; D.8 interviews synthesized and the ledger default view (running vs itemized) chosen from measured preference.

**Dependencies.** None on earlier phases. Owner decisions needed first: managed Postgres and object storage vendor with BAA; stay on NextAuth Credentials + TOTP; hosting vendor; pricing model for the Phase 1 financial layer.

## Phase 1 — Money spine and enforced controls, piloted beside the incumbent PMS (12–14 weeks)

**Scope.** Patients, guarantor accounts, and coverage as minimal headers. The readable ledger with the property-test suite (dual coverage, partial payments, secondary posting, reversals, refunds) written before any UI; DB-enforced invariants; reason codes under maker-checker; estimates table; "Explain this balance"; statements; day close frozen atomically; hosted-field card processing. `postGuarded()` enforcing `evaluateRelease` inside every money-moving transaction; approval-requests inbox; control policies and exceptions editor with expiry summary; SoD detection over real `user_entitlements` with grant-time refusal and the setup-wizard conflict preview; deposits and bank reconciliation (statement import first; aggregator feed enabled per tenant when its agreement is signed) with the variance queue and the named runtime SoD set; control snapshots, COSO and residual scores with measured effectiveness, decision journal, hard-event alerts, weekly digest, tip channel. Report-import ETL of day sheets, AR aging, deposit slips, and patient and coverage headers from **Curve Hero first** (the design partner, Cornerstone, runs Curve Hero; the critic found the plan had no Curve path anywhere), then Open Dental and Dentrix, so one pilot practice runs the ledger and controls in parallel with its incumbent without double entry. In-house membership plans (`membership_plans`: fee, renewal, covered procedures, discount rules), recurring payment plans (`payment_plans`: schedule, auto-charge through the processor token), and third-party financing (CareCredit or Sunbit class, with merchant fees as typed, reason-coded adjustments) are in the ledger property-test suite from the start, because they are the daily events that produce the "transfer adjustment" confusion the research describes and the signature ledger would otherwise fail on the first uninsured patient. A month-end accounting package for the CPA seat: a general-ledger journal export mapped from `gl_bucket` and reason codes to QuickBooks and Xero import formats, a deposit register, and an AR roll-forward, so the independent reconciler has something to review without re-keying. A responsive approvals-and-alerts surface (installable web app) with step-up MFA, so the second approver, usually the owner in another operatory or offsite, can act from a phone within minutes; without it dual release becomes a bottleneck and gets waived. Trust page with published pricing and exit terms. The recorded-vs-enforced table in-product. The pilot agreement states plainly that Phase 1 dual release enforces on the shadow ledger fed by import, not on the incumbent's posting path.

**Exit criteria.** The pilot practice's opening AR ties to the incumbent to the cent for 30 consecutive business days; every over-threshold refund or write-off in the shadow ledger has a second named approver or a logged owner waiver, with zero exceptions found by the independent verifier; the owner clears reconciliation daily with median variance investigation under 10 minutes; a planted $300 skim (deposit short + after-hours adjustment) surfaces as a variance and a finding within one business day in a simulated month; the D.8 ledger-reading probe with at least five billers shows higher task success than the incumbent ledger; at least one real SoD conflict is detected from grants and closed by a decision; zero ledger rows updated or deleted (role + trigger test); scores reproduce byte-identically for a frozen snapshot; the pilot office manager says on a recorded interview that they would choose this ledger; the month-end package imports into the pilot's accounting file without manual re-keying and the CPA confirms it; a second approver has approved a held posting from a phone in under five minutes in the simulated month; the financial layer is priceable and sold to at least one non-pilot practice as a standalone.

**Dependencies.** Phase 0, including its PHI-readiness exit criteria (vendor SRA, countersigned pilot BAA, encryption, RLS, PHI access log, incident plan), because the report-import shadow ledger is PHI from its first row. Bank aggregator agreement or the statement-import path; card processor with hosted vault (and its BAA, per the vendor table); CPA-reviewed default thresholds and reason-code catalog; Curve Hero's report-export or API documentation and its data-exit terms, obtained from the design partner's contract in Phase 0; the pilot practice as **one tenant covering all three Cornerstone locations** (a one-location pilot of a three-location practice would generate ledger and reconciliation variances that are artifacts of the pilot boundary, because staff rotate and patients are seen at any site, and would contaminate the very metric Phase 1 validates) or, if that is too large, a true single-location practice recruited through the D.8 interviews; a support-operations plan (hours, channels, BAA-covered ticketing, on-call rotation, published response targets) staffed before the pilot posts money in the product. **Start now for Phase 2:** clearinghouse contract and BAA, and per-payer EDI enrollment (up to 30 business days per payer).

## Phase 2 — Board, encounters, insurance, and claims: the product becomes a PMS (14–16 weeks)

**Scope.** Scheduling Board with operatories, appointment-type behavior contracts, provider templates, recall and waitlist, supervision validation at booking, check-in and checkout flows, per-chair status strip. Encounters and procedures (CDT licence loaded) so charges have structural parents. Fee schedules per plan per provider. One clearinghouse adapter for 270/271 eligibility with the 6 am sweep and arrival re-run. Claim assembly with a scrubber consuming the justification and completeness rules; secondary auto-generation; batch 837D with frozen bytes; claims tracker; 835 review-then-post through `postGuarded` producing insurance-payment and contractual write-off entries; denial and appeal worklists; denial-suppression detection; pre-authorizations. Money Desk home. Canned reports v1 over ledger views. Read-only public API v1 for the practice's accountant. Conversion: a **Curve Hero mapper first** (the design partner's incumbent), then the Open Dental full importer, each with the conversion cockpit and the public "what does not convert" list; EDI re-enrollment runbook. Provider-payer credentialing (`provider_payer_credentials`: rendering vs billing provider, NPI, TIN, taxonomy, per-payer participation status, expiries) alongside fee schedules, because out-of-network posting under an in-network fee schedule mis-states patient responsibility and estimated write-offs. A claim-attachment vendor (NEA FastAttach or Vyne) chosen with the clearinghouse, with attachment presence a scrubber rule for attachment-required CDT codes (SRP, crowns, most appeals). First-party appointment confirmations and reminders, moved up from Phase 4 through a BAA-covered messaging vendor, so a practice that converts in this phase does not lose confirmations (a must-have regression and a no-show cost) when it leaves Weave, RevenueWell, or NexHealth; the comms-partner API stays in Phase 5. TennCare: record decision 22 (ship Medicaid claim and documentation rules in this phase, or exclude Medicaid-heavy practices at launch and say so on the trust page). Engage the SOC 2 auditor now so the observation period overlaps the build.

**Exit criteria.** The pilot practice runs scheduling, billing, and claims entirely in the PMS for 60 days with the incumbent switched off for billing; first-pass clearinghouse acceptance ≥95% and days-to-payment at or better than the prior 90-day baseline; ERA auto-post ≥85–90% of clean remittance lines with every unmatched line surfaced amber; eligibility on the Board for ≥90–95% of tomorrow's insured appointments by 6 am; check-in in 1 tap, checkout in ≤4 clicks on one screen, scheduling in 3 interactions (moderated test with a coordinator and a biller); the dual-coverage + partial-payment scenario posts end to end and "Explain this balance" renders it; conversion from Open Dental completes with AR tie-out and zero orphaned claims; a second practice converted at the published fixed price; every report total reconciles to ledger views in an automated check.

**Dependencies.** Phase 1. Clearinghouse and attachment-vendor contracts and BAAs; payer enrollment; ADA CDT licence (and the currently adopted CDT version); messaging vendor BAA; the TennCare decision; the ONC-certification decision and the health-information-network self-assessment recorded before API v1 is published; the support-operations plan staffed.

## Phase 3 — Clinical record (14–16 weeks)

**Scope.** Port the Smile Notes clinical core from `packages/clinical-core` into the Encounter page: note drafts and filed notes with NOT NULL encounter FK, three identities, `signNoteAtomic`, byteaudit verification, addendum chain, attestation tiers, omission licences, module field-id deprecation registry with round-trip test, jurisdiction parameter, PHI rules → egress classifier, about 100 EDR strings removed, `BuilderShell.tsx` decomposed with `runTextAudit` kept pure. Editable odontogram on `chart_events` with history layer and paint-to-chart macros writing chart, plan, note scaffold, and pending charge. Treatment plans with phases, estimates, and case presentation; conversion gated on encounter completion. Six-point perio with the keyboard/foot-pedal grammar and prior-exam deltas. Consents, medications, allergies, medical-history snapshots, anesthesia timeline. Documents in object storage with signed URLs, **plus DICOM and JPEG image import with a reference viewer**, so radiographs and their dentist interpretations live in the encounter during the clinical pilot (the sensor bridge follows in Phase 4; without an in-encounter image the pilot would re-create the two-application hop the merge exists to remove, and Tennessee's record rule counts radiographs and interpretations as record components). Lab cases (`lab_cases`: encounter and procedure FK, lab vendor, sent, due, received, shade and material, fee posted to the ledger) with a Board warning when a seat appointment precedes the case's due date, and lab vendors wired into the vendor-master dual-release channel (lab invoices are a recurring fictitious-vendor fraud vector). Referrals (`referrals`, `referral_packets` with the four-slot packet gate from the oral-surgery panel in the knowledge base, a specialist directory, loop-closure tracking, because referral loop failure is a named malpractice pattern) and a letters-and-correspondence merge module, every send a disclosure row. A recommended operatory device list and mobile-device-management profile (PIN unlock, kiosk mode). Hygienist and dentist homes. Shared-device profile with PIN author switch and encrypted-or-disabled draft mirror. Records-request workflow with SLA clock and retention clocks. Read-only degraded mode (service worker + encrypted read cache of today's schedule, alerts, and chart summaries; disabled on shared devices). Dictation through the engine seam with browser SpeechRecognition blocked on PHI fields.

**Exit criteria.** Full-mouth perio by one operator at or under the incumbent's median time, measured on five to ten hygienists during the D.8 probes (the 8-minute figure from the design is a provisional target until that baseline exists; the underlying 60%-skip statistic is a vendor survey with undisclosed sample size), with completion rate up versus the pilot's baseline; a keyboard-only end-to-end test passes for all five daily flows and the accessibility checks in CI stay green; a radiograph imported into an encounter opens from the tooth on the odontogram and carries a dentist's interpretation; a lab case due after its seat appointment raises the Board warning; a restorative visit charted and signed in ≤10 taps; every filed note reproduces byte-identically from frozen artifacts and an addendum never alters the original; the precision harness shows zero blocking false positives on the 34-note corpus after the PHI re-scope; a draft saved under an older module set round-trips; wrong-site and chart/note/claim contradictions fire as S0; zero cleartext PHI on a shared device after logout (inspection); zero wrong-author events on shared tablets; pilot buy gates instrumented (≥70% of eligible charts in the PMS by week 4; median ready → filed ≤ baseline + 20%, target ≤ 0 by week 8; ≤90 minutes paid training per writer); usability sessions observed with a hygienist and an assistant; the degraded-mode tabletop passes (board and chart visible, no writes accepted, banner shown); Tennessee counsel review of the rule set and consent fields started.

**Dependencies.** Phase 2 (encounters and procedures exist). The speech-engine decision recorded (on-device Whisper WASM with a frozen dental word-error-rate corpus vs a BAA vendor) for Phase 5. A 1–3 dentist practice recruited as the clinical pilot.

## Phase 4 — Hardening, general availability, compliance program, communications, forensics (12–14 weeks)

**Scope.** Third-party penetration test; SOC 2 Type 2 fieldwork; a HIPAA security risk analysis performed on the PMS itself (documenting the cleartext name and DOB decision); nonce-based CSP; key-rotation runbook; incident-response drill; hosting promoted to two-region active/passive; PgBouncer and a short-TTL session cache with revocation invalidation if latency requires. HIPAA/OSHA program after counsel review (SRA questionnaire → tailored versioned policies → remediation tasks → server-verified training with certificates; BAA document management on the Phase 0 registry; sterilizer and equipment logs; incident clocks; annual SRA reminder). Recall campaigns (confirmations and reminders already shipped in Phase 2). Imaging sensor bridge (TWAIN and DICOM) with a published certified-hardware list, viewer upgrade, one-click DICOM export. A BAA-covered eFax vendor in the registry for referrals and records requests. A payroll-provider read integration (Gusto or ADP class) so the payroll dual-release channel gates real approval events; until it exists the channel is shown as "external / attested" on the recorded-vs-enforced table and excluded from scores, never as enforced. A VPAT and accessibility statement at GA. Forensic suite (Benford, round-number, duplicates, adjustment velocity) as owner-private control-test signals. Reporting filter builder and scheduled delivery. Bank-aggregator hardening or statement-import polish based on Phase 1 telemetry. Dentrix and Eaglesoft CSV + image-folder importers. Marketing that never claims "HIPAA compliant," "lawsuit-proof," "board-proof," or "AI-powered."

**Exit criteria.** Pen-test findings closed; SOC 2 Type 2 observation period complete or report issued; three paying practices live with 90-day retention; published uptime ≥99.9% over the prior quarter with post-mortems; compliance state recalculates as risks open and close; no integration enables without a live BAA (test); a records request fulfilled as a full-record export within ten working days in a drill; Tennessee counsel sign-off on policy templates and legal content; owner-panel buy gates held across all live practices; no per-person ranking anywhere (review checklist); the second and third practices onboarded using only the published conversion price and timeline.

**Dependencies.** Phase 3. Counsel review; the SOC 2 auditor engaged in Phase 2; messaging vendor BAA.

## Phase 5 — Growth: voice perio, caged AI, portal, eRx, groups, API write, offline decision (ongoing; first 16 weeks after GA, gated on demand)

**Scope.** Voice perio and dictation through the `DictationEngine` seam once the on-device engine or a BAA speech vendor clears the frozen word-error-rate corpus. Caged AI assist behind a BAA-covered zero-retention provider with the field-level minimum-necessary gate, `verifyMeaning` on every output, per-call disclosure rows, a controls coach that sees role labels only, included in the price. Patient portal (separate identity realm, plain-language and stigma delivery gate, guardian voice), two-way texting, online booking, digital intake. eRx through a certified vendor with medication-safety pre-flight. Organization tier for 2–9 location groups (location-scoped financial access, cross-location clinical grants, org catalogs with inheritance, consolidated reconciliation, SAML/OIDC + SCIM). Public API v1 write access, HMAC webhooks, sandbox tenants; FHIR R4 subset. Bounded offline capture (queued clinical notes and perio with explicit human reconciliation on reconnect, never money or claims) designed and shipped only if degraded-mode telemetry shows demand, with the decision recorded in an ADR with measured outage minutes. Native imaging acquisition evaluated as a regulatory project. Per-state jurisdiction packs beyond Tennessee. Specialty scoping decision (native or a public "not in scope").

**Exit criteria.** Voice perio word-error rate on the frozen corpus meets the bar before enablement; AI refusal rate has a denominator, model identity is logged on every call, and a redaction test verifies zero PHI in provider logs; the portal blocks 100% of summaries with open plain-language findings; the first 2–9 location group is live with location-scoped money and cross-location clinical cover, and SCIM deprovisioning revokes grants and sessions within one minute; the API is used by one external accountant or partner in sandbox; twelve months of published uptime; the offline ADR is recorded.

**Dependencies.** Phase 4 GA. BAAs with the speech, model, eRx, and messaging vendors; a signed group customer; a per-state legal-research budget.

## Sequencing contingencies (what changes if a gate fails)

- **Phase 1 ledger probe fails** (billers do not out-perform the incumbent ledger): do not proceed to Phase 2 on the same ledger UI. Re-run the D.8 reaction test on the alternative default view; if both fail, the ledger model itself is wrong and the plan reverts to option (b) of decision 2 (clinical record first) while the ledger is redesigned with a CPA and two billers in the room.
- **Clearinghouse contract or payer enrollment slips**: Phase 2 splits. Board, encounters, procedures, fee schedules, Money Desk, reports, and the Open Dental importer proceed; claims and ERA posting slide behind the contract. The pilot keeps billing in the incumbent until claims land.
- **Bank aggregator agreement never signs**: the statement importer is the permanent floor; the reconciliation screen states "statement import" as its source and the design-effectiveness score for independent reconciliation stays capped below the feed-backed value.
- **Cornerstone cannot pilot all three locations under one tenant**: do not fall back to one location (the reconciliation metric would be contaminated by pilot-boundary artifacts); recruit the 1–3 dentist practice from the D.8 interviews for Phase 1 as well, and move Cornerstone to the Phase 5 group tier.
- **Curve Hero export access is refused or its exit terms are punitive**: the Phase 1 report-import ETL cannot be built against the design partner; recruit an Open Dental practice (public schema, importer already planned) as the Phase 1 partner and keep Cornerstone for a later conversion once the terms are known.
- **The budget and staffing plan (decision 23) does not close**: shorten the plan to Phase 0 plus Phase 1 only, sell the financial layer standalone, and re-decide the PMS phases against pilot revenue rather than committing to Phases 2–4 on the stated durations.
- **Counsel review of compliance content slips**: the HIPAA/OSHA program ships with every template labelled provisional and the module hidden behind a tenant flag; Phase 4 GA proceeds without it.
- **SOC 2 observation period runs past GA**: publish the pen-test summary, the SRA, and the incident policy on the Trust page and say plainly that the SOC 2 report is pending; early buyers are asked to trust those artifacts for up to a year.
- **A Phase 0 boot guard or RLS negative test cannot pass on the chosen Postgres vendor**: change vendor before Phase 1, not after; no PHI is held in Phase 0, so the switch is cheap.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation built into the plan |
|---|---|
| The readable ledger is the central bet and the market's observed success rate is zero (Open Dental, CareStack, Curve, and Oryx each shipped ledgers their own billers hate) | Property-test the allocation model before any UI; choose the default view from the D.8 reaction test; the biller/CPA ledger-reading probe is a Phase 1 exit gate; exactly one ledger view; pilot beside an incumbent so failure is cheap and visible |
| The Phase 1 pilot enforces on a mirror: refunds and write-offs are still posted in the incumbent, so dual release runs over the shadow ledger and the new tool's own roles | Say so in the pilot agreement and the exit criteria; scope the report-import ETL as its own small conversion project |
| Clearinghouse and payer connectivity is the largest build risk and the top buying criterion; EDI re-enrollment takes up to 30 business days per payer | One adapter, one vendor; contract and enrollments started a full phase early; ERA review-then-post never silent; payer coverage published honestly |
| Holding PHI is a step change in liability from Smile Notes' de-identified posture (breach economics, OCR and TN notification, FTC exposure on every security claim per the Dentrix G5 precedent, PCI scope, a subprocessor inventory each needing a BAA) | Everything both repos got "for free" from not holding PHI is re-derived in the security plan; the adversarial panels in the dental knowledge base are the acceptance criteria |
| Enforced dual release trades speed for trust; a rigid threshold gets disabled the first week, and a one-owner, one-office-manager practice has a two-person approver pool | Exceptions ship with the gate; thresholds set with the CPA at onboarding; the owner can second anything; mobile one-tap approval; MFA step-up only above a high-value band |
| Bank reconciliation depends on data from outside the PMS (aggregator relationship, its agreement, small-bank coverage) | Statement import is the permanent floor; the "green" state is honestly downgraded when the feed is absent; the reconciliation logic lives in the sealed verifier |
| Solo-owner capacity: both repos are single-author, and this is 18–24 months of work for a small team even with reuse | Phase 1 exists to earn revenue and a falsifier before the full PMS is built; everything after Phase 3 is resequenced against pilot data |
| Deferring the clinical record to Phase 3 idles the most mature asset for about a year while incumbents ship voice perio and eligibility | Accepted deliberately in exchange for testing the novel thesis first; the keyboard perio grammar and the speech-engine seam limit the window loss |
| Precog's math has known defects and zero tests; its multipliers and priors are illustrative; shipping them as actuarial numbers is a liability for an attorney-owned product | Fix on port with golden and monotonicity tests; versioned constants table; "directional" framing until calibrated; measured effectiveness wherever the PMS has the data |
| RLS + fresh-row authorization + PHI access logging + hash chaining on every request adds latency | Acceptable at single-practice scale; short-TTL session cache with revocation invalidation, partitioned log tables, PgBouncer, and read replicas before group scale |
| Field-level encryption vs searchability: names and DOB stay cleartext for search | Documented as an explicit choice in the product's own SRA; SSNs, member ids, MFA secrets, and bank identifiers are encrypted |
| Hiring-pool familiarity with Dentrix and Eaglesoft cannot be met by a new entrant | Command palette, role-before-work, temp quick-start, free certification; measured in pilot, not asserted |
| Migration is the acquisition cost (imaging conversion, in-flight secondary claims, non-converting benefits, EDI re-enrollment); Eaglesoft's proprietary imaging may be unbridgeable | Conversion is a product with a public fixed price and a public "does not convert" list; tie-out-or-refuse go-live |
| Tennessee-only legal content limits reach; no counsel or carrier has reviewed the existing content | Sell in Tennessee first; per-state packs are a budgeted legal-research cost |
| Pricing anchors are low and the controls module cannot carry standalone revenue above roughly $100 per month | The Phase 1 wedge validates but funds little; the rate card must be sustainable in the $149–$700 per location PMS band |
| Well-capitalized incumbents are moving on the same gaps | The controls-and-reconciliation wedge is the part they are least likely to copy because it requires admitting their ledgers are the problem |
| Owners suspecting theft will ask for per-person adjustment reports; the no-scoreboard doctrine reads as a missing feature | Hold the line; private, evidence-linked signals and a decision journal for the owner and a reviewer seat; it is also the liability posture |
| Keeping NextAuth v5 beta is a beta dependency in a PHI system | Pin the version; all session logic behind `guards.ts` and the sessions table; revisit at Phase 4 |
| Both source repos have effectively no commit history, so "battle-tested" claims about individual fixes cannot be bisected | The test suites and e2e probes are the evidence; the Precog engines have none until Phase 0 adds them |
| Re-scoping the PHI rules from "block filing" to "outbound boundary" removes the premise every safety argument in the dental knowledge base rests on and touches about 100 strings, the precision harness, and the byteaudit contract | Phase 3 is sized for it; keep `runTextAudit` pure and pass context as arguments |
| Unit economics and staffing are unmodeled: clearinghouse, attachment, messaging, cloud, processor, SOC 2, pen test, counsel, security officer, insurance, and CDT licence costs against a one-per-location published price, executed by a team of one | Decision 23 makes the 24-month budget and staffing plan a Phase 0 deliverable and a gate on the Phase 1 durations; price the Phase 1 layer only after the cost model exists |
| The design partner's incumbent is Curve Hero, whose data-exit terms (90-day notice, remaining term owed per the research) and export access are outside the team's control | Obtain Curve's export or API documentation and exit terms from the partner's contract in Phase 0; build the Curve report-export ETL first; if access is refused or the terms are punitive, recruit an Open Dental practice as the Phase 1 partner |
| Every phase exit threshold (perio minutes, first-pass acceptance, ERA auto-post, eligibility coverage, click counts) is a target, not a measured baseline | The D.8 visits measure incumbent baselines and the first-party metrics pipeline computes the metrics; thresholds are re-set relative to baseline at Phase 0 exit |
