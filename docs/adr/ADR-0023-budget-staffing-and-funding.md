# ADR 23: Budget, staffing, and funding

**Status:** Proposed (awaiting owner confirmation)

## Context

See `docs/10-decisions-for-owner.md`, row 23, and the design and security documents this decision affects.

## Options

(a) commit to the roadmap durations only after a 24-month budget and staffing plan exists (second engineer and fractional compliance officer by Phase 2; support operations before the pilot bills in the product); (b) proceed solo on the stated durations

## Recommendation

(a)

## Why

Both source repos are single-author; the risk register sizes the work at 18–24 months for a small team; every compliance cadence item is unstaffed labor until someone is named; the published rate card is the cheapest differentiator only if the cost model shows it is sustainable. This is the decision the rest of the roadmap depends on
