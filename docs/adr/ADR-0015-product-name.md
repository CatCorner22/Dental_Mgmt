# ADR 15: Product name

**Status:** Proposed (awaiting owner confirmation)

## Context

See `docs/10-decisions-for-owner.md`, row 15, and the design and security documents this decision affects.

## Options

Thirty candidates in three registers (clinical precision; modern software; warmth); six pass every criterion: Daybook, Onefold, Denote, Wellkept, Chartroom, Evident

## Recommendation

Retire "Precog Pioneer" and "Smile Notes" as product names; send **Daybook**, **Onefold**, and **Denote** to trademark counsel for a Class 9/42 knock-out search in that order, with **Wellkept** as the warm alternative; keep "Smile Notes" at most as an internal module label after the same search

## Why

"Precog" and the military vocabulary read as surveillance in exactly the setting where an owner suspects staff; "Smile" is the most contested prefix in dental marks; Daybook names the artifact (the day sheet matching the bank) that is the product's central promise and was proposed independently by two agents. Your call is the register: money-truth (Daybook, Onefold, Denote), warmth (Wellkept), or clinical (Chartroom, Evident)
