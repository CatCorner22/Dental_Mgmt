# ADR 18: Pricing and packaging of the Phase 1 financial layer

**Status:** Proposed (awaiting owner confirmation)

## Context

See `docs/10-decisions-for-owner.md`, row 18, and the design and security documents this decision affects.

## Options

(a) standalone controls-and-reconciliation product in the $39–$115 per month product-led band, credited toward the PMS price on conversion; (b) an annual $499–$1,200 CPA-channel price; (c) pilot only, not sold

## Recommendation

(a), priced near the top of the band per location, credited toward the PMS, with the PMS itself priced as one per-location fee with unlimited users

## Why

The controls module cannot carry standalone revenue above roughly $100 per month, so it validates the thesis and funds little; but a published price, a real invoice, and a real churn number are the falsifier the roadmap needs, and the credit makes it the wedge into the switch. Whether the number is $79 or $149 is your call; the rate card must be sustainable in the $149–$700 per location PMS band
