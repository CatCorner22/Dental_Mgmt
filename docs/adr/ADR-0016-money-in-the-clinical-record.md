# ADR 16: Money in the clinical record

**Status:** Proposed (awaiting owner confirmation)

## Context

See `docs/10-decisions-for-owner.md`, row 16, and the design and security documents this decision affects.

## Options

exclude entirely vs separate surfaces

## Recommendation

Separate surfaces: money on plan cards and the ledger, never inside note text

## Why

Resolves the practice's own "money stays out of the clinical record" instruction with Curve's case-acceptance evidence
