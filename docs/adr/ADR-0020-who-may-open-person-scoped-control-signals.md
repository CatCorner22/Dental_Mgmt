# ADR 20: Who may open person-scoped control signals

**Status:** Proposed (awaiting owner confirmation)

## Context

See `docs/10-decisions-for-owner.md`, row 20, and the design and security documents this decision affects.

## Options

(a) owner only; (b) owner plus a designated external reviewer/CPA seat; (c) owner plus office manager

## Recommendation

(b), never the office manager by default

## Why

The CPA seat is what makes the decision journal an evidence pack a carrier or Board investigator accepts, and that person is the most likely independent reconciler; the office manager is the most common embezzler profile in the research and must not review signals about themselves
