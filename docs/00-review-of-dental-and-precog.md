# Review of the dental (Smile Notes) and precog (Precog Pioneer) repositories

> Source: generated from the approved consolidation plan (planning session of 2026-09-03; workflow `wf_8edc6cab-3ac`, 44 agents). The evidence behind every claim is under `knowledge/reviews/`. Legal statements carry one of four labels: PRIMARY, SECONDARY, REPO, UNVERIFIED (see `docs/11`).

## What was verified directly

## dental (Smile Notes)

- **Premise to reverse.** The product holds no patient identifiers by design; `knowledge/benchmarks/smile-notes-vs-curve-hero.md` lists scheduling, billing, claims, treatment plans, portal, recare, imaging, odontogram-of-record, perio charting, patient/visit/procedure objects as "deliberately not building." A PMS reverses every one of those decisions, so the de-identification premise (PHI gate that *blocks* identifiers) must become a PHI *custody* model (identifiers are first-class, protected data).
- **Schema** (`src/lib/db/schema.ts`): `users` (role enum readonly/user/lead/manager/admin; separate `clinical_role` axis; `mfa_secret` stored as plaintext base32; session watermarks `password_changed_at` / `sessions_revoked_at`; SoD columns `email_changed_by`, `created_by_id`), `offices`, `user_offices`, `drafts` (jsonb `note_state`, optimistic `version`), `draft_revisions` (capped ring), `submissions` (frozen `note_markdown`, `audit_report`, `rule_version`, `gpa`, `assist_provenance`, `filing_rollup`), `points_ledger` (append-only), `store_items`, `redemptions`, `user_blocks`, `audit_log` (frozen actor name, no FK), `auth_throttle` (DB-backed lockout), `password_reset_tokens` (hash only), `wishes`, `practice_packs` + `practice_pack_events`. **Single practice, no tenant column, no patient entity.**
- **Authorization** (`src/lib/auth/roles.ts`, `guards.ts`): rank ladder + target-keyed capability predicates; `requireRole()` re-reads role/active from DB per request, enforces session revocation watermark and legal-notice acknowledgment. 37 of 41 API routes call `requireRole`; the 4 that do not are auth, setup, reset (public by design) and the cron-secret law-watch alert. **Production-grade; liftable.**
- **Clinical scope axis** (`src/lib/auth/clinicalRoles.ts`, `src/lib/law/license-scope.ts`): assistant/hygienist/dentist scope from Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460; dentist-owned Assessment/Plan sections; Public Chapter 1107 (2026) effective-dated supervision rule. **Unique domain IP.**
- **Audit engine + vocabulary** (`src/lib/audit/**`, `src/lib/vocab/**`, `src/lib/modules/**`): S0–S4 severities, ADA Universal tooth validation across four dentitions, surface legality, PHI heuristics, medication-safety gates, anticipatory completeness, 31 add-on modules + Universal Core, 94.6% shorthand clause coverage (ratcheted in CI), `RULESET_VERSION` 2.25.2 stamped on every filing with a CI guard that fails a PR when rules change without a bump. **Production-grade domain IP; the strongest asset in either repo.**
- **AI posture** (`docs/model-charter.md`): deterministic core; optional Level-0 assist behind PHI gate with `verifyMeaning()` refusing any output that changes clinical substance; "training on filed notes: hard no." Keep this charter as the PMS AI policy.
- **Migrations**: hand-rolled idempotent DDL in `src/lib/db/ddl.ts` gated by `SCHEMA_BOOT_VERSION` (currently 2), separate from Drizzle's `drizzle/` folder (comment says "keep in sync"). Two sources of schema truth — a weakness to fix in the merged product.
- **Known HIPAA exposure recorded in the repo's own red-team files** (`knowledge/sources/adversarial-it-hipaa-security.md`, `adversarial-privacy-hipaa-attorney-hate.md`): MFA default-off (`MFA_ENABLED`), long-lived sessions on shared tablets (the corpus says about 30 days; the shipped `auth.config.ts` actually sets 12 hours with a 15-minute refresh, so the real exposure is the dismissible client-only idle lock, not the token lifetime), clipboard as egress, plaintext IndexedDB/localStorage draft mirrors, S2 bare-name findings still exportable, title → emailed filename. Each becomes a requirement in the security plan.
- **Tests/CI**: `npx tsc --noEmit` → `npm test` (1,636 tests across 98 files per charter; 201 test files now) → `npm run build`; cross-browser Playwright smoke (Chromium/Firefox/WebKit) non-blocking. Deploy target Vercel + Neon/Vercel Postgres; production refuses to boot without `POSTGRES_URL`.

## C. What the merge structurally fixes

- The front desk's "I cannot see note-in-Curve from the board" and the clipboard-as-egress problem both disappear when the PMS owns the chart: no second app, no paste, and the two-identifier chart check becomes a real technical control.
- Precog's COSO Principle 9 ("assess change") and Principle 11 ("technology general controls") are hard-coded weak because the standalone tool cannot see role grants; in a PMS every role change and termination is an observed control event.
- A standalone controls product (Zeldent, Prosperident) can only observe; a PMS can **prevent**: every SoD conflict maps to a permission the PMS grants and every dual-release rule maps to a transaction the PMS executes.
- The insurance auditor's core complaint (code and finding never matched because they live in two systems) is answerable only when the note and the claim share a data model.
