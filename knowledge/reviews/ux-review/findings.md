# Findings of the UI and UX review

Registered heuristics: `registered-heuristics.md` beside this file. Method and results: `docs/16-ux-review.md`.

Every row below is a root cause: one registered heuristic, breached on one or more screens. Ten screen auditors produced 350 screen-level breach rows against the 117 registered heuristics; those collapse into the 87 root causes here. Each was then put to an adversarial verifier whose default position was *refuted* and who re-measured from a clean page rather than accepting the auditor's number. The `Verdict` column is that verifier's, and the `Measured` column is the verifier's own measurement, not the auditor's.

**47 confirmed · 37 confirmed with a correction to scope or numbers · 3 refuted · 0 could not be measured.** Severity: 17 P1, 36 P2, 34 P3. A verifier could lower a severity but never raise it.
