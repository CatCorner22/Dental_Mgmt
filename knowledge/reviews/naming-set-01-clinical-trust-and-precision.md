## Summary

Ten candidate names from the angle "clinical trust and precision", each with rationale, tagline, spelling risk, domain ideas, and recalled conflicts.

## Angle

clinical trust and precision (names that sound like a system a dentist and an auditor both trust; evoke charting, records, clarity, accountability)

## Names


### Item 1
- **name**: Denote
**rationale**

A real verb meaning to mark or indicate precisely, which is what a clinician does in a chart and what an auditor does with an exception. It also reads as Dent + Note, quietly carrying forward the Smile Notes lineage (standardized clinical notes, deterministic audit engine) into a full PMS without saying 'dental' or 'notes' outright. Sounds like an established word, not a coined mashup, which helps the trust angle.

- **tagline**: Chart it. Post it. Prove it.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Low. Two syllables, dictionary spelling. Slight risk of being heard as 'the note' on the phone; no misspelling pattern.

#### domain ideas
- denote.dental
- denotehq.com
- getdenote.com
- denotepms.com
- denote.health
- **known conflicts from memory**: denote.com is almost certainly taken (common English word). I recall small note-taking and design-studio uses of 'Denote' but no dental or health-software product by that name. As a dictionary word the mark is weaker in the abstract, but it should be registrable in the practice-management class with a distinctive wordmark; run a USPTO TESS search for Class 9/42.

### Item 2
- **name**: Loupe
**rationale**

Dental loupes are on the face of nearly every dentist and hygienist, and a jeweler's or examiner's loupe is the universal symbol of looking closely to verify authenticity. That is exactly the dual promise: chairside clarity (perio at hygiene speed, an odontogram you can read) and owner/auditor scrutiny (a ledger and reports that reconcile to the bank). Short, one syllable, distinctly clinical without being cute.

- **tagline**: Look closer. Every chart, every dollar.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Medium-high spelling risk: homophone of 'loop', so people will type loop.com and 'Loop' is heavily used. Pronunciation itself is trivial once seen. Plan to own loop-adjacent typos or use a suffix (LoupeHQ, Loupe Dental).

#### domain ideas
- loupe.dental
- loupehq.com
- useloupe.com
- getloupe.com
- loupepms.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Loupe (loupe.app) was a live-shopping app for sports cards that raised venture money around 2021; Gibraltar Software sells a .NET logging/monitoring tool called Loupe; Loupedeck is a hardware console brand. Loupe manufacturers (SurgiTel, Designs for Vision, Orascoptic) sell to the same dentists, which is a marketing-confusion risk more than a legal one. No dental PMS named Loupe that I know of.

### Item 3
- **name**: Sextant
- **rationale**: Perio charting divides the mouth into six sextants, so every hygienist already uses the word. To everyone else a sextant is the navigator's precision instrument for fixing true position, which is what the Precog side does for the owner: tell you exactly where the practice stands on residual risk and controls. One word that a clinician and an auditor each read as their own.
- **tagline**: Know exactly where your practice stands.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Low spelling risk. The 'sex-' prefix invites juvenile jokes and may trip crude keyword filters in ad platforms; worth a quick test before committing.

#### domain ideas
- sextant.dental
- sextanthq.com
- sextantpms.com
- sextantpractice.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Generic word with scattered small users (Sextant Stays hospitality, Sextant Strategies consulting, a few investment advisers). I do not recall any dental, health, or GRC software called Sextant. sextant.com is likely held.

### Item 4
- **name**: Caliper
**rationale**

Dentists measure with calipers and Boley gauges; machinists and inspectors trust a caliper more than any other hand tool. The name says the system measures rather than estimates, which speaks directly to the research finding that no PMS is praised for AR clarity after dual coverage and partial payments, and that reporting rarely reconciles to the bank. 'Measured' also connotes deliberate and trustworthy.

- **tagline**: Measured to the tenth. Reconciled to the penny.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Low. Only risk is the British 'calliper' spelling. Three syllables, easy to say on the phone.

#### domain ideas
- caliper.dental
- caliperpms.com
- caliperhq.com
- caliperpractice.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Crowded generic: Caliper Corp (now Talogy) personality assessments; Caliper Life Sciences (PerkinElmer/Revvity); Caliper Foods; Caliper Analytics is an IMS Global learning-analytics standard; Google's Caliper was a Java microbenchmark library. None are dental or PMS, so Class 9/42 medical-practice-software registration is plausible but expect a search hit list.

### Item 5
- **name**: Quadrant
- **rationale**: Every tooth in the mouth is located by quadrant (UR, UL, LL, LR), and the COSO impact-by-likelihood heat map at the heart of Precog is literally a four-quadrant grid. One word that names how a dentist locates a tooth and how an auditor places a risk. Suggests structure, completeness, and nothing falling between the cracks.
- **tagline**: Every tooth located. Every risk placed.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Low. Familiar word, no misspelling pattern.

#### domain ideas
- quadrant.dental
- quadrantpms.com
- quadranthq.com
- quadrantpractice.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Very common corporate word: Gartner's 'Magic Quadrant' association is strong; Quadrant Knowledge Solutions (analyst firm), Quadrant Biosciences, Quadrant Private Equity, and many dental practices named 'Quadrant Dental' exist. Trademark protection would be thin; treat as a good descriptor if paired with a distinctive second element.

### Item 6
- **name**: Occlusio
- **rationale**: From occlusion, the way upper and lower teeth meet. A correct occlusion is the dentist's own definition of everything fitting together. The merged product's central promise is the same: chart, ledger, claims, deposits, and controls that fit and line up, so what the biller posts is what the CPA reads and what the bank shows. Coined Latinate form gives it an ownable, instrument-grade feel.
- **tagline**: When everything lines up.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Medium. Four syllables (ok-KLOO-zee-oh); laypeople may not know occlusion or may associate it with vascular blockage; the double-c and s-vs-z are misspelling points. Dentists and staff will have no trouble.

#### domain ideas
- occlusio.com
- occlusio.dental
- occlusiohq.com
- getocclusio.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Bausch sells the OccluSense digital bite sensor; 'occlusal' appears in many dental product names. I do not recall any software product called Occlusio. The root is descriptive in dentistry, so search carefully for Occlusio/Oclusio variants.

### Item 7
- **name**: Evident
**rationale**

Means clear to see, and it literally ends in -dent. Evidence is the auditor's standard and 'clinically evident' is the clinician's. Positions the product as the system that makes the practice legible to everyone who must trust it: the hygienist, the biller, the owner, the CPA, the HIPAA auditor. If the bare word proves too contested, 'Evidental' is the more ownable (though punnier and scope-limiting) fallback.

- **tagline**: Clear chart. Clean ledger. Full proof.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Low. Common adjective; the challenge is making an adjective feel like a product name, which a strong wordmark handles.

#### domain ideas
- evident.dental
- evidentpms.com
- evidenthq.com
- evidental.com
- getevident.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Evident Scientific is Olympus's 2022 spin-off of its microscopy and industrial-inspection business (evidentscientific.com), a well-funded mark in adjacent 'precision instrument' territory; Evident ID sells identity verification. evident.com is taken. No dental PMS by this name that I recall. 'Evidental' I do not recall as a live product.

### Item 8
- **name**: Indelible
- **rationale**: An indelible record cannot be erased or quietly altered. That is the exact property an auditor wants in an audit trail and a clinician wants in a chart, and it speaks to the research finding that only 17% of dental embezzlement is caught by designed controls, usually because the trail was editable or unread. Carries a legal-document seriousness that suits the owner's background.
- **tagline**: Written once. Trusted always.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Medium. Four syllables and frequently misspelled (indelable, indellible). Less playful than the rest of the list; best for a buyer who values gravity over friendliness.

#### domain ideas
- indelible.dental
- indeliblepms.com
- indeliblehq.com
- indeliblerecords.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Several small creative agencies and an ed-tech (Indelible Learning) use the word; indelible.com is taken. No dental, health-IT, or GRC software product that I know of. As a descriptive term for tamper-evident record-keeping the mark may be judged descriptive, so pair it with a distinctive logo.

### Item 9
- **name**: Precedent
- **rationale**: The legal word for an authoritative record that later decisions rely on, and it ends in -dent. It nods to the owner's attorney background, to Smile Notes' standardized note templates (the practice's own precedents), and to the internal-controls coach that turns a one-time assessment into a standing policy. Says 'standards, records, defensibility' in one word.
- **tagline**: Set the standard. Keep the record.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Medium-high. PRESS-ih-dent is routinely misheard as 'president' on the phone, and precedent/precedence is a common written confusion.

#### domain ideas
- precedent.dental
- precedentpms.com
- precedenthq.com
- precedentpractice.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: I recall Precedent (precedent.com, Austin) as a venture-backed AI platform for personal-injury claims, which is health-adjacent and would be the main conflict; 'Precedent' also appears in legal-research tool names and in Precedent Management (furniture). No dental PMS use recalled.

### Item 10
- **name**: Alveo
- **rationale**: From alveolar bone, the socket bone that anchors every tooth; without it nothing holds. A foundation word that dentists recognize instantly and that sounds modern and calm to everyone else. Frames the product as the base layer under the practice: the record, the ledger, and the controls that everything else stands on.
- **tagline**: The foundation under every tooth and every transaction.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Medium. Could be said al-VAY-oh or AL-vee-oh; may be misspelled Alvio or Alveeo. Short enough that it settles quickly once heard.

#### domain ideas
- alveo.dental
- alveopms.com
- alveohq.com
- getalveo.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Alveo Technologies (Alameda, CA) is a molecular-diagnostics company, health-adjacent enough to matter in a clearance search; Alveo Land is Ayala Land's residential developer in the Philippines. alveo.com is likely held. No dental software by this name that I recall.
