## Summary

Ten candidate names from the angle "modern software brand", each with rationale, tagline, spelling risk, domain ideas, and recalled conflicts.

## Angle

modern software brand (short, coined or compound, easy to say and spell, .com-plausible; evoke flow, clarity, calm, a single source of truth)

## Names


### Item 1
- **name**: Headwater
**rationale**

The headwater is the single source a whole river system flows from. The research's core complaint is five copies of the truth (schedule, chart, ledger, claim, bank) that never reconcile; this name says every downstream record starts from one source. Calm mountain-water imagery fits the owner's 'calm clinical surfaces' brand rule, and 'Smile Notes' and 'Precog' survive cleanly as module names inside it.

- **tagline**: Everything downstream starts here.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Low. HED-waw-ter; no ambiguous letters. Only risk is people adding an s (Headwaters), so register both.

#### domain ideas
- headwater.com (likely taken/parked; check)
- headwater.health
- headwater.dental
- headwaterpms.com
- tryheadwater.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Headwaters Inc. (building materials, acquired by Boral 2017); Headwaters MB (investment bank); Headwaters Health Care Centre (Ontario hospital); Headwater Companies (natural resources). No dental or PMS product recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 2
- **name**: Sooth
**rationale**

An archaic English word for truth (soothsayer, forsooth) and the root of 'soothe' — truth and calm in one five-letter word, and it rhymes with tooth. It answers the two things the research says no PMS delivers: a ledger that tells the truth after dual insurance and partial payments, and a UI that does not add stress. The Precog risk module already trades on foresight, so the soothsayer echo is on-brand rather than accidental.

- **tagline**: Rhymes with tooth. Means truth.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Medium. Most will say it correctly (like tooth) but some will write 'soothe' or voice the th as in 'soothe'; the tagline is designed to teach both. Slight fortune-telling connotation to manage in compliance marketing.

#### domain ideas
- sooth.com (likely taken/parked; check)
- sooth.health
- sooth.dental
- soothpms.com
- getsooth.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Soothe (on-demand massage/wellness app, soothe.com) is the closest phonetic neighbor, different class. No dental or health-software 'Sooth' recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 3
- **name**: Daybook
**rationale**

The daybook is the accountant's book of original entry: every transaction, in the order of the day, before anything is allocated or hidden. That is exactly the ledger transparency the report says no product is praised for (Open Dental's allocated/unallocated/hidden payments; CareStack transfer adjustments). It also reads as the practice's 'day' — the schedule — so it covers front desk and back office in one plain compound.

- **tagline**: Every day, accounted for.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Very low. DAY-book; only variance is one word vs two. Trademark note: mildly descriptive for accounting software, suggestive for a PMS, so file in the dental-software class.

#### domain ideas
- daybook.com (taken; check resale)
- daybook.health
- daybook.dental
- daybookpms.com
- usedaybook.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Several consumer journaling apps named Daybook (Android 'Daybook – Diary, Journal'); the word is a generic accounting term so broad exclusivity is unlikely. No dental PMS recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 4
- **name**: Evenkeel
**rationale**

'On an even keel' is the idiom for steady and upright through rough water. The report's cloud complaints are six-hour outages with no offline mode, claim-sending failures, and update chaos; the merged product's offline-tolerant PGlite heritage and deterministic audit engine make steadiness a real differentiator, not a slogan. It is a coined single word in the Everlane/Evernote shape and sounds like a modern brand rather than a dental one.

- **tagline**: Level, whatever the day brings.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Low-medium. EE-ven-keel; possible misspellings Evenkeal or Even Keel as two words. Register the two-word form too.

#### domain ideas
- evenkeel.com (likely taken; check)
- evenkeel.health
- evenkeel.dental
- evenkeelpms.com
- getevenkeel.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: 'Even Keel' is a very common name for counseling and therapy practices (services class, not software); possibly small wellness or fintech uses. No dental or health-software product recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 5
- **name**: Cusp
- **rationale**: The cusp is the working edge of a tooth and 'on the cusp' means the leading edge — dental-native without the tired 'dent-' prefix that makes Dentrix, Denticon, DentiMax and Dentally blur together. Four letters, one syllable, and it gives the design team a geometric mark (a point, a peak) that suits the owner's 'geometry, not costume' brand rule.
- **tagline**: Where the whole practice comes to a point.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Very low. KUSP; dentists know the word, laypeople know the idiom. Only risk is plural confusion (cusps).

#### domain ideas
- cusp.com (taken; check)
- cusp.dental
- cusp.health
- cusppms.com
- getcusp.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: 'Cusp Dental' boutique clinics exist (Austin TX and elsewhere; services class). CUSP is also the Johns Hopkins Comprehensive Unit-based Safety Program (patient-safety, healthcare-adjacent) and NYU's Center for Urban Science and Progress. No dental PMS recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 6
- **name**: Chartroom
- **rationale**: A ship's chart room is where the course is plotted from one set of charts; the clinical chart is the center of a PMS. One room for every chart, schedule, ledger, claim and control is the single-source-of-truth promise stated in plain English. It also positions the internal-controls module naturally: the owner sees the whole course, not fragments.
- **tagline**: Every chart. One room.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Very low. CHART-room; only variance is one word vs two.

#### domain ideas
- chartroom.com (likely taken/parked; check)
- chartroom.health
- chartroom.dental
- chartroompms.com
- thechartroom.app
- **known conflicts from memory**: 'The Chart Room' is a common nautical bar/restaurant name (New Orleans, Newport RI). Possible small EHR add-ons using ChartRoom; none recalled with certainty. No dental PMS recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 7
- **name**: Kept
- **rationale**: Books kept, records kept, appointments kept, recalls kept, controls kept — one past participle covers the audit trail, the retention metrics, and the promise that order happens without effort. It is the punchiest name in the set and fits the modern one-word wordmark style. If it proves too terse or too ambiguous, 'Wellkept' is the safer compound with the same meaning.
- **tagline**: Books kept. Promises kept. Patients kept.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Very low to say and spell. Connotation risk: 'kept' alone can read as 'kept woman' or feel incomplete as a noun; the tagline supplies the objects. Fallback: Wellkept.

#### domain ideas
- kept.com (almost certainly taken; check resale)
- kept.health
- kept.dental
- keptpms.com
- getkept.com
- wellkept.health (fallback)
- **known conflicts from memory**: Faint recollection of small bookkeeping or photo-print startups named Kept; 'Well Kept' is used by cleaning, home-organization and beauty brands (different classes). No dental or health-software product recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 8
- **name**: Onefold
- **rationale**: A real but rarely used word meaning single and simple — the opposite of manifold. It states the architectural thesis directly: one record per patient, one ledger, one truth, with Smile Notes and Precog folded in rather than bolted on. It reads as a coined modern brand, is trivially spelled, and gives designers a literal fold motif.
- **tagline**: One practice. One record. One truth.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Very low. WUN-fold. Minor connotation of 'the fold' (church, sheep); no spelling ambiguity.

#### domain ideas
- onefold.com (likely taken; check)
- onefold.health
- onefold.dental
- onefoldpms.com
- getonefold.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Faint recollection of a small crypto or wallet startup styled OneFold; not confirmed. No dental or health-software product recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 9
- **name**: Plainly
- **rationale**: The research's single loudest theme is money that trained billers and CPAs cannot read, plus quote-only pricing and renewal creep. 'Plainly' commits the brand to plain-language ledgers, plain pricing, and nothing hidden — the adverb-brand shape (Grammarly, Calendly) buyers already read as modern SaaS. It also fits the owner's stated tone of minimal, calm, no slogans.
- **tagline**: The money, the schedule, the chart — in plain sight.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: Very low. PLAYN-lee. Connotation risk that 'plain' reads as basic; the tagline reframes it as transparency.

#### domain ideas
- plainly.com (likely taken; check)
- plainly.health
- plainly.dental
- plainlypms.com
- getplainly.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Plainly (video personalization SaaS, plainlyvideos.com); the adverb is likely used by small plain-language or legal-tech tools. No dental or health-software product recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.

### Item 10
- **name**: Wholesum
**rationale**

Whole plus sum: the whole practice in one sum that adds up to the bank statement, which the report identifies as the control point for both the 'reading the money' theme and the 48%-embezzled / 17%-caught-by-design data. Spoken, it is 'wholesome' — a warm, trustworthy sound for a product that will hold PHI. It is the most conceptually on-target name for the reconciliation gap and the riskiest to spell.

- **tagline**: Everything adds up.
- **pronunciation and spelling risk**: High spelling risk: nearly everyone will type 'wholesome' first, and that .com is not obtainable, so paid search and the tagline must carry the spelling. Pronunciation itself is trivial.

#### domain ideas
- wholesum.com (likely taken by a produce brand; check)
- wholesum.health
- wholesum.dental
- wholesumpms.com
- getwholesum.com
- **known conflicts from memory**: Wholesum Harvest / Wholesum Family Farms (organic produce, Arizona/Mexico) and other food brands use the same spelling in different classes. No dental or health-software product recalled. Unverified: no web or USPTO search in this read-only pass.
