# The glove pad is the right size for my hands, but my PIN went in and the tablet still said Priya was charting

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Ana Lucía Torres (he/him), 20, one year as a registered dental assistant; before this office we ran Open Dental, so "Log Off and pick your user", "Perio Chart", and "Set Complete" are the words in my head.
I would call my tech comfort 4 of 5; I learn a screen fast, and I test it at the speed of a busy morning with a patient already in the chair.
No access adjustments, but I work gloved and often with wet hands at chairside on a shared tablet, so any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a defect I will hit, not a preference.

## What I did

**da-1, switch author by PIN on the shared tablet (6 taps, budget 6, NOT completed).** I tapped the "PR PR" chip at the top right and got "Who is charting?" with a twelve-key pad, "Enter the other person's PIN. Their session opens on this page; yours is revoked and local drafts are wiped after autosave." Every key measured 72 x 56 px with 8 px between them (seq 2-3), which is more than the 56 px the task sheet asked for, and the cursor landed on the 1 key for me. I tapped 3 3 3 3, four dots showed, and tapped Go. The dialog closed. And that was all: the chip still read "PR PR", the aria-label still said "Who is charting: Priya Raman", nothing was announced, and the store said the current user was Priya (seq 10-12). A `sessions` row was written for `sess-u-da-1`, so the system knows Jo Ramirez's PIN matched, but the tablet did not become Jo. In Open Dental, if I Log Off and log in as me, the next thing I chart carries my user; here I got a dialog that closed politely and left the other person signed in. I did it again later on the perio screen to be sure (seq 89-101): same result, session written, chip still "BL BL · RDH". The card's first task, "Switch author by PIN before charting", cannot be done for an RDA on this build.

**da-2, arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 taps, budget 2, done).** Arrive on the 9:00 card, "Marisol Vega arrived. Seat is the next step on the same card" and the cursor already on Seat; Seat, "Seated in chair 1. The chair strip now shows BL", and the strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH" (seq 13-21). Two taps, both 44 px, exactly as promised. What bothers me is under the hood: both appointment events say "by Priya Raman" (seq 16, 20), because my PIN in da-1 did not take. I did the Board's work under her name. On privacy: the task sheet asked about `?privacy=1`, so I tried the top-bar "Privacy" button on the same Board later (seq 103-110). With it on, every card read initials only, "9:00 am · MV", the queue rows "DP", "IO", the Arrive buttons "Arrive OM", and the live line "OM arrived. Seat is the next step on the same card." No patient name anywhere, and the chair strip did not change because it never had a patient in it. One note for whoever writes the panel scripts: `window.__proto.set({privacy: true})` flips the flag but does not repaint the Board (my first privacy reading between seq 21 and 22 showed full names for that reason); use the button or the URL parameter.

**da-3, open Perio with the glove pad and record 12 sites by tapping (13 taps, budget 15, done).** The route put me on "Perio · Marisol Vega" as Bree ("BL BL · RDH" in the chip) without any PIN; that is the second time today the tablet decided who I am without asking me. The Glove pad button was 121 x 44 px and its aria-label promised "44 px keys for gloved fingers". I tapped it (seq 24) and nothing appeared on screen: the pad renders under the grid at y = 1135 px on a 768 px tall tablet, 579 px below the fold, so with wet gloves I have to scroll the canvas most of a screen before I can touch a key, and once I am down there the tooth-number header has scrolled off. When I got to it, the keys were honest: 1 to 9, Bleed, →, ⌫ all exactly 44 x 44 px with 8 px between them, "Next tooth" 252 x 56 px, no overlaps (seq 24-26). One label cheats: "Bleed" is set in 17 px bold and is 54 px wide inside a 44 px key, so the text hangs 5 px over each edge and the visual gap to the 9 key is 3 px, not 8; my thumb does not know where the key ends. Twelve taps on 3 (seq 26-51) filled teeth 2 to 5 on sites 1 to 3 and the stamp read "Tooth 6 · site 1 · prior 4" and "Sites recorded: 12/168"; focus stayed on the 3 key after each tap so my thumb never had to move, and Tab showed a 3 px solid blue ring (probe, events-probe-d.json seq 2-18). Bleed on the pad marked the last recorded site "3 mm, bleeding" but changed nothing in the stamp, so I only knew it worked by reading the cell; Undo said "Undo: removed 3 at tooth 2 site 2", twice, in two live regions.

**da-4, chair strip shows initials and no patient data (0 taps, budget 0, done).** Back on the Board (seq 52) the strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH", "Chair 2 · HK DDS", "Chair 3 · HK DDS"; the aria-labels said "Chair 1, author Bree Lawson, RDH. Show device author." I checked every patient name in the seed against the strip: none. As a probe I tapped Chair 1 (seq 53-57): it opened one line, "Bree Lawson, RDH is the author on the Op 1 device", and focus stayed on the chair button. Staff names, no patients. This is what I want the front desk to see about my operatory and nothing more.

**da-5, day pass for a new hire, then the first-shift rail (9 taps by the contract formula, budget 8, NOT completed as scripted).** Roles opened from the route, "Add day pass" put the cursor in the name field, I typed Sam Lee, chose Front desk and Main Street, and Issue day pass wrote `dp-1` and the line "Day pass issued to Sam Lee · Front desk · expires 5:30 pm + 30 min grace · magic link sent to their phone; TOTP on their own device" (seq 60-79). "Sign in as this temp" landed me on `#/temp/board` with a rail, "Your first shift: Arrive ✓ Seat ✓ Checkout Take payment Find a patient Hide" (seq 81-82). That is the problem: Sam Lee had done nothing yet, and two chips were already ticked, because Priya's Arrive and Seat from da-2 had retired them; the rail state is one bucket for the whole tablet, not for the person on the pass. I tapped the Arrive chip anyway (seq 84): it pulsed the live "Arrive" button on Hank Haddad's card and announced "Tap Arrive on the first card", which is the right kind of help. Then Arrive on Hank (seq 86-87) wrote the appointment event "by Sam Lee" but no `firstRunState` row, because the chip was already retired (seq 88). On taps: the eight I meant were seven script steps plus one chip tap to see the pulse; the formula counts the space I typed in "Sam Lee" as a ninth (seq 66, a key " " on `roles.daypass.name`). I re-ran the task from a fresh store as a probe (events-probe-c.json seq 2-34): the rail came up with all five chips live, the Arrive chip pulsed `board.card.a-1042.arrive` in view with "Tap Arrive on the first card", the Checkout chip pulsed Ruth Adler's Checkout with "Open Checkout from the card", and after Arrive the chip flipped to "Arrive ✓" and `frs-arrive` was written (seq 33). So the rail works when it starts clean; it just does not start clean for the second person on the tablet. Also for the record: a-1043 is seeded "seated", so the sheet's note that it is not arrivable is correct.
