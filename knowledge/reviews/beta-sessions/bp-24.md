# The grammar is the best perio keyboard I have used, but Space goes to whatever button I last touched, and half the mouth is below the fold

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Jordan, 39, twelve years in hygiene and lead for our hygiene team; the last office ran CareStack, so I think in its words: auto-advance, bleeding on the site, the recall interval, the perio chart as its own tab.
Tech comfort 5 of 5; I do not need a tutorial, I need the keys to do the same thing every time and the screen to keep up with a busy morning.
Operatory screen at 1024 by 768, light theme, motion on auto. I work gloved and often with wet hands, so any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a target I will hit by accident, and I measured every one.

## What I did

Event numbers below are the `gseq` field in events.json: the prototype's `reset()` wipes `window.__events`, and I had to start fresh twice (hy-3 and hy-6, explained there) plus once per probe, so I kept one running count across the segments. Taps are physical presses. The contract's literal rule counts a keyboard Enter twice (the `key` and the browser's synthesized `click`, e.g. gseq 2-3) and counts the two spaces I typed inside "suspected caries DO" as taps because the text box carries a testid (gseq 416, 423); I give the literal count in the JSON as `taps_literal` so nobody has to redo the arithmetic.

**hy-1, open Perio for Marisol Vega from my first card (1 tap, budget 1, done; gseq 1-4).** The 9:00 card told me everything before I touched it: a stop chip "New anticoagulant (apixaban), intake 2 days ago", then the strip "Since last visit · Med hx changed · Perio 14 mo ago · BWX due, practice rule: 12 mo · What helped last time: stop signal, sunglasses". What changed, what is due, what helped, on the card, zero taps. Two things bothered me. Marisol was not my first card: the first card was "7:30 · Devon Price · Filed later · Chair 1 · Hygiene · Minor: guardian Alicia Price", a patient who left an hour ago and whose note is somebody's paperwork problem, sitting at the head of a list called "Chairs · mine". And after Enter on Perio the grid was up with tooth 2 site 1 highlighted and every cell ghosting the July 2025 depth (168 ghosts), but my keyboard focus was on nothing (`document.activeElement` = body). The ghost numbers are 10 px type in the corner of a 44 px cell; at arm's length on the operatory monitor I read them as grey dust. The subtitle is 49 words in one line; I am fast, and it still took me two reads.

**hy-2, full mouth, keyboard only, one pass (1 tap, budget 1; 188 keystrokes, budget 200; done; gseq 5-198).** I typed 168 depths with 20 Spaces for bleeding straight through, and checked the cursor against where it should be after every 21 keys: it never jumped, not once. The line above the grid kept me oriented without my eyes on the cells: "Tooth 8 · site 1 · prior 3 · Sites recorded: 18/168", later "Tooth 4 · site 4 · prior 5 · 75/168", "Tooth 19 · site 6 · prior 3 · 131/168". Under the grid the stamp read "Saved to tooth #31 (draft, this session)". Save was one Enter (gseq 194), no licence asked, and the card that came back was the note I would have typed by hand in CareStack: "Perio: 168 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 20 sites." then "SRP evidence: 25 sites at or above 5 mm." then "Chart status: full-mouth six-point chart recorded on 168 sites." and "Next visit (practice policy, not a recommendation): 4-month perio maintenance with BWX". The focus landed on "Tag for dentist", which is where I wanted to go next. This task is the reason I would take this system.

**hy-3, undo a mistyped depth and skip a site without recording zero (0 taps for the undo and skip, budget 0; then 4 taps to save with the licence; done only after a fresh start).** Run as written, straight after hy-2, nothing happened: the exam was saved, the line read "Exam saved · grid is read-only", and 9, Backspace, 3, Right went to the "Tag for dentist" button that held focus and did nothing there, no flash, no message, no Save, no way to open a second chart (gseq 199-202). So I reset and opened Perio again (204-207). Fresh: 9 put "9" in tooth 2 site 1 and moved on; Backspace put "—" back and flashed "Undo: removed 9 at tooth 2 site 1" (208-209); 3 went in; Right made site 2 "not probed, prior 2" and the counter said "1/168 · 1 not probed" (210-211). The stored row has site 2 as depth null, skipped true, never a 0. The undo feedback is a 15 px line at the top of the page for two seconds plus a screen-reader announcement; with my eyes in the mouth I would not see it, and there is no sound, so I would only know by looking up. I typed the rest of the mouth (212-377) and pressed Save (378-383): "Name why 1 sites were not probed" with one 176 by 44 px button "Choose a reason", and Save turned into an outlined "Held". The reason chooser was not there yet; one more Enter on "Choose a reason" opened "Why were 1 sites not probed?" with four reasons, I chose Crown margin, and the confirm changed from "Choose a reason above" to "Save exam with this reason" (384-397). The script said two taps for the licence; it took three, four with Save. The note came out honest: "Perio: 167 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 0 sites, 1 not probed (crown_margin)." and "Chart status: partial chart; 1 sites not probed (Crown margin). This never reads as a full chart." It says "1 sites" three times on one screen.

**hy-4, tag #30 for the dentist as a finding (2 taps, budget 3, done; gseq 398-431).** Enter on "Tag for dentist" opened "Tag for Dr. Kim" with the tooth box already holding "3", the deepest pocket, not the tooth I meant. If I had typed my note and pressed Enter I would have tagged tooth 3 with "suspected caries DO". Ctrl+A, 30, Tab landed on "Observation" as it should, I typed the words, Enter on Save tag. The row is tag-2, tooth 30, "suspected caries DO", disposition null, author Bree Lawson. The copy under the heading reads "A finding, not a diagnosis. The tag lands as a checklist row on the dentist's exam; sign refuses until it has a disposition.", the chip after says "Tagged #30 · waiting for dentist", and the announcement repeated "A finding, not a diagnosis." Nothing policed my words: six observation buttons ("Suspected caries", "Fractured restoration", "Recession ≥3 mm", "Mobility", "Soft-tissue lesion", "Other observation", all 44 px tall) were offered, not forced, and the page never said the word diagnosis to me. That is my scope described correctly.

**hy-5, mark ready for exam (2 taps, budget 2, done; gseq 432-441).** Enter on Chairs; focus went to the body again after the route change, so I had to find the card by hand. The card now carried "Perio charted today · 167 sites probed, deepest 6 mm" in the strip and a 290 by 44 px "Ready for exam" button. Enter wrote `encounter.exam_requested` (ae-1), the status chip became "Exam requested", a chip "Exam: 1st in queue" appeared, the announcement said "Marisol Vega ready for exam: 1st in queue. The Board chair strip shows Exam requested.", and focus moved to Note on the same card. Clean.

**hy-6, run the screening lane (3 taps, budget 2; 6 keystrokes, budget 12; done only after a fresh start).** As written, after hy-2 to hy-5, Perio reopened the saved exam and "Screening" looked live (not disabled, not Held, aria-pressed false); Enter did nothing, no refusal, no announcement (gseq 442-448). Fresh (450-476): Perio, Screening, and the grid was replaced by six 88 px boxes "UR · 1–5", "UA · 6–11" and so on, with the line "Sextant UR (teeth 1–5) · keys 0–4 or *"; yes, it is obviously a different lane. I typed 2 1 3 0 2 1; the boxes filled, UL went orange for the 3, the line read "All six sextants coded · Save exam · Codes: 6/6 · Full chart due". But the keyboard focus ring stayed on the UR box the whole time while the active box moved on (459-470), so the screen showed two "current" boxes; and the subtitle still said "Keys: 1–9 depth · Space bleed · S suppuration" and the legend under the boxes still said "small grey = prior exam · ● bleeding", the full-chart text under the screening lane. Save gave "Screening saved · exam pe-2 · Screening codes: UR 2 · UA 1 · UL 3 · LL 0 · LA 2 · LR 1 · Full chart due" and "Next visit: Full chart due: a full six-point chart is booked into the next hygiene visit", which is right. Then I read the derived note the same save wrote and the announcement that followed it: "Perio: 0 sites probed, deepest 0 mm, bleeding at 0 sites." (474-476). That sentence in a hygiene note after a screening is wrong, and a dentist or an auditor reading it would think I charted nothing. The script's own steps (Perio, Screening, Save) need three taps against a budget of two.

**Probes (gseq 478-915 in events.json; 1-171 in events-probe2.json).** Gloved measurements first: 39 controls on Chairs, 183 on Perio, 196 with the glove pad open, 21 in the screening lane, every one at least 44 by 44 px and none closer than 8 px to a neighbour; the grid cells are 44 px on an 8 px border spacing, the pad keys 44 by 44 at 8 px, the focus ring 3 px solid rgb(43,108,184) with a 2 px offset on Save, on a cell, and on Screening; no horizontal overflow at 1024 px (the grid is 992 px wide inside a 992 px wrap). I could not find one gloved miss. Then the bad news. (1) After Enter on Perio the focus is on the body; five Tabs land on Save. With focus on Save, digits still chart (the grammar listens on the document) and the screen puts focus back on Save after every key; I typed a full mouth that way and the Space for bleeding on the last site pressed Save: the exam saved, irreversible, with "bleeding at 0 sites" (538-878, probe-fullmouth-space-saves.png). With one site entered the same Space produced "Name why 167 sites were not probed" and a Held Save (901-907). The same thing happens after a gloved tap on the "Glove pad" button: the digit charted, Space toggled the pad ("Hide glove pad") and no bleeding was recorded (515-522). Space only means bleeding when focus is on the body or on a cell (508 toggled bleeding correctly on tooth 8). (2) At 1024 by 768 the lower arch is not on the screen while I chart it: the Lower header sits at y 725, tooth 31 site 1 is at y 768 when the cursor reaches it after 84 keys, and it stays off-screen through the last site at y 1028; nothing scrolls the active cell into view and the page did not scroll at all (events-probe2.json 46-171, probe2-lower-arch-fold.png). The orientation line at y 284 is all I have for half the mouth. (3) The glove pad opens at y 1135 to 1347 on a 768 px viewport (gseq 485-488, probe-pad-viewport.png): the toggle at the top says "Hide glove pad" and nothing on screen has changed; a gloved tap on a cell also opens the pad, out of sight (501-503). When I scrolled to it, the pad worked: 4, 5, Bleed, 3 gave "4", "5●", "3" and focus followed the cursor (489-500). (4) The over-15 refusal reads "Depth 17 mm is above the 15 mm limit", nine words against the contract's eight (913-915); the omission line is seven.
