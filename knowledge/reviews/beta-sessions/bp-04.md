# Two taps to seat and every chip carries its word, but switch on high contrast and I cannot see which button is pressed

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Dana, 54, seventeen years at the front desk and the lead for the last nine; we ran Curve before this, so I say "schedule", "Sidekick", and "patient portion" without thinking.
I would put my tech comfort at 4 of 5: I read the release notes, I set up the templates, and I am the one the temps ask.
I have low vision; my monitor runs at 150% zoom with high contrast on, so I check that nothing overflows or gets cut off, that I can always see where the focus is, and that every status has a shape and a word, not just a color.

## What I did

**fo-1, arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 taps, budget 2, done; seq 6-13).** I clicked Arrive on the 9:00 card; the screen reader line said "Marisol Vega arrived. Seat is the next step on the same card" and the focus ring was already sitting on Seat, so the second click was lined up. Seat said "Seated in chair 1. The chair strip now shows BL", the chair strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH", and the card flipped to "In chart". All 99 controls on the Board measured at least 44 px and nothing sat closer than 8 px to its neighbour. The eligibility chip question: before I touched anything, the card (283 px wide at 1280) had "Hygiene" and "Verify" on one line and the "Re-verify" button wrapped to the next line beside the "1 alert" chip (tops 974 vs 1010, seq 5-6), so it read to me as "Re-verify · 1 alert", not as the Verify chip's control. At my 150% zoom the card is 405 px wide and the pair sits together, so it is the un-zoomed desk that breaks it. After Arrive and Seat the card still said "Verify" with a live "Re-verify" button, although Details had promised "re-runs on arrival"; I could not tell whether the re-run had happened. There is also "Re-verify all" in the strip above for the same two patients.

**fo-2, Ines Okoro, $44 by card (3 taps, budget 4, done; seq 14-43).** Checkout opened from the card with "Collect" already pressed (aria-pressed true), the estimate column had its own shaded header "Patient portion (estimate)", the totals row read "$261.00" fee and "$44.00 est." with "Estimate is separate from the balance above; it never enters the ledger", and the amount field was prefilled 44.00. So yes, I could tell the estimate from the balance. Card, sixteen digits, Post: "Posted. 1 ledger rows and the collection decision are written", focus moved to Back to Board. What I could not explain: the three tiles above the table said "$0.00 Patient due · $0.00 Waiting on insurance · $0.00 Credit" while the table said she owed $44 (seq 15-16), and after Post the Credit tile read "$88.00" for one $44 payment (seq 37-42; the store's balances() returns credit 8800). The chip "Note unfiled — Filed-later lane" and the posted line "held as credit until the note is filed" I understood. Back on the Board (one extra click on Back to Board, seq 44, outside the budget) her card was not "checked out" as the task sheet promised; it had moved to a "Filed later" lane reading "Paid at the window · charges and claim release when Dr. Kim files the note", and her queue row now offered only "Ping chair". One thing for keyboard users: after the route changed into Checkout, focus was on the page body, not on the heading or the first control (seq 15-16).

**fo-3, Ruth Adler, $0 portion (2 taps, budget 2, done; seq 46-54).** Checkout, Post. "Nothing due today" was already pressed, no tender buttons were shown, and the text under it read "Patient portion is $0. Post still writes the collection decision (reason zero_due) so the day's 'Not collected at window' line stays honest." Post wrote two charges and "Collection decision cd-2: Nothing due today" and no payment row; no refusal anywhere. In Curve a zero walkout meant a $0 payment line to keep the day sheet tidy; this is cleaner and I had nothing to pick.

**fo-4, Samir Haddad pays in full, off insurance (5 taps, budget 5, done; seq 57-81).** Both "Paid in full — don't send to insurance" toggles were on screen the moment Checkout opened, before I chose Cash (seq 58-59), because the amount was prefilled at $168.00 and already covered each fee; when I typed $50 into the amount they disappeared and at $168 they came back (seq 63-64), so the rule is about the amount, not the tender the task sheet told me to wait for. The toggle text is plain, the accessible name says which procedure ("Paid in full, do not send Limited exam to insurance"), each is 344 by 44 px, and there is no policy paragraph and no "HIPAA" anywhere; I could read it to Samir as is. After Post: charges, "Payment $168.00 by cash", two allocations, "Self-pay restriction on pr-421: claim assembly refuses it" for both lines, and "Collection decision cd-3: Collect, patient portion $168.00". Two things bothered me. The task sheet called it a $180 exam and the screen said $168.00 fee and $168.00 est. (seq 58-59). And the pressed toggle differs from the unpressed one only by a lighter fill (1.68:1 against the unpressed background) and a blue border; the word does not change. When I ran the same screen with forced colors on, the pressed toggle, the pressed "Cash", and the pressed "Collect" looked the same as their neighbours: black fill, white text, white border, no glyph (forced-colors-pressed.png; the prototype has no forced-colors or prefers-contrast rule at all). With my high contrast on I would be pressing Post without being able to see what Collect is pointing at.

**fo-5, patient at the window whose note is not filed (2 taps of 3, NOT completed; seq 84-89).** Devon Price's queue row already read "7:30 am · Devon Price · Filed later · Note ◆ Open · BL · Claim ▬ Held · Note not filed — Bree L." with one button, "Ping chair", and the stamp "Paid at the window · in the Filed-later lane until Bree L. files". I pinged (seq 85-86): a messages row was written, the row stamped "Pinged chair 1 · 8:40 am · one-to-one, not broadcast", and my focus dropped to the page body. Nobody made me the note cop; the pressure went to chair 1 and the row says so. Then the script's second step, `board.queue.row.a-1050.checkout`, did not exist: Devon is seeded already checked out, the lane card offers only Details and Rail, and there was no Post to press, so the task failed as written and no allocation intent was written. Because the first ping seemed to do nothing to my focus I pressed it again (seq 87-89) and got a refusal, "Already pinged this chair in the last 15 minutes": nine words, no control, and again focus on the body. The contract says one verb line of at most eight words and one 44 px control. As a probe outside the script (seq 90-94) I typed the checkout address for Devon: the screen showed an "Already checked out" chip, "Nothing due today" pre-selected, a Credit tile of $95.00, and a live Post; Post went through with "Posted. 0 ledger rows and the collection decision are written" and wrote a second decision, cd-4, for an encounter that was already done.

**fo-6, find Lena Fischer with three letters (2 taps, budget 3, done; seq 97-117).** Ctrl+K put me in the search box with the placeholder "Type three letters… try walkout statement, day sheet, Office Journal", "fis" listed three Fischers, and every row already showed "DOB 7/8/1969 · …0126" and so on for the other two (seq 100-103). I clicked Lena; the box read "Confirm date of birth", the patient line read "Lena Fischer · …0126 · MRN-306", the hint said "Second identifier. Ask the patient, or read it from the appointment card." I typed 07/08/1969, Enter, a PHI access row was written, and the Sidekick, here called the Rail, opened on the left: "Lena Fischer / DOB 7/8/1969 · phone …0126 / No critical alerts", with the ledger's three numbers. The gate asked me for a date it had shown me one screen earlier, and it put the phone last-4 and the MRN on the confirm screen too, so anyone at my desk can pass it without Lena. With a wrong date (01/01/1970, seq 118-138) the gate did its job: "Date of birth does not match", six words, one 114 by 44 px "Try again", the Open chart button turned into an outlined "Held" with a lock, the screen reader line said "Date of birth does not match. Try again", and "Try again" put me back in the field with "Open chart" restored (seq 139-141). After the Rail opened, focus was on the body again (seq 116-117). With the Rail open at 1280 the Board kept its two-column layout inside a 960 px canvas: Operatory 3 ran from 800 to 1020 px and the Checkout queue started at 904 px, so Lena's own Checkout button on the Op 3 card hit-tested to Devon Price's queue row.

**What I checked because of how I work.** At 150% zoom on a 1280 by 900 monitor (853 by 600 CSS px), nothing overflowed and nothing was cut off on the Board, on Checkout, or in the search: no horizontal scroll, no clipped label, no control pushed off the right edge, the top bar wrapped cleanly to two rows, the checkout table fit its 795 px wrapper, and every control still measured 44 px. That is the negative result I hoped for. What the zoom did change is distance: the first appointment card starts 716 px down a 600 px screen because the "Before open" strip (362 px tall) and the chair strip fill the first view; the first Arrive button is at 995 px; the Filed-later lane is at 3224 px and the Checkout queue at 3482 px, six screens down. Hiding the strip brings the first card up to 424 px. On Checkout at zoom, Collect is at 792 px and Post at 1107 px, two screens below the tiles. In dark theme every text I sampled sits between 7.6:1 and 14.5:1 against its background, the chips between 8.6:1 and 11.4:1, and every chip on the Board and Checkout carries a shape glyph and a word (0 failures of 99 controls and all chips). The focus ring is 3 px solid rgb(143,193,245) with a 2 px surface halo on Tab and on the Arrive button. Pressing the single key A arrived Marisol Vega and announced her name with focus on Seat; I like it, and I would want a temp told that one key does an irreversible thing.
