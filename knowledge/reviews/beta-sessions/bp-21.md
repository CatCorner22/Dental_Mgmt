# The keys are right for my hands; the screen still asks my eyes to do too much, and Space can hit Save

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Lucas, 59, twenty-six years a hygienist; the last office ran Curve, so I think in Curve's words: auto-advance, bleeding on the site, the recall interval.
Tech comfort 3 of 5. I read slowly (dyslexia), so I time every line I have to read; a glyph beats a sentence, and more than eight words on a verb line is a finding for me.
Operatory screen, light theme, motion left on auto. I chart gloved and often wet, so anything under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a target I will miss.

## What I did

Event numbers below are the `gseq` field in events.json. The prototype's `reset()` wipes `window.__events`, and I had to reset twice (explained under hy-3 and hy-6), so I kept one global count across the runs: A = 1-200, B = 201-445, C = 446-473, probes D/E = 474-535. Taps are counted by the contract rule, except that a keyboard Enter on a button logs both a `key` and a browser `click` for one press (I count it once; the literal count is in `raw_taps_contract` in results.json), and the two spaces I typed inside the tag text field are characters, not taps. Reading times assume my pace of about 120 words a minute.

**hy-1, open Perio from my first card (1 tap, budget 1, done).** The 9:00 card said what I need before I touch it: a stop chip "New anticoagulant (apixaban), intake 2 days ago", then the strip "Since last visit · Med hx changed · Perio 14 mo ago · BWX due, practice rule: 12 mo · What helped last time: stop signal, sunglasses". So yes: what changed, what is due, what helped, all on the card, no tap to read them. I put the cursor on Perio and pressed Enter (gseq 1-4). The grid opened at "Tooth 2 · site 1 · prior 3" because 1 is missing, the sub-line read "Prior exam 7/1/2025 ghosted", and every one of the 168 cells had the old depth in the corner. Good. Two things I noticed: my keyboard focus was left on the page body, not on the first cell (`document.activeElement` = body), and the line under the title is 49 words ("Chair 1 · Prior exam 7/1/2025 ghosted · 28 teeth (x = missing: 1, 16, 17, 32) · Keys: 1–9 depth · Space bleed · S suppuration · ⌫ undo · → skip · PgDn next tooth"), about 25 seconds for me. The key legend belongs in Settings or on the pad, not in a line I have to read past every time I open a chart.

**hy-2, full mouth, keyboard only, one pass (1 tap, budget 1; 187 keystrokes, budget 200; done).** I typed 168 depths straight through with 18 Spaces for bleeding (gseq 5-190). I checked the cursor against where I expected it after every seventh key: it never jumped once. The big line above the grid ("Tooth 12 · site 1 · prior 4 · Sites recorded: 30/168") kept me oriented without looking at the cells, and when the last site went in it changed to "All 168 sites entered · Save exam". Under the grid a stamp read "Saved to tooth #31 (draft, this session)". Save was one Enter (gseq 191-195), no licence asked, and the note line came back as "Perio: 168 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 18 sites." with the recall "4-month perio maintenance with BWX". This is Curve's auto-advance without the mouse, and it is the best part of the day. What I did not like: the saved card is 82 words (about 41 seconds for me) before I get to the one line I came for, and the prior depths are 10 px grey digits in the cell corners (contrast 5.3:1, so they pass, but 10 px is below what I can read at arm's length with the light on the patient).

**hy-3, undo a mistyped depth and skip a site without recording zero (4 taps, budget 0; done only after a fresh start).** Run as written, straight after hy-2, this task cannot happen: the exam is saved, the grid says "Exam saved · grid is read-only", and 9, Backspace, 3, Right did nothing at all, not even a flash (gseq 197-200). No Save button, no Held, no refusal telling me why, and no "new exam" or "amend" anywhere. So I reset and opened the chart again (setup, gseq 201-205, one tap not counted here). Fresh: 9 put a 9 in tooth 2 site 1; Backspace showed "Undo: removed 9 at tooth 2 site 1" in 15 px above the grid and the cell went back to "—" with its ghost 3 (gseq 206-207); 3 corrected it; Right skipped site 2, the cell read "—" with the description "not probed, prior 2" and the count line said "Sites recorded: 1/168 · 1 not probed" (gseq 208-209). The store held `{depth: null, skipped: true}`, never 0. The undo line is eight words and was gone in 1.9 seconds; I read at about two words a second, so I cannot finish it before it disappears, and I was looking at the mouth anyway. The cell reverting is the only feedback that stays. I finished the other 166 sites (gseq 210-375) and pressed Save: "Name why 1 sites were not probed" with one button "Choose a reason" and Save turned into a locked "Held" (gseq 376-380). The task sheet says to press `perio.licence.<code>` next, but the licence buttons do not exist until "Choose a reason" is pressed (gseq 381-384), so the save took four taps: Save, Choose a reason, Patient could not tolerate, "Save exam with this reason" (gseq 385-393). The note line then read "Perio: 167 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 0 sites, 1 not probed (not_tolerated)." The underscore code is in my clinical note. And "1 sites" appears three times: the verb line, the chooser heading "Why were 1 sites not probed?", and the chart status.

**hy-4, tag #30 for the dentist as a finding (2 taps, budget 3, done).** "Tag for dentist" opened a card "Tag for Dr. Kim" with the tooth box already holding "15" (the deepest pocket; I wanted 30), so I had to select it and type 30 (gseq 396-402), then type "suspected caries DO" and Enter on Save tag (gseq 403-427). The row landed as tooth 30, text as typed, disposition empty, author Bree Lawson, and the screen said "Tagged tooth 30 for the dentist. A finding, not a diagnosis." with a chip "Tagged #30 · waiting for dentist". The scope is respected, not policed: nobody asked me for a code or a surface. The helper text is 23 words (about 12 seconds), and the observation buttons ("Suspected caries", "Fractured restoration"...) are a good idea I did not need because I already knew what to type.

**hy-5, mark ready for exam (2 taps, budget 2, done).** Chairs, Enter; the card now carried "Perio charted today · 167 sites probed, deepest 6 mm" in the strip and a 44 px "Ready for exam" button (gseq 429-433). Enter on it wrote `encounter.exam_requested`, the status chip changed to "Exam requested", a chip "Exam: 1st in queue" appeared, and the screen reader line said "Marisol Vega ready for exam: 1st in queue. The Board chair strip shows Exam requested." (gseq 434-438). Focus moved to Note on the same card, which is the right next thing. The card is 72 words, and the anticoagulant alert is printed twice on it, once as a stop chip and once in the strip as "Med hx changed".

**hy-6, run the screening lane (3 taps, budget 2; done only after a fresh start).** As written, after hy-2 to hy-5: Perio opened the saved exam and "Screening" looked like a live button, not disabled, not Held; Enter on it did nothing, the mode stayed full, no refusal, no announcement (gseq 439-445). Same lock as hy-3. Fresh (gseq 446-450 Perio, 451-455 Screening): the grid disappeared and six 88 px boxes took its place, "Sextant UR (teeth 1–5) · keys 0–4 or *", focus moved onto the first box, and 2 1 3 2 1 2 filled them in six keystrokes with "Codes: 6/6 · Full chart due" (gseq 456-467). Obviously a different lane; nobody would mistake it for the full chart. Save was one Enter (gseq 468-473) and wrote a perioExams row with mode "screening". But the derived note read "Perio: 0 sites probed, deepest 0 mm, bleeding at 0 sites." and the announcement read that sentence aloud before "Full chart due". A screening that reads as zero sites probed is a wrong note. Three presses are on the task sheet against a budget of two.

**Probes (gseq 474-535).** Opening Perio leaves focus on the body; five Tabs land on "Save exam" and the sixth on the first cell (gseq 501-513). While focus sits on Save, digits still chart (the grammar listens on the document) and the screen re-focuses Save after each key, so the first Space for bleeding fired Save: "Name why 167 sites were not probed", Save went Held, and no bleeding was recorded (gseq 479-495, probe-space-on-save.png). With a complete chart that Space would have saved the exam for good. The glove pad's 13 keys measured 44 × 44 with 8 px between them and "Next tooth" 252 × 56; a pad key advanced the site and left the pad open, but one keyboard key closed it (gseq 527-532). Depth 17 via 0 then 7 was refused with "Depth 17 mm is above the 15 mm limit", nine words, one button "Re-enter the depth", and focus came back to the cell afterwards (gseq 517-524). Settings echoed "Last key pressed: 4 → Depth 4 mm at tooth 2 site 3, next site". On every screen I measured, no control was under 44 px and no two live controls were closer than 8 px; focus rings were 3 px solid rgb(43, 108, 184) with a 2 px offset; nothing scrolled sideways at 1024 px (grid wrap 992 of 992).
