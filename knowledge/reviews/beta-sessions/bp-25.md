# The keys charted a mouth I had never seen faster than Dentrix ever did, but the glove pad and the lower arch open where a gloved hygienist cannot see them

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Ingrid, 48, nineteen years a hygienist and the last four through a temp agency, so every Monday is a new office, a new front desk, and a new chart I have to learn before the 8:00 patient sits down; the last office ran Dentrix, so I think in its words: perio chart, auto-advance, click the site for bleeding, recall due.
Tech comfort 3 of 5, no access adjustments; I test at the speed of a busy morning and I judge a screen by whether I can run it without looking up from the mouth.
Operatory screen at 1024 by 768, dark theme, motion on auto; I work gloved and often with wet hands, so any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a target I will hit, and I measured every one on every screen I touched.

## What I did

Event numbers below are the `gseq` field in events.json. The prototype's `reset()` wipes `window.__events`, and I had to start fresh twice (hy-3 and hy-6, explained there) and once more for the probes, so I kept one running count across the segments (the resets fall at gseq 201, 465 and 493). Taps are physical presses. The contract's literal rule counts a keyboard Enter twice (the `key` and the browser's synthesized `click`, e.g. gseq 3-4) and counts the two spaces inside "suspected caries DO" as taps because the text box carries a testid (gseq 433, 440); the literal count is in the JSON as `taps_literal` so nobody has to redo the arithmetic.

**hy-1, open Perio for Marisol Vega from my first card (1 tap, budget 1, done; gseq 1-4).** This is the card I want as a temp. I have never met Marisol Vega, and before I touched anything the 9:00 card told me: a red stop chip "New anticoagulant (apixaban), intake 2 days ago", then the strip "Since last visit · Med hx changed · Perio 14 mo ago · BWX due, practice rule: 12 mo · What helped last time: stop signal, sunglasses". What changed, what is due, what helped, zero taps, and it says "practice rule" so I know whose rule the 12 months is. Two things bothered me. Marisol was not my first card: the first card was "7:30 · Devon Price · Filed later · Chair 1 · Hygiene · Minor: guardian Alicia Price", a patient who left an hour ago and whose unfiled note belongs to whoever was in this chair before me; on my first morning in an office that card reads as my problem. And after Enter on Perio the grid was up with tooth 2 site 1 highlighted and every cell ghosting the July 2025 depth (168 ghosts), but my keyboard focus was on the page body (`document.activeElement` = BODY, gseq 4). The ghosts are 10 px type in the corner of a 44 px cell, light grey on the dark cell (7.2:1, so the contrast is fine); at arm's length on the operatory monitor they are dust, and I would not chart against them. The subtitle is 37 words in one line and took me two reads. The third button on every card says "Rail"; in Dentrix I would look for "Patient chart", and "Rail" told me nothing.

**hy-2, full mouth, keyboard only, one pass (1 tap, budget 1; 188 keystrokes, budget 200; done; gseq 5-197).** I typed 168 depths with 19 Spaces for bleeding straight through and checked the cursor against where it should be after every 21 keys: it never jumped, not once (0 jumps at 9 checkpoints). The line above the grid kept me oriented: "Tooth 8 · site 2 · prior 2 · Sites recorded: 19/168", later "Tooth 28 · site 3 · prior 3 · 95/168", and the stamp under the grid read "Saved to tooth #31 (draft, this session)". But from the 85th digit (gseq 98) to the last (gseq 191) the active cell was below the fold: tooth 28 site 3 sat at y 872 and tooth 20 site 4 at y 924 on a 768 px screen, and nothing scrolled; the Lower header itself is at y 749 with the grid unscrolled. I charted the whole lower arch off the orientation line, never seeing a cell. Save was one Enter (gseq 193), no licence asked, and the card that came back is the note I would have typed by hand in Dentrix: "Perio: 168 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 19 sites." then "SRP evidence: 19 sites at or above 5 mm." then "Chart status: full-mouth six-point chart recorded on 168 sites." and "Next visit (practice policy, not a recommendation): 4-month perio maintenance with BWX". Focus landed on "Tag for dentist". This task is why I would want this system in the next office.

**hy-3, undo a mistyped depth and skip a site without recording zero (as sequenced: failed, 0 taps, gseq 198-201; after a fresh start: done, 0 taps for the undo and skip against a budget of 0, then 4 taps to save with the licence; gseq 203-414).** Run as written, straight after hy-2, nothing happened: the line read "Exam saved · grid is read-only", focus was on "Tag for dentist", and 9, Backspace, 3, Right went to that button and did nothing; no flash, no message, no Save, no way to open a second chart (gseq 198-201, hy-3-as-written.png). So I reset and opened Perio again (gseq 203-206). Fresh: 9 put "9" in tooth 2 site 1 and moved on; Backspace put "—" back and the flash read "Undo: removed 9 at tooth 2 site 1" for two seconds (gseq 208, cleared after 2000 ms); 3 went in; Right made site 2 "not probed, prior 2" and the counter read "1/168 · 1 not probed" (gseq 209-210). The stored row has site 2 as depth null, skipped true, never a 0. But look at hy-3-undo-flash.png: the undo flash is not on the screen. The page had opened scrolled (the scroll position from the previous screen carried across the route change) and the flash rendered at y 61, behind the fixed top bar whose bottom is at y 110; the orientation line and the mode buttons were under the bar too. With my eyes in the mouth I would have had no undo feedback at all, and even looking up I had none. I typed the rest of the mouth (gseq 211-394) and pressed Save (gseq 396-398): "Name why 1 sites were not probed" with one 176 by 44 px button "Choose a reason", and Save turned into an outlined "Held". The reason chooser was not there yet; one more Enter on "Choose a reason" opened "Why were 1 sites not probed?" with four reasons, I chose Crown margin, and the confirm changed from "Choose a reason above" to "Save exam with this reason" (gseq 401-411). The script said two licence taps; it took three, four with Save. The note came out honest: "Perio: 167 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 18 sites, 1 not probed (crown_margin)." and "Chart status: partial chart; 1 sites not probed (Crown margin). This never reads as a full chart." It says "1 sites" three times on one screen, and the note prints the code "crown_margin" with the underscore where the card prints "Crown margin".

**hy-4, tag #30 for the dentist as a finding (2 taps, budget 3, done; gseq 415-448).** Enter on "Tag for dentist" opened "Tag for Dr. Kim" with the tooth box already holding "6", the deepest pocket in my chart, not the tooth I meant (gseq 415-417, hy-4-open.png). If I had typed my note and pressed Enter I would have tagged tooth 6 with "suspected caries DO". Ctrl+A, 30, Tab landed on "Observation" as it should, I typed the words, Enter on Save tag. The row is tag-2, tooth 30, "suspected caries DO", disposition null, author Bree Lawson. The copy under the heading reads "A finding, not a diagnosis. The tag lands as a checklist row on the dentist's exam; sign refuses until it has a disposition.", the chip after says "Tagged #30 · waiting for dentist", and the announcement repeated "A finding, not a diagnosis." The six observation buttons (all 44 px tall, 96 to 210 px wide, 8 px apart) were offered, not forced, and nothing policed my words. That is my scope described the way I would describe it to a new dentist.

**hy-5, mark ready for exam (2 taps, budget 2, done; gseq 449-458).** Enter on Chairs; focus went to the body again after the route change (gseq 452), and the list opened scrolled to the middle: Devon's card gone, Marisol's card cut off at "intake 2 days ago", the "Ready for exam" button at y 380 (hy-5-before.png). The card now carried "Perio charted today · 167 sites probed, deepest 6 mm" in the strip and a 290 by 44 px "Ready for exam" button. Enter wrote `encounter.exam_requested` (ae-1), the status chip became "Exam requested", a chip "Exam: 1st in queue" appeared, the announcement said "Marisol Vega ready for exam: 1st in queue. The Board chair strip shows Exam requested.", and focus moved to Note on the same card. Clean, and the queue position is exactly what I would want to tell the patient.

**hy-6, run the screening lane (as sequenced: failed, 2 taps, gseq 459-465; after a fresh start: done, 3 taps against a budget of 2; 6 keystrokes, budget 12; gseq 467-493).** As written, after hy-2 to hy-5, Perio reopened the saved exam and "Screening" looked live (not disabled, not Held, aria-pressed false); Enter did nothing, no refusal, no announcement (gseq 463-465, hy-6-as-written.png). Fresh (gseq 467-493): Perio, Screening, and the grid was replaced by six 325 by 88 px boxes "UR · 1–5", "UA · 6–11" and so on with the line "Sextant UR (teeth 1–5) · keys 0–4 or *"; yes, it is obviously a different lane. I typed 2 1 3 0 2 1; the boxes filled, UL went orange for the 3, the line read "All six sextants coded · Save exam · Codes: 6/6 · Full chart due". But the keyboard focus ring stayed on the UR box the whole time while the active box moved on (gseq 476-487, hy-6-coded.png), so two boxes looked current; the subtitle still said "Keys: 1–9 depth · Space bleed · S suppuration" and the legend under the boxes still said "small grey = prior exam · ● bleeding", the full-chart text. Save gave "Screening saved · exam pe-2 · Screening codes: UR 2 · UA 1 · UL 3 · LL 0 · LA 2 · LR 1 · Full chart due" and "Next visit: Full chart due: a full six-point chart is booked into the next hygiene visit", which is right. Then the note the same save wrote, and the announcement: "Perio: 0 sites probed, deepest 0 mm, bleeding at 0 sites." (gseq 491-493). A screening that writes "deepest 0 mm" into my note is a chart I did not do, under someone's name. The script's own steps (Perio, Screening, Save) are three taps against a budget of two.

**Probes (gseq 495-548).** Gloved measurements first: 33 controls on Chairs, 207 on Perio, 220 with the glove pad open, 21 in the screening lane, every one at least 44 by 44 px and none closer than 8 px to a neighbour (the grid's border-spacing is 8 px; the pad keys are 44 by 44 on an 8 px gap; the observation buttons and the licence buttons the same). The focus ring in dark is 3 px solid rgb(143,193,245) with a 2 px offset on a card button and on a cell; no horizontal overflow at 1024 px (grid 992 px in a 992 px wrap). I could not find one gloved miss on size or spacing. Now the bad news for a gloved hand. (1) The glove pad, the control built for me, opens below the fold: Enter on "Glove pad" at y 212 flipped the label to "Hide glove pad" and nothing on the screen changed, because the pad rendered at y 1135 to 1347 on a 768 px viewport (gseq 523-526, probe-pad-viewport.png). When I scrolled to it, it worked: 4, 5, Bleed gave "4", "5●" (gseq 527-535); the next keyboard digit hid the pad again (gseq 536), which is by design but will surprise anyone mixing pad taps and keys. (2) After a Tab, or after any button press, Space means the button, not bleeding: with focus on Save I typed 4 (it charted) and pressed Space for bleeding; Save fired, the screen said "Name why 164 sites were not probed", Save went to Held, and no bleeding was recorded (gseq 537-543, probe-space-on-save.png). With a full mouth entered that same Space would have saved the exam irreversibly without my last bleeding mark. (3) The over-15 refusal reads "Depth 17 mm is above the 15 mm limit", nine words against the contract's eight; the control "Re-enter the depth" put focus back on the cell and the cell kept its "—" (gseq 516-522). (4) Settings, where a temp would go to learn the keys, opens at y 1249, also off-screen; it reads "Last key pressed: 4 → Depth 4 mm at tooth 3 site 1, next site" and "Keystrokes this exam: 7 (counted, never scored per person)", and the grammar is a collapsed "The five-key grammar" inside it. The practice line under Chairs reads "Perio completion this week: 71% (practice)" with no per-person number anywhere; as a temp who has been scored by a spreadsheet before, I checked.
