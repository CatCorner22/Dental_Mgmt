# Three lines to read back instead of forty-one, but the hold told me to chase the wrong dentist

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Marcus, 29, six years posting insurance; before this office we ran Eaglesoft, so "day sheet", "EOB", and "adjustment" are the words in my head.
My tech comfort is 1 of 5: I learn a screen by doing the same thing every morning until my hands know it, and I do not read policy prose.
I am hard of hearing, so I test that nothing depends on a sound cue and that every state change shows up in place, on the thing I just touched; desk monitor, light theme, motion on auto.

## What I did

**bo-1, post the matched Delta lines and read back only the differing ones (4 taps, budget 4, done).** The ERA tab opened with "Delta 835 · 41 lines · 37 posted before you sat down · EFT $4,812.33 · TRN 20260903-90112 · matched to bank line" and three chips, "37 posted · 3 deltas · 1 denial". I pressed Post matched and the card changed to "Read back: 3 lines differ from the claim" with exactly three rows, not forty-one (seq 2-3). Each row said it plainly: "Cole Fischer · Line 14 · D2740 Crown, porcelain/ceramic · #19 · Contract variance / expected $590.00, ERA says $540.00 · CARC 45 (contract underpayment)". That is the line I used to build by hand from the EOB. Confirm on 14, Hold on 22, Dispute contract variance on 31; each time the row I finished disappeared and the chips moved in place ("38 posted · 2 deltas", then "1 held", then "1 disputed"), so I never needed the announcement to know it took (seq 4-15). The end card read "Batch complete: 38 posted, 1 held, 1 disputed, 1 denial moved to Denials". The Confirm button's long name, "Confirm line 14: post $540.00 and a contractual write-off of $50.00", is only in the aria-label; the button itself just says Confirm. I would like the $50.00 on the screen, since that is the number I am agreeing to write off.

**bo-2, appeal c-88 from the record (3 taps, budget 2, done, one over).** Denials tab: "Ines Aziz $285.00 · c-88 · D4341 SRP, 4+ teeth per quadrant · Delta Dental · Appeal by 11/2", then "Delta says the claim is missing information: the perio chart was not attached." and "Next: Appeal with the perio chart and the SRP narrative from the note" (seq 16-18). That is CARC 16 / N4 decoded without me opening a code book; the codes themselves are under a "Why this code" fold. Appeal built "Appeal packet · ap-1 · built from the record" with four slots all marked Clear: perio chart pe-1 dated 7/1/2025, narrative from the filed note, bitewings, letter (seq 19-21). "What the patient reads: Delta asked for your gum chart; we are sending it. You owe nothing while they review." Send wrote the disclosure and the tab flipped to "No open denials · Appeal for c-88 sent · disclosure recorded." visible on the page (seq 22-25). The extra tap is the Denials tab itself; after the batch completed the cursor was put on the Denials tab for me, but I still had to press it.

**bo-3, try to bill the patient for the denied claim (0 taps, budget 1, NOT completed as scripted).** There was no Bill patient button to press. Once I sent the appeal in bo-2, c-88 left the Denials list entirely and the screen read "No open denials"; Aging shows only submitted and pended claims, so an appealed claim is on no worklist at all (seq 22-26). I could not run the step. I opened a fresh copy of the prototype and pressed Bill patient before appealing (probe-bill-events.json, seq 5-9): the row grew one line, "Appeal first — denial with no appeal", seven words, one 44 px button "Build appeal", a Why fold, and nothing posted. Pressing Build appeal opened the same packet and put my cursor on Send. That gate I understood without reading the Why. But in the scripted order the task is not runnable, and the bigger problem is that after I appeal, I have nowhere to look for c-88.

**bo-4, $410 courtesy write-off on Lena Fischer, held not dead (5 taps, budget 4, NOT completed).** The write-off card only lives under the ERA and Aging tabs, and I was on Denials, so the first press was the ERA tab (seq 27). "Lena Fischer $410.00 open · Crown #19 · MetLife paid; patient portion outstanding since 9/1", Write-off or adjustment, amount prefilled 410.00 with "At or above $150.00 a second approver is needed; the posting is held, never silently allowed.", Courtesy, Post (seq 30-36). The gate came up in place: "Needs a second approver — Dr. Reagan or Dr. Kim" with one button, "Request approval" (seq 38). I pressed it; Post turned into an outlined "🔒 Held", a chip read "Request ar-1 waiting", and under it: "Dana or Dr. Reagan will see it on their phone; this flips to Posted when they approve." (seq 40-41). So the same card told me two different pairs of people. The seed and the phone card say Dana or Dr. Reagan; the red-railed line I read first says Dr. Kim. If I walked down the hall I would have interrupted Dr. Kim mid-prep for nothing. The verb line is also ten words, over the eight the contract allows. And the refusal box with its live "Request approval" button stayed on screen next to Held, so I had two controls for one held state; pressing Request approval a second time did not write a second request (probe, approvals stayed at 1), but it looked like it was still waiting on me. Pressing Held changed nothing I could see (seq 42). The Approvals tab did show "Waiting $410.00 Write-off $410.00 on Lena Fischer (courtesy) requested by Sam Dawson at 08:40 ar-1" (seq 44).

**bo-5, "Office Journal" and "walkout statement" through the palette (3 taps by the contract's count, budget 4, done).** Ctrl+K opened the box with the placeholder "Type three letters… try walkout statement, day sheet, Office Journal". "office journal" gave one row, "Audit log (sentences) · office journal — called that in Dentrix", and pressing it took me to Practice risk (seq 47-66). "walkout" gave "Statement · walkout statement — called that in Dentrix" and took me back to the Money Desk (seq 67-79). Both worked first try. The two words in the script are Dentrix words, not mine, so I tried my own (seq 80-138, not counted against the task): "day sheet" and "deposit slip" both came back "Daily Close · called that in Eaglesoft", "eob" came back "ERA batch", "ledger" came back "Ledger (three numbers and Explain)". The words of my last office worked. Two of the three taps are the row presses; the third is the space I typed between "office" and "journal", which the tap formula counts because it is a space on a control with a test id.

**bo-6, send a held statement with its reason visible (3 taps, budget 3, done).** Statements tab: "Hana Lindqvist $84.00 · held: window deferral · deferred at the window 9/2 so insurance could settle first" (seq 140). Preview is outlined teal; Send is filled navy with the bar glyph, so I could tell the one that comes back from the one that does not before I touched either. Preview pressed in (blue fill) and opened a box under the buttons whose first line was the patient sentence: "Your share is $84.00 after insurance. We held this statement so your plan could settle first; nothing here is an estimate." (seq 143-145). Send removed Hana's row and the tab count went from 2 to 1; that is all that changed on the page. "Statement sent · disclosure recorded" went only to the screen-reader region, which is invisible (seq 146-148). The appeal in bo-2 left a sentence on the page; the statement did not. For an irreversible send, I want the word Sent where the row was.

**Interview probe, Ines Okoro from the Ledger (seq 149).** Balance $0.00, Patient due $0.00, Waiting on insurance $0.00, and three rows: SRP charge $217.00 on 7/14, Delta paid $156.00 on 8/2, MetLife paid $61.00 on 8/20. One sentence: "Ines, both plans paid the SRP in full, so you owe nothing." I could say that from this screen.
