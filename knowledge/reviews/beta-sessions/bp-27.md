# The glove pad is the first one I could hit with a wet glove, but I typed my PIN and the tablet kept saying it was Priya

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Theo, 28, five years as a surgical dental assistant; the last office ran Eaglesoft, so "OnSchedule", "the chart", and "who's logged in on the op computer" are the words in my head.
I have ADHD and I lose my place when a screen changes under me, so I watch for every modal that steals focus and every moment the thing I was working on moves.
At the chair I work gloved, often wet: anything under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a defect I will hit. Operatory tablet at 1024 px, light theme, reduced motion.

## What I did

**da-1, switch author by PIN on the shared tablet (6 taps, budget 6, NOT completed).** I tapped the "PR PR" chip in the top bar and a "Who is charting?" dialog opened with my cursor already on key 1. The hint read "Enter the other person's PIN. Their session opens on this page; yours is revoked and local drafts are wiped after autosave." The keys measured 72 × 56 px with an 8 px gap, so a gloved thumb has no trouble; the dots showed "••••" as I went. I tapped 3-3-3-3 and Go. The dialog closed, a `sessions` row was written (seq 11, `sess-u-da-1`), my focus came back to the chip, and the chip still said "PR PR" (seq 10-12). No "Now charting as Jo Ramirez", nothing on the screen changed, and `Proto.store.currentUser()` was still Priya Raman. In a separate probe I typed Bree's PIN 1111 the same way and the chip flipped to "BL BL · RDH" with "Now charting as Bree Lawson" announced, so the pad works for hygienists and dentists and silently swallows an RDA. That is the exact Eaglesoft problem I came here to get away from: my PIN goes in and the hygienist's name comes out on what I chart. Smaller thing: the chip reads "PR PR" and "BL BL · RDH", the initials twice, on the shared and operatory devices (seq 1-2, da-1.png).

**da-2, arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 taps, budget 2, done).** Arrive on the 9:00 card; the screen said "Marisol Vega arrived. Seat is the next step on the same card" and the cursor was already on Seat (seq 14-17). Seat; "Seated in chair 1. The chair strip now shows BL." The card flipped to "In chart" and the strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH". Two appointmentEvents writes, status seated. The one place I lost the object: after Seat my focus jumped from the card up to the chair strip at the top of the page (seq 18-21), so the card I had just been working on was no longer under my hand. Privacy check with `?privacy=1` (separate probe run): every card read initials only, "9:00 am · MV", "Chair 1 · —" before seating, and none of the seven seed names appeared anywhere on the page; the top-bar Privacy button toggles it back and forth with a full re-render. Good.

**da-3, open Perio with the glove pad and record 12 sites (14 taps, budget 15, done; 13 were the task and 1 was a Bleed probe).** The route landed me in Perio as Bree ("BL BL · RDH" in the chip) with "Chair 1 · Prior exam 7/1/2025 ghosted · 28 teeth (x = missing: 1, 16, 17, 32)". I tapped "Glove pad" and the pad appeared: every key 44 × 44 px, gaps exactly 8.0 px, Next tooth 252 × 56 px, focus ring 3 px solid blue with a 2 px offset when reached by keyboard, no horizontal overflow at 1024. Twelve taps on 3 walked "Tooth 2 · site 1" through "Tooth 6 · site 1", "Sites recorded: 12/168", 12 cells painted, and my focus stayed on the 3 key every time even though the pad is rebuilt on each tap (seq 26-49). Bleed marked the last site "3 mm, bleeding" (seq 51-53). What confused me: the pad opened at 1135 px down a 768 px screen (seq 24-25). The toggle and the "Tooth 2 · site 1" line stayed in view and the pad did not; I had to scroll the grid past to reach it, and once the pad was under my thumb the heading, the "Tooth n · site n" line, the flash line, and the whole upper arch were off the top of the screen (probe-pad-inview.png). For the upper arch I was tapping blind, and when I tapped Bleed nothing I could see changed, because the only confirmation is the dot in a cell 166 px above the viewport.

**da-4, chair strip shows initials and no patient data (0 taps, budget 0, done; 2 probe taps at seq 55-58 to open and close Chair 1).** The strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH", "Chair 2 · HK DDS", "Chair 3 · HK DDS"; the labels say "Chair 1, author Bree Lawson, RDH. Show device author", and opening Chair 1 adds one line, "Bree Lawson, RDH is the author on the Op 1 device". No patient names, no counts. Chairs 2 and 3 both say HK, which is what the seed says. Note that this is the same failure as da-1 seen from the other side: I seated that patient after entering my PIN and the device author stayed Bree. One thing about arriving here: the Board opened with the canvas already scrolled 579 px from the Perio page, heading off the top (seq 54-55, da-4.png), so I landed mid-page with no title to orient on.

**da-5, day pass for Sam Lee, then the first-shift rail (9 taps, budget 8, NOT completed; 8 were the script and 1 was the "Arrive" chip the watch line asked me to try).** Roles opened from the nav; "Add day pass" put my cursor in Name; I typed Sam Lee, tapped Front desk and Main Street, and the preview said "Will grant: Schedule · Post payments until 5:30 pm + 30 min grace · Nonclinical role · no licence needed · No SoD conflicts". "Issue day pass" wrote dp-1 and the card read "Day pass issued to Sam Lee · Front desk · expires 5:30 pm + 30 min grace". "Sign in as this temp" took me to #/temp/board as "SL SL". Then the rail said "Your first shift: Arrive ✓ Seat ✓ Checkout Take payment Find a patient" before Sam had touched anything (seq 85-86): Priya's arrive and seat from da-2 had already retired her chips. I tapped the Arrive chip anyway: with reduced motion the pulse animation runs 0 seconds and the announcement lives in a screen-reader-only div, so nothing visible pointed at anything (seq 86-87, da-5-pulse.png); the Board only scrolled. I arrived Hank Haddad (a-1061; a-1043 has no Arrive, as the script warned) and the chip stayed "Arrive ✓" with no firstRunState write because it was already retired (seq 88-91). In a fresh-store probe the mechanism itself works: Arrive on a-1042 flipped the chip to "Arrive ✓" and wrote `frs-arrive`. So the rail is right about the steps and wrong about whose steps they were.
