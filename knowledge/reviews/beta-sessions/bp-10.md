# The ERA and the appeal are faster than anything in Open Dental; the write-off gate cannot decide who I am waiting on, and half my old words find nothing

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Owen, 19, one year as practice administrator; the last office ran Open Dental, so my words are "Account", "Claims", "Enter Payment", "Adjustment", "Billing", "Aging Report", "Commlog".
Tech comfort 5 of 5: I read the key hints on a screen before I touch it, I try the shortcut first, and I go looking for the reason when a screen refuses me.
I use a wheelchair; my desk height is fixed and the monitor sits at arm's length, so I checked that every target and every line of text still holds at 820 px and 1024 px wide, in the dark theme, on the desk profile.

## What I did

**bo-1, post the matched Delta lines and read back only the differing ones (4 taps, budget 4, done).** The desk opened on ERA: "Delta 835 · 41 lines · 37 posted before you sat down · EFT $4,812.33 · TRN 20260903-90112 · matched to bank line", chips "37 posted · 3 deltas · 1 denial", one filled button Post matched with "Posts the 37 matched lines; only lines that differ from the claim will ask you." I pressed it (seq 2) and the card became "Read back: 3 lines differ from the claim" with exactly three rows, not forty-one. Each row is the sentence I used to build by hand in Open Dental's Enter Payment window: "Cole Fischer · Line 14 · D2740 Crown, porcelain/ceramic · #19 · Contract variance" then "expected $590.00, ERA says $540.00 · CARC 45 (contract underpayment)". Line 31 read "expected $260.00, ERA says $208.00 · CARC 131 (downcoded to D2391)"; the CARC is decoded in the same line, not in a lookup. Confirm on 14 wrote the insurance payment (-$540.00) and the contractual write-off (-$50.00) at seq 5-6; Hold on 22 and Dispute contract variance on 31 each removed their row and moved the chips ("38 posted · 2 deltas", then "1 held · 1 disputed"); the end card read "Batch complete: 38 posted, 1 held, 1 disputed, 1 denial moved to Denials." One thing I noticed as someone who reads buttons: the visible label is just "Confirm"; the $50.00 I am agreeing to write off lives only in the aria-label ("Confirm line 14: post $540.00 and a contractual write-off of $50.00"). I can subtract, but the number should be on the row.

**bo-2, appeal c-88 from the record (3 taps, budget 2, done, one over).** When the batch completed my focus landed on the Denials tab (seq 15) but the selected tab was still ERA and the ERA card was still on screen, so I pressed the tab myself (seq 16); that is the tap over budget. Denials read "Ines Aziz $285.00 · c-88 · D4341 SRP, 4+ teeth per quadrant · Delta Dental · Appeal by 11/2", then "Delta says the claim is missing information: the perio chart was not attached." and "Next: Appeal with the perio chart and the SRP narrative from the note". That is CARC 16 / N4 in English with one deterministic next step; the codes sit under a "Why this code" fold I opened only to check. Appeal (seq 19) built "Appeal packet · ap-1 · built from the record" with four slots all marked Clear (perio chart pe-1 dated 7/1/2025, narrative from the filed note, bitewings, letter) and the line "What the patient reads: Delta asked for your gum chart; we are sending it. You owe nothing while they review." Focus was already on Send, whose caption said "Irreversible: writes a disclosure row with artifact hashes." I pressed it (seq 22); the claim went to appealed, disclosure dis-1 was written (seq 23-24), and the tab read "No open denials · Appeal for c-88 sent · disclosure recorded." In Open Dental that was the Claims window, the Perio module, a Word narrative, and the Commlog. Here it was two presses after the tab.

**bo-3, try to bill the patient for the denied claim (0 taps, budget 1, NOT completed).** There was nothing to press. After Send the Denials tab shows "No open denials" and the row that carried Appeal / Fix / Bill patient is gone (seq 22-26); c-88 is status appealed and appears on no worklist. The task cannot run in this order. To see the gate at all I opened a fresh copy (probe-bill-events.json, seq 1-7): Bill patient before appealing gave "Appeal first — denial with no appeal" (six words), one 141 x 44 px control "Build appeal", a Why fold, refusal code denial_suppression, and the ledger stayed at 114 rows. I understood it without opening Why, and Build appeal opened the same packet with focus on Send (probe seq 8-10). The gate is good. The ordering is the defect: an appealed claim needs somewhere to live.

**bo-4, $410 courtesy write-off on Lena Fischer, held not dead (5 taps, budget 4, done, one over).** I was on Denials and there was no write-off control; the Open balances card only renders under ERA and Aging, so the first press was the ERA tab (seq 27). The same card now named two different pairs an inch apart, and the red line with its live Request approval button stayed on screen beside Held, so the card carried five buttons for one waiting state. But I do not know which door to knock on.

"write-off" with a hyphen matched the same row (259-271), but "write off" (246-258) and "writeoff" (272-283) found nothing. "account" (76-86), "account module" (87-104), "claim"/"claims" (119-137), "insurance payment" (138-158), "ins pay", "batch ins", "billing" (183-193), "aging"/"aging report" (221-245), "payment plan", "recall", "commlog" (300-310), "appeal", "denial"/"denials" (435-465), "fee schedule", "security log" all returned "Nothing matches … Patient search does not widen to phonetic matches; try the last four digits of the phone or the MRN." "eob", "era", "day sheet", "deposit", "ledger", "okoro", "c-88", "delta" all worked. Score for an Open Dental person: one word in, ten words out.

"Statement sent · disclosure recorded" went only to the 1 px screen-reader region; there is no visible Sent where the row was. The appeal left a sentence behind; the statement did not.
