# Every button is big enough for my gloved hand and the whole tooth took eight presses; but the one button I will press twice by accident charted #30 twice, and the letter keys that would save me thirty-six Tabs are a secret

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Hannah, 67, twenty-seven years a general dentist; the last office ran Open Dental, so my words are the Chart module, the Tx Plan, the procedure list, Ins Est, and "set complete".
Tech comfort 1 of 5: I use keys before the mouse because my hands shake, and I read every screen slowly before I touch it; I do not open a Why fold unless the screen has stopped me.
I work gloved and often wet at the chair in an operatory, dark theme: I measured every target I had to hit, and any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a defect I will hit, as is any control that undoes itself when my finger lands twice.

## What I did

The driver ran three page loads and two probe loads, all keyboard: focus the control by its test id, press Enter. Load A carried dn-1 into dn-2 as one state. Load B redid the six setup presses (open, Chart it, D, O, Composite, starter; seq 1001-1027, not counted) because dn-3 has to happen before a note is filed; dn-4 read the card in that same state. Load C redid the setup with `?device=shared` for dn-5. In `events.json` load A keeps its seqs, B is offset by +1000, C by +2000, the letter-key probe (K) by +3000 and the focus and Tab-order probe (P) by +4000; the raw logs are `events-A.json` through `events-P.json` beside it. One accounting note first: every Enter I pressed on a button is in the log twice, once as `key Enter` and once as the `click` the browser makes from it (seq 2-3, 6-7, 11-12 and so on), so the contract's own tap formula in §5 scores my eight-press flow as 16. The taps below are presses; the formula's numbers are given beside them.

**dn-1, open Theo Brandt from Exams to sign (1 press, budget 1, done; formula 2).** The queue read "Exams to sign · 3 waiting · ordered by time in chair": Ines Okoro "Waiting 26 min · Note ready for signature", Theo Brandt "Waiting 14 min · Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L." with a chip "2 BWX, no interpretation", then Devon Price "Out of chair · 7:30 am visit · Checked out, note unfiled — filing releases the held payment". Longest in the chair on top, and Bree's finding is on the row before I open it; in Open Dental I had to open the chart to find out why the hygienist wanted me. Enter on Open (seq 2-4) took me to `#/dentist/encounter/enc-9002`. The first section under the patient head was "Hygienist tags · Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L. · Needs disposition · Chart it · Dismiss"; the tag sat at y = 274 of a 768 px screen because the sticky top bar wraps to two rows at 1024 px and takes 113 px, and the head another 80. I would call that first, with a tall hat on. Focus, though, went to the page body after the route change (seq 4), so the keyboard path to Chart it starts over from the top bar: Riverbend, Main St, Search, Exams to sign, Chairs, Board, the author chip, Privacy, Light, Sign out, Rail, Back to Exams, and then Chart it, thirteen stops.

**dn-2, chart #30 DO from the tag, plan, note, and File (7 presses here, 8 from the queue, budget 10, done; formula 14 and 16).** Chart it (seq 6-7) selected tooth 30, put focus on it, and the surface row showed O and D dashed: "Selected: #30 · D O", with the buttons reading "Distal, suggested by the tag, tap to confirm". D (seq 11-12) and O (15-16) turned solid and the line stayed "Selected: #30 · D O". Composite 2-surf D2392 (seq 19-20) wrote three rows in one press, chartEvents ce-1, procedures pr-500, planItems pl-1 (seq 21-23), and the card said so in words I can read chairside: "One paint, one transaction · Chart event Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO · ce-1 · Dr. Kim · Procedure D2392 Composite, 2 surf posterior · $260.00 pending charge, released at File · pr-500 · Note scaffold Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO · Plan card · pl-1 · $260.00 Fee · $130.00 Your plan pays about · $130.00 You'd owe about · Why · Undo last paint". Bree's tag flipped to Charted. The first starter, "Caries confirmed, composite today" (seq 26-27), filled Assessment "Caries #30 DO confirmed clinically and on BWX; asymptomatic; vitality normal." and Plan "Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given." and left the cursor at the end of Assessment for me. That is the note I would have typed, tooth and surfaces taken from my paint, not from a template's blanks. File (seq 31-32) stopped at the read-back (refusal readback, seq 33): "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt", eight words, one 182.6 x 44 control "Confirm and file", already focused. Enter on it (seq 36-37) wrote filedNotes nf-1, ledger le-5000 for $260.00 by the File event, and claims c-100 (seq 38-40), and the screen became "Filed · ruleset 2.25.2 · byteaudit verified · By Dr. Hana Kim at 2026-09-03 08:40 · nf-1 · text and version frozen; corrections are addenda, never edits. · Charges released: 1 · $260.00 #30 · Claim queued · c-100 to Cigna · Queued to clearinghouse". Eight presses from the queue to a filed note with the charge out; in Open Dental that was the chart, the procedure, set complete, the note, and the sign, on three tabs. The killer strip never appeared in this run because I never left a note field before File; the gate said "Nothing outstanding" at seq 33.

**dn-3, put money in the note and fix it with one control (2 presses on controls, budget 3, done; the formula says 6 because the four spaces I typed count as taps, and 8 with the doubled Enters).** Fresh chart, same six setup presses (seq 1001-1027). Before the task I did two things a shaking hand does. I pressed Enter twice on the B surface: once gave "Selected: #30 · D O B", twice took it back to "Selected: #30 · D O" (seq 1030-1036); that is the documented "tap once to confirm, again to remove", and it means a double press erases my surface. Then I pressed Enter a second time on Composite 2-surf D2392 (seq 1038-1039): it painted #30 DO again, ce-2, pr-501, pl-2 (seq 1040-1042), a second card and a second $260.00 pending charge, and the announcement said "Charted Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO. Pending charge $260.00" as if it were the first. Nothing said "#30 DO is already charted today." Undo last paint (seq 1045-1047) removed it and said so: "Undid Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO. Chart event, procedure, and plan item removed; nothing had reached the ledger." Then the task: I moved to the Plan field, pressed End, and typed " Patient portion $130 discussed" (seq 1049-1081). Shift+Tab out of the field (seq 1082-1083) brought the strip up at once, before I had touched File: "Before File · 1 to fix · Money stays out of the note · Move to plan card · Why" (refusal money_in_note at seq 1084), six words, one 182 x 44 control, and File had already turned into the outlined "Held". Enter on Held (seq 1087-1088) redrew the same strip (seq 1089) and did nothing else, which is right. Enter on Move to plan card (seq 1092-1093) rewrote my Plan to "Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given. See plan card for the quoted amount.", left Assessment alone, put a chip "Quoted to patient 9/3" on the plan card, and the gate returned to "Nothing outstanding · File". One line, one button, no lecture; the Why was there and I did not need it. But after the press my focus was on the page body (seq 1093): the row I was standing on vanished and the keyboard took me nowhere, so I would have Tabbed from the top again to reach File.

**dn-4, the plan card estimate and its rule trace (1 press, budget 1, done; formula 2).** The card read "Plan card · pl-1 · Quoted to patient 9/3 · $260.00 Fee · $130.00 Your plan pays about · $130.00 You'd owe about · Why". Enter on Why (seq 1095-1096, a 49 x 44 disclosure) opened "Cigna PPO: 50% after deductible (met) → patient est. $130.00. This is an estimate until the plan pays; it never joins the balance." Theo's primary carrier in the record is Cigna, so the trace names the right payer, the percentage, and the deductible. Could I defend it chairside? Carrier, percentage, deductible, yes, and "about" is the right word. What I would be asked and cannot answer from this card: the allowed amount against my $260 fee, and how much of his annual maximum is left. In Open Dental the Tx Plan showed Ins Est and Allowed side by side; here the fee is my fee and the trace does not say whether Cigna's allowed is the same number.

**dn-5, the read-back line before File on a shared device (2 presses, budget 2, done; formula 4).** Fresh load with `?device=shared`, same six setup presses (seq 2001-2027). The author chip in the bar read "DH  DH · DDS", the initials twice and then the licence, on this profile and on the operatory one; I read it twice to be sure it was one person. File (seq 2031-2032) stopped at the read-back (refusal readback, seq 2033): verb "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt", eight words, control "Confirm and file" at 182.6 x 44 with focus on it, the Why, then a second line "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for TB, DOB 11/1990, #30 DO" beside "Switch author" (154 x 44), and under that a 966 px outlined "Held — confirm the read-back". Nothing was written before I confirmed (filedNotes had no enc-9002 row at seq 2033). Enter on Confirm and file (seq 2036-2037) wrote nf-1, le-5000, c-100 (seq 2038-2040), author Dr. Hana Kim. Would it catch a stale author on a passed tablet? Yes: the author's full name is the second word of the line I must confirm, and Switch author is a press away. I would say the gate carries three controls where the contract promises one; the Held bar at the bottom is a fourth thing to read on a screen I am trying to leave.

**My measurements (gloved, shaking, dark theme, 1024 x 768).** Across eight screen states (Exams; encounter landing; after Chart it; after the paint; the read-back gate on operatory and on shared; the money strip; the plan card open) with 13 to 68 visible controls each, every button, field, and Why disclosure measured at least 44 x 44 px by getBoundingClientRect: 32 teeth at 44 x 44 with 8.00 px between them, five surfaces at 44 x 44 and 8.00 px apart, Chart it 99.9 x 44 and Dismiss 101.2 x 44, the starters 322, 254 and 154 by 44, the note fields 966 x 88, File 966 x 44, the Why folds 49 x 44. No two page controls sat closer than 8 px (the only pair under 8 was the off-screen skip link over the procedure strip, which is not on the same layer). No horizontal overflow: document and canvas scrollWidth 1024 at 1024. Focus is a 3 px solid rgb(143, 193, 245) ring on every control I landed on, and it always landed where the screen said it would (tooth 30 after Chart it, Assessment after the starter, Confirm and file after File). Contrast in the dark theme is generous: refusal verb 13.7:1 at 15 px, the killer control 10.6:1, muted lines 10.4:1 at 13 px, chips 9.0 to 11.4:1, plan-card labels 9.8:1 at 13 px, plan-card numbers 13.7:1 at 20 px. The 13 px muted text is the smallest thing I read and I would enlarge it at the chair. Verb lines: "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt" eight words, "Money stays out of the note" six, "Hygienist tag #30 has no disposition" six, "Assessment is empty" three; each with exactly one control in its row.

**Keyboard reach (the probe that matters most to me).** On a fresh load (P, seq 4000s) I Tabbed from Chart it to File: 56 stops. Every one of the 32 teeth is a Tab stop, so from tooth 30, where Chart it leaves me, it is 16 Tabs to the D surface, 19 to the procedure, and the arrow keys, which do move me along the arch, do not shorten Tab. On the letter-key probe (K), with focus on tooth 30 after Chart it, pressing d confirmed D and pressing o confirmed O with no tap at all (seq 3005-3009: two keystrokes, zero clicks); that is exactly the shortcut a hand like mine wants, and nothing on the screen says it exists. The only hint reads "A dashed surface came from the tag: tap once to confirm, again to remove." Tabbing through the two note fields on the way to File also armed the strip ("Before File · 2 to fix · Hygienist tag #30 has no disposition · Chart it or dismiss · Assessment is empty · Add assessment", refusals at seq 4017-4018 and 4124-4125) and turned File into Held before I had done anything wrong; I understand why, and it is honest, but a keyboard user passes through fields she does not mean to edit. Those two codes, `assessment_required` and the `tooth_required` I saw in the source, are not in the contract's list of codes in §6.

**Interview answers.** Would I sign hygiene-drafted notes here rather than bypass to a template? Yes, because they are not hygiene-drafted: Bree's finding is a tag that becomes my chart event only when I press Chart it, the tooth and surfaces in the note come from my paint, and the starter fills my Assessment in my words and leaves the cursor in it. In Open Dental the auto-note dropped blanks I had to hunt. Which killer would I resent, which would I thank? I thank the money one: it took my sentence out, put the amount on a dated plan card, and asked me nothing. I would resent the read-back on my own operatory, where I am the only author all day; on a passed tablet I want it. What would make me trust the estimate the front desk quotes? The trace already names Cigna, 50 percent and the deductible; add the allowed amount and the remaining maximum on the same card and I would let the desk read it to the patient without me.
