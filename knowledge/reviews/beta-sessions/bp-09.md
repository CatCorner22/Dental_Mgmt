# The ERA posted itself and read me three lines; on my phone the top bar ate a third of the screen and search lost the name of the thing it found

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Noor, 67, twenty-seven years in accounts receivable; my last office ran Dentrix, so my words are "Ledger", "Office Journal", "walkout", "Family File", "guarantor", "insurance aging".
Tech comfort 4 of 5: I will learn a new screen fast if it tells the truth about money, and I test at the speed of a busy morning with a payer on hold.
No access adjustments. Today I am on my phone, 420 by 860, light theme, motion left alone, tapping with a thumb.

## What I did

**bo-1, post the matched Delta lines and read back only the differing ones (4 taps, budget 4, done).** The Money Desk opened on ERA: "Delta 835 · 41 lines · 37 posted before you sat down · EFT $4,812.33 · TRN 20260903-90112 · matched to bank line", chips "37 posted · 3 deltas · 1 denial", and one filled button, Post matched, with "Posts the 37 matched lines; only lines that differ from the claim will ask you." One tap (seq 2) and the card became "Read back: 3 lines differ from the claim" with exactly three rows, not forty-one. Each row read like the sticky note I used to write off the paper EOB: "Cole Fischer · Line 14 · D2740 Crown, porcelain/ceramic · #19 · Contract variance · expected $590.00, ERA says $540.00 · CARC 45 (contract underpayment)"; the money line is ten words on two phone lines, about thirty words a row. Confirm on 14 wrote the insurance payment and the contractual write-off (seq 4-6), Hold on 22 (seq 9-10), Dispute contract variance on 31 (seq 13-14); the card ended "Batch complete: 38 posted, 1 held, 1 disputed, 1 denial moved to Denials." What the phone changed: each row is about 300 px tall, so only one of the three fits under the top bar at a time (row 1 at 364-700, row 2 starts at 708 of an 860 screen); I scrolled between every read-back. And the $50.00 I agreed to write off on line 14 is nowhere on the row or the button; it lives only in the hidden label "Confirm line 14: post $540.00 and a contractual write-off of $50.00". In Dentrix I saw the write-off amount before I posted it.

**bo-2, appeal c-88 from the record (3 taps, budget 2, done, one over).** After the batch finished, the cursor had moved to the Denials tab (seq 15) but the ERA card was still on the screen, so the tab was a tap of my own (seq 16). Denials read "Ines Aziz $285.00 · c-88 · D4341 SRP, 4+ teeth per quadrant · Delta Dental · Appeal by 11/2", then "Delta says the claim is missing information: the perio chart was not attached." and "Next: Appeal with the perio chart and the SRP narrative from the note." Thirteen words each; that is CARC 16 / N4 in English, and the codes are under a "Why this code" fold I did not open. On the phone the Appeal, Fix and Bill patient buttons sat at y 862, just under the bottom edge of an 860 screen, so I scrolled to reach them. Appeal (seq 19-20) opened "Appeal packet · ap-1 · built from the record" with four slots all Clear (perio chart pe-1 dated 7/1/2025, narrative from the filed note, bitewings, letter) and the box "What the patient reads: Delta asked for your gum chart; we are sending it. You owe nothing while they review." The Send button and that sentence were both in view; the drawer's Close button was not, it had slid up behind the sticky top bar (y 169, bar bottom 269). Send (seq 22-24) wrote the claim event and a disclosure, and the tab flipped to "No open denials · Appeal for c-88 sent · disclosure recorded." In Dentrix that appeal was a half hour with the perio module and a Word letter.

**bo-3, try to bill the patient for the denied claim (0 taps, budget 1, NOT completed as scripted).** There was nothing to tap. Once the appeal was sent, c-88 left the Denials tab and its Bill patient button went with it (seq 22-25; the selector `money.denial.c-88.bill` was absent). The scripted order cannot be run on this prototype. To see the gate at all I opened a fresh copy on the same phone (probe-bill-events.json, seq 5-7): Bill patient, before any appeal, gave an orange-railed line "Appeal first — denial with no appeal" (seven words), one 44 px button "Build appeal", a Why fold left closed, refusal code denial_suppression, and the ledger stayed at 114 rows. I understood it without opening Why. Build appeal opened the same packet and put me on Send. Good gate, wrong order in the script; and in real life I want to see c-88 somewhere with "appealed 9/3" on it, the way it sat in my Dentrix Insurance Aging with a status.

**bo-4, $410 courtesy write-off on Lena Fischer, held not dead (5 taps, budget 4, done, one over).** I was left on Denials and there was no write-off control there; it lives only under ERA and Aging, so the ERA tab was my first tap (seq 27). Then "Lena Fischer $410.00 open · Crown #19 · MetLife paid; patient portion outstanding since 9/1", Write-off or adjustment (seq 30), amount prefilled 410.00 with the cursor in it and the sentence "At or above $150.00 a second approver is needed; the posting is held, never silently allowed.", Courtesy (seq 33), Post (seq 36). Two things happened on the one tap: approval request ar-1 was written (seq 37) and an orange-railed line came up, "Needs a second approver — Dr. Reagan or Dr. Kim", with one button, "Request approval" (seq 38-39). On the phone that is all I could see: the verb sat at 729-772, the button at 780-824, and the Post button, which had already turned into the outlined padlock "Held", was at y 897, below the bottom of the screen. So from where I stood the request looked unmade until I tapped Request approval (seq 40). That tap wrote nothing new; it added a chip "Request ar-1 waiting" and the sentence "Dana or Dr. Reagan will see it on their phone; this flips to Posted when they approve." Now one card named two different pairs of people two inches apart, and the red line with its live Request approval button stayed on screen next to Held. Tapping Held afterwards only announced, to a screen reader I do not use, "Waiting on Dr. Reagan or Dr. Kim for request ar-1." Dana is the office manager; Dr. Kim has a patient in the chair. I only get to interrupt one of them. The Approvals tab did show "Waiting $410.00 · Write-off $410.00 on Lena Fischer (courtesy) requested by Sam Dawson at 08:40 · ar-1" (seq 43), which is the frozen sentence I would want the approver to read. Held, not dead: yes. But it told me the wrong names first, and on a phone it hid the Held state.

**bo-5, "Office Journal" and "walkout statement" through the palette (5 taps by the contract's count, budget 4, done, one over).** A phone has no Ctrl key, so I tapped "Search ⌘K" in the top bar (seq 46); the box opened with the placeholder "Type three letters… try walkout statement, day sheet, Office Journal", which is a fair hint but also a fair way to make the test pass. Typing "office journal" (seq 48-61) gave one row, and tapping it took me to Practice risk (seq 63-65). Back to Search, "walkout" (seq 67-75), one row, tap, Money Desk (seq 77-79). Both of my Dentrix words worked first try, and each row said "called that in Dentrix". But the row itself was broken at my width: the row is 364 px, and the name of the thing it found, the PMS word "Statement" or "Audit log (sentences)", was squeezed to zero pixels; on the walkout row the grey "Action" chip sat on top of the synonym text so I read "Actio walkout statement — called that in Dentrix" with no idea what screen I was about to open. The old word showed; the new word, the whole point of the translation, did not. The walkout row also landed me on the Approvals tab, where I had last been, not on Statements. Of the five taps the contract counts, one is the space bar between "office" and "journal" (seq 54). I then tried my own Dentrix words, not against the budget (seq 80-306): "ledger" found the Ledger ("called that in all"), "eob" found the ERA batch, "day sheet" found the Daily Close ("called that in Eaglesoft"), "adjustment" found the write-off. But "family file", "guarantor", "insurance", "aging", "account", "claim", "appeal", "denial" and "write off" with a space all came back empty. Half of my vocabulary works. Ctrl+K did open the box when I pressed it from a keyboard (seq 307-309), so a desk user is fine.

**bo-6, send a held statement with its reason visible (3 taps, budget 3, done).** Statements tab (seq 313): "Hana Lindqvist $84.00 · held: window deferral · deferred at the window 9/2 so insurance could settle first", then Send as a filled navy button with the bar glyph and Preview as an outlined teal one; I could tell which one comes back before I touched either. Preview (seq 316) pressed in, and the first line under the buttons was the patient's sentence, "Your share is $84.00 after insurance. We held this statement so your plan could settle first; nothing here is an estimate." It started at y 809 on an 860 screen, so I read the first line and scrolled for the rest. Send (seq 319-320) wrote the disclosure and Hana's row vanished; the tab count went 2 to 1. That was the only visible change. "Statement sent · disclosure recorded" went to a 1 by 1 px region for screen readers. On a busy morning a row that disappears looks the same whether I sent it or lost it.

**Interview probe, Ines Okoro from the Ledger (seq 322).** On the phone the three numbers were plain: Balance $0.00, Patient due $0.00, Waiting on insurance $0.00. The rows table, though, is 535 px wide in a 362 px box, and the Amount column, the one column I need, sits at 478-564, off the right edge; I would have to side-scroll a table with my thumb to see $217.00, −$156.00, −$61.00. Explain saved it: "SRP, 4+ teeth per quadrant on 7/14/2026: charge $217.00; Delta Dental paid $156.00 on 8/2; MetLife paid $61.00 on 8/20; paid in full." So to Ines, in one sentence: "Delta paid $156 and MetLife paid $61 on your $217 deep cleaning, so you owe nothing." I could say that from my phone in the parking lot.

**Measurements I took because I was on a phone.** Every control with a data-testid on every screen I used measured at least 44 by 44 px (refusal.verb at 331 by 43.5 is text, not a control), no two controls were closer than the 8 px gap (nearest measured 8.00), and no screen scrolled sideways (documentElement.scrollWidth 420). The sticky top bar wraps to five rows of buttons at this width, 269 px tall, 31% of the 860 px screen, and the working canvas underneath is 591 px; that is why the first primary action on ERA (Post matched, y 750) was in view but the write-off button (y 1055), the Denials row buttons (y 862), the write-off Post (y 1072) and the statement preview (y 809-971) were not. Focus rings after a tap were not drawn (`:focus-visible` false), which is right for a thumb.
