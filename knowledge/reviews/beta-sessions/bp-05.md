# By keys it is two presses to seat and three to collect, but on my phone I posted $44 before I could see whose name was on the screen

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Imani, 56, twenty-five years as a treatment coordinator; the last office ran Curve Hero, so I think in Scheduler, Sidekick, and Ledger.
I have an essential tremor, so I go to the keyboard before the mouse, I want every target at 44 px with 8 px of air around it, and today I am on a phone at 420 px wide, light theme.
Tech comfort 5 of 5: I measure what I hit (getBoundingClientRect on every control before I press it), I read the focus ring, and I count presses.

## What I did

**fo-1, arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 presses, budget 2, done).** I focused Arrive on the 9:00 card (99.6 x 44 px, focus ring 3 px solid rgb(43,108,184)) and pressed Enter; the card flipped to "Arrived" and the focus was already sitting on Seat (74.3 x 44), so the second Enter seated her and moved my focus onto "Chair 1 · BL RDH" in the chair strip. Store: a-1042 status seated, appointmentEvents ae-1 and ae-2 written (seq 4-13). Before arrival the card read "◆ Verify" with one 44 px "Re-verify" button beside it (94.5 x 44), one line and one control, exactly as promised; the strip above had a second place, "2 insured patients came back amber at 6 am — first at 9:00 am / Re-verify all". Two places for one job, but each one is honest. What I want on the record: the event log counted my two presses as four (seq 4-5 and 9-10 each show a `key Enter` and a `click` on the same testid), so by the contract's own tap formula a keyboard user's 2-press check-in scores 4 against a budget of 2. All 100 Board controls measured at least 44 x 44 and no two were closer than 8 px; the page did not scroll sideways (scrollWidth 420).

**fo-2, Ines Okoro, $44 by card (3 presses, budget 4, done; plus one Back to Board to continue).** Checkout from the card, "Collect" already pressed, Card, sixteen digits, Post: a collect decision cd-1 and a −$44.00 patient_payment are in the store (seq 14-45). But look at what my phone showed me when Checkout opened (seq 15-17): the screen landed on "Payment / Collect / Send statement / Card Cash Check / Amount 44.00 / Post". No name, no tiles, no procedures; the heading "Checkout · Ines Okoro" was 589 px above the top of the scroll area because the canvas kept the Board's scroll position (scrollTop 822 on a brand-new screen). So on a phone the estimate is "visibly separate from the balance" mostly by being invisible. I understood the lane; I could not explain $88.

**fo-4, Samir Haddad pays in full, off insurance (5 presses, budget 5, done by keyboard; plus one Back).** Checkout, Cash, both "Paid in full — don't send to insurance" toggles, Post: pr-421 and pr-422 are selfPayRestricted, cd-3 is a collect decision (seq 64-90). Now the part that matters for a tremor on a phone: both toggles sit at x 576-687 in a 420 px viewport, 267 px past the right edge, inside a table that scrolls sideways (scrollWidth 670, clientWidth 362). By touch I would have to drag the table 308 px to the left with a shaking finger to reach a 111 x 94 px button. By keyboard the focus() scrolled the table for me (seq 72-73), but then the Procedure column was gone (its right edge at −99 px), so I was pressing a toggle on a row whose name I could no longer see. And in tab order the toggles come before the tender row, so from Cash it is 19 Tabs forward to reach the first one. The toggles were on screen before I chose Cash (seq 67), because the Amount was prefilled at 168.00; the task sheet said they would appear once the tender covered the fee. Three numbers for one exam: Board Details "Balance $180.00 · patient portion, estimate separate", Checkout "$168.00 est.", task title "$180 exam" (seq 64-67; probe-samir-details.png).

**fo-5, patient at the window whose note is not filed (1 press of 3, NOT completed).** Devon Price's queue row already read "◆ Filed later · Note ◆ Open · BL · Claim ▬ Held · Note not filed — Bree L. · Ping chair · Paid at the window · in the Filed-later lane until Bree L. files". I could not run steps two and three, so the task failed as written. No gate.
