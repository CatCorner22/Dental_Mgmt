# Every key was big enough for my gloves; the one thing that failed was the tablet believing it was me

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Simone Baptiste (he/him), 35, eleven years as an expanded-functions dental assistant; the last office ran Curve, so "check in", "seat", the perio chart and switching users from the Scheduler are the words I reach for.
Tech comfort 4 of 5: I learn a screen in a morning, and I test at the speed of a busy morning with a patient already in the chair and a shared tablet between operatories.
I am in my third trimester, so I get tired and I keep sessions short; at the chair my hands are gloved and often wet, and any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is something I will hit by mistake, not a preference.

## What I did

**da-1, switch author by PIN on the shared tablet (6 taps, budget 6, NOT completed).** The chip at the top right read "PR PR" (Priya Raman, the front desk). I tapped it and got "Who is charting?" with "Enter the other person's PIN. Their session opens on this page; yours is revoked and local drafts are wiped after autosave." and a twelve-key pad. I measured every key at 72 x 56 px with 8 px between them (seq 2-3), which is more than the 56 px the sheet asked for, the cursor was already on the 1 key, and the Go key carried the lock glyph so I knew it was the one that commits. I tapped 3 3 3 3, four dots appeared, Go. The dialog closed and nothing else happened: the chip still said "PR PR", the aria-label still said "Who is charting: Priya Raman", no live line said "Now charting as Jo Ramirez", and the store still had Priya as the current user (seq 10-12). A `sessions` row for `sess-u-da-1` was written at seq 11, so the PIN matched. As a probe I tried Bree's PIN 1111 from a fresh store (events-probe-pin.json seq 3-13): that one worked, "Now charting as Bree Lawson", chip "BL BL · RDH", and the page moved to `#/hygienist/board`. So the pad switches people who have a persona and silently drops people who only have a PIN, which on this tablet is the assistants. Curve at least made me log out and pick myself; here I did the whole morning as Priya without knowing it. One more thing I read on the way in: the Board's readiness panel said "Shared tablet Op 2 still signed in as J.R. from yesterday" — J.R. is the person whose PIN I just entered, so the system knows about stale device sessions and still could not give me a fresh one.

**da-2, arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 taps, budget 2, done).** Arrive on the 9:00 card: "Marisol Vega arrived. Seat is the next step on the same card" and the cursor already sitting on Seat; Seat: "Seated in chair 1. The chair strip now shows BL" and the strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH" (seq 14-20). Both buttons 44 px tall, two taps, exactly as promised. Two things under the surface: both appointment events say "by Priya Raman" (seq 15, 19), because my PIN did not take, and two `firstRunState` rows (`frs-arrive`, `frs-seat`) were written at seq 16 and 20 even though Priya is not a temp; that came back to bite in da-5. One more I noticed as a tired person: on load the 9:00 Arrive button sits at y = 1062 px on a 768 px screen, under the "Before open" panel and the 8:00 cards, and even after "Hide" on the panel it lands at y = 770, two pixels under the fold; the first Arrive of the morning is a scroll before it is a tap. On privacy I tapped the top-bar Privacy button (seq 23-25): every card became "9:00 am · MV", "10:00 am · HH", no patient name anywhere in the canvas, and the chair strip did not change because it never had a patient in it. Good.

**da-3, open Perio with the glove pad and record 12 sites by tapping (14 taps, budget 15, done).** The route made me "BL BL · RDH" (Bree) without asking for a PIN, the second time today the tablet decided who I am. The "Glove pad" button was 121 x 44 px and its label promised "44 px keys for gloved fingers". I tapped it (seq 28) and nothing visibly changed, because the pad renders under the whole grid: top edge at y = 1135 px in a 768 px viewport, canvas 1306 px tall against 655 px visible. I had to scroll 579 px before a single key was under my thumb. Once there, the keys were honest: 1 to 9, Bleed, →, ⌫ all exactly 44 x 44 px with 8 px between them horizontally and vertically, "Next tooth" 252 x 56 px, no control on the page under 44 px, no horizontal overflow at 1024 px. Twelve taps on 3 (seq 31-53) took me from "Tooth 2 · site 1 · prior 3, Sites recorded: 0/168" to "Tooth 6 · site 1 · prior 4, Sites recorded: 12/168", and focus stayed on the 3 key after every tap, so my thumb never moved. Tab showed a 3 px solid light-blue ring with 2 px offset, and the ring also showed after a mouse tap, which is what I want with gloves. But the stamp that tells me where I am sits 800 px above the pad (stamp bottom at y = -251 when the pad is in view, upper-arch tooth numbers at y = -195), so while I tap I cannot see which tooth or site I am on. Bleed on the pad (seq 56) marked tooth 5 site 3 with a bleed class, but nothing was announced, the cell's aria-label stayed "Tooth 5 site 3", the flash line stayed empty, and the cell itself was off screen; the only proof it registered was in the DOM. Undo, by contrast, said "Undo: removed 3 at tooth 5 site 3" in two live regions and moved the stamp back to 11/168. And the "Bleed" label is 54 px of 17 px bold text on a 44 px key, hanging 5 px over each edge, so the visible gap to the 9 key reads as 3 px, not 8.

**da-4, chair strip shows initials and no patient data (0 taps, budget 0, done).** Back on the Board (seq 58) the strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH", "Chair 2 · HK DDS", "Chair 3 · HK DDS", 44 px tall, 8 px apart, aria-labels "Chair 1, author Bree Lawson, RDH. Show device author", and none of the eight seeded patient names appeared in the strip; the licence text measured 10.4:1 contrast on the dark theme. As a probe I tapped Chair 1 (seq 60): one line, "Bree Lawson, RDH is the author on the Op 1 device", focus stayed on the chair. Staff names only, which is right. What was wrong was where the Board put me: it landed at the canvas scroll position I had left the perio screen at, 579 px down, so the strip was at y = 17, the "Board · Main Street" heading was at y = -450 and the Op 1/2/3 column headers were clipped at the top of the screen (probe3.json). I had to scroll up to find out which screen I was on.

**da-5, day pass for a new hire, then the first-shift rail (8 taps by the contract formula, budget 8, NOT completed as scripted).** Roles opened at the same carried-over scroll, with "Add day pass" 48 px above the top of the screen (seq 64-66). "Add day pass" put the cursor in the name field, I typed Sam Lee, chose Front desk and Main Street (44 px segments), and "Issue day pass" wrote `dp-1` and the line "Day pass issued to Sam Lee · Front desk · expires 5:30 pm + 30 min grace · magic link sent to their phone; TOTP on their own device" (seq 82-84). "Sign in as this temp" took me to `#/temp/board` as "SL SL" with the rail "Your first shift: Arrive ✓ Seat ✓ Checkout Take payment Find a patient Hide" (seq 86-87). Sam Lee had not touched anything and two chips were already ticked, because Priya's Arrive and Seat from da-2 had retired them at seq 16 and 20; the rail is one bucket for the whole tablet. The chips also had no `data-retired` attribute at all, so the done ones looked exactly like the live ones apart from the check mark: same colour, same border, no strike-through. I tapped the Arrive chip anyway (seq 89): it pulsed the live Arrive on Hank Haddad's card, in view, and said "Tap Arrive on the first card", which is the right kind of help. Arrive on Hank (seq 91) wrote the appointment event "by Sam Lee" but no `firstRunState` row, so the rail never reacted to the temp's own first event. On taps: seven controls, and the formula counted the space in "Sam Lee" as an eighth (seq 71). I re-ran the task from a fresh store as a probe (events-probe-fresh.json seq 2-31): all five chips live, the Arrive chip pulsed `board.card.a-1042.arrive` with "Tap Arrive on the first card", and after Arrive the chip became "Arrive ✓" with `frs-arrive` written at seq 30 by Sam Lee. The rail works when it starts clean; it just does not start clean for the second person on the tablet. For the record, a-1043 is seeded "seated", so the sheet's note that it is not arrivable is correct.
