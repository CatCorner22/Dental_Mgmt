# One paint did the chart, the plan, and the note in eight taps; then my phone showed me a screen 866 pixels wide and every File button was off the right edge

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Sasha, 49, sixteen years an associate; the last office ran Eaglesoft, so my words are the chart, the Tx Plan tab, Auto Notes, service codes, and the walkout.
Tech comfort 4 of 5; I test at the speed of a busy morning and I do not read the Why fold unless a screen refuses me.
I work gloved and often wet at the chair, on my phone today: any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a defect I will hit, and anything I have to pan sideways to reach is a defect I will hit with a wet thumb.

## What I did

The driver ran three page loads: A for dn-1 and dn-2 (one continuous state), B for dn-3 and dn-4 (dn-3 has to happen before a note is filed, so it needed a fresh chart), C for dn-5 (the script says start with `?device=shared`). In `events.json` phase A keeps its seqs, phase B is offset by +1000 and phase C by +2000; the raw per-phase logs are `events-A.json`, `events-B.json`, `events-C.json` in the same folder. Seq ranges below use the combined numbering.

**dn-1, open Theo Brandt from Exams to sign (1 tap, budget 1, done).** The queue read "Exams to sign · 3 waiting · ordered by time in chair": Ines Okoro "Waiting 26 min · Note ready for signature", Theo Brandt "Waiting 14 min · Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L." with a chip "2 BWX, no interpretation", then Devon Price "Out of chair · 7:30 am visit · Checked out, note unfiled — filing releases the held payment". Longest in the chair first, and each row tells me why it is there in one line; that is more than Eaglesoft's OnSchedule ever told me. Open on Theo (seq 2) took me to `#/dentist/encounter/enc-9002` (seq 3). Was the hygienist tag the first thing on the screen? It was the first thing in the content: "Hygienist tags · Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L. · Needs disposition · Chart it · Dismiss". But on my phone it sat at y = 450 of an 860 px screen, because the top bar is 217 px tall (four rows of buttons: Riverbend / Main St / Search, Exams to sign / Chairs / Board, DH Dr. Kim / Privacy / Dark, Sign out) and stays stuck there, and under it comes "Theo Brandt · DOS 9/3/2026 · Dr. Kim · Chair 2 · Restorative · DOB 11/2/1990 · Rail · Back to Exams". The finding is on the screen without scrolling, just below the halfway line.

**dn-2, chart #30 DO from the tag, plan, note, File (7 taps here, 8 from the queue, budget 10, done).** Chart it (seq 5) selected tooth 30, put focus on it, and drew O and D as dashed surfaces: "Selected: #30 · D O", with the surface buttons reading "Occlusal, suggested by the tag, tap to confirm". I tapped D and O to confirm them (seq 9, 12); reading the code afterwards I believe the procedure button would have taken the dashed surfaces as they were, but nothing on the screen said so, so I spent the two taps. Composite 2-surf D2392 (seq 15) wrote three rows in one gesture: chartEvents ce-1, procedures pr-500, planItems pl-1 (seq 16-18), and the card said "One paint, one transaction · Chart event Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO · ce-1 · Dr. Kim · Procedure D2392 · $260.00 pending charge, released at File · pr-500 · Note scaffold Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO · Plan card · pl-1 · $260.00 Fee · $130.00 Your plan pays about · $130.00 You'd owe about". The tag flipped from "Needs disposition" to "Charted" by itself. In Eaglesoft that is the chart, then the Tx Plan tab, then an Auto Note, then the walkout. The first starter, "Caries confirmed, composite today" (seq 21), filled Assessment ("Caries #30 DO confirmed clinically and on BWX; asymptomatic; vitality normal.") and Plan ("Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given.") and left the cursor at the end of Assessment for me to edit. File (seq 25) did not file: the gate said "Nothing outstanding · Ruleset 2.25.2" and then "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt" with a "Confirm and file" button, a Why, then a second line "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for TB, DOB 11/1990, #30 DO" with "Switch author", then a full-width "Held — confirm the read-back". The same sentence twice, three buttons, on my own phone. Confirm and file (seq 29) wrote filedNotes nf-1, the $260.00 charge le-5000 with actorKind file_event, and claim c-100 to Cigna (seq 30-32); the screen read "Filed · ruleset 2.25.2 · byteaudit verified · By Dr. Hana Kim at 2026-09-03 08:40 · nf-1 · text and version frozen; corrections are addenda, never edits. Charges released: 1 · $260.00 #30 · Claim queued · c-100 to Cigna". Eight taps from the queue to a filed, charged, claimed note. No killer row appeared at all, because there was nothing to fix.

**dn-3, money in the note (3 gestures, 7 by the contract's count, budget 3, done).** Fresh load, same six setup taps (seq 1001-1023, not counted). I tapped the Plan field (seq 1025), pressed End and typed " Patient portion $130 discussed" (seq 1026-1057); the field is 824 px wide on a 420 px phone, so the caret ran off the right edge while I typed. I left the field with Shift+Tab (seq 1058-1059) and the strip came up at once, before I touched File: "Before File · 1 to fix · Money stays out of the note · Move to plan card · Why", and the File button had already become "Held" (refusal money_in_note, seq 1060). Six words, one button, no sermon; the Why, which I opened only afterwards, says "Clinical text is exported on records requests; quoted amounts live on the plan card with the date, never in the note." Pressing Held (seq 1063) re-ran the same audit and showed the same one row (seq 1064). Move to plan card (seq 1067) rewrote my sentence to "See plan card for the quoted amount.", left the rest of the plan alone, put a chip "Quoted to patient 9/3" on the plan card, and the gate went back to "Nothing outstanding" with a live File. That is the fix I would have made by hand, done for me and dated. The count: three of my taps were taps (field, Held, fix); the other four are the spaces in the sentence the script told me to type, which the contract's formula counts because the textarea carries a data-testid. On the phone the "Move to plan card" button started at x = 628, past the right edge; the driver panned to it, my thumb would have had to.

**dn-4, the plan card estimate and its rule trace (1 tap, budget 1, done).** After the fix the plan card read "Plan card · pl-1 · Quoted to patient 9/3 · $260.00 Fee · $130.00 Your plan pays about · $130.00 You'd owe about · Why". Why (seq 1069, a 49 x 44 px disclosure) opened "Cigna PPO: 50% after deductible (met) → patient est. $130.00. This is an estimate until the plan pays; it never joins the balance." Theo's carrier in the record is Cigna, so the trace names the right payer and the rule. Could I defend it chairside? The carrier, the percentage, and the deductible, yes. I would also want the remaining annual maximum and when eligibility was last pulled, because "50% after deductible" is exactly what a patient has heard before the max ran out. And on my phone the three tiles are 259 px each side by side, so the third one, "You'd owe about", the number I actually say out loud, sits at x = 552 and is off the screen until I pan.

**dn-5, the read-back line on a shared device (2 taps, budget 2, done).** Fresh load with `?device=shared`, same six setup taps (seq 2001-2023). The top bar chip now read "DH · DDS" instead of "DH Dr. Kim": on the shared tablet the bar names me by two initials. File (seq 2025) stopped at the read-back (refusal readback, seq 2026): "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt", eight words, with "Confirm and file" (183 x 44), a Why, the second line "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for TB, DOB 11/1990, #30 DO" with "Switch author" (154 x 44), and the 824 px "Held — confirm the read-back". Focus landed on Confirm and file. Confirm (seq 2029) filed nf-1 as Dr. Hana Kim and released the charge and claim (seq 2030-2032). Would it catch a stale author on a passed tablet? Yes, if the author reads it: the verb names the person and the patient, and Switch author is right there. My worry is that the same gate fired on my own phone in dn-2, where there is no stale author to catch, so by the tenth note of the morning it is a reflex tap, and a reflex tap does not read.

**My gloved-hand checks.** Across six screen states (Exams, encounter landing, after Chart it, the read-back gate on phone, the money strip, the read-back gate on shared) with 13 to 68 visible controls each, no button, field, or Why disclosure measured under 44 x 44 px by getBoundingClientRect: teeth 44 x 44 with 8.0 px between them, surfaces 44 x 44 at 8.0 px, Chart it 99.9 x 44 and Dismiss 101.2 x 44 at 8.0 px, Today / Planned / Existing at 8.00 px (a first rounded pass said 7; the fractional edges are 111.73 and 119.73). The only pairs under 8 px were page controls passing under the sticky top bar, which is a different layer. What failed is width. `.enc-layout` resolves to an 850 px column on both a 420 px and a 390 px phone (the 16-tooth arch is 824 px and the grid item will not shrink below it), so the `.canvas` scrolls horizontally, scrollWidth 866 against a 420 px viewport: the Assessment and Plan fields are 824 px wide (x 27 to 851), File is 824 px wide, Held is 824 px wide, Confirm and file sits at x 628 to 811, Switch author at x 670 to 824, Move to plan card at x 628, Undo last paint 819 px wide, the third plan tile at x 552, and teeth 8 to 16 and 17 to 25, the Crown and Periodic exam procedure buttons, and the second and third starters are all past the right edge. The arch's own horizontal scroller never engages because it is never narrower than its content. The top bar is 217 px tall and sticky, a quarter of the 860 px screen, on every one of those states.

**Interview answers.** Would I sign hygiene-drafted notes here rather than bypass to a template? Yes: the hygienist's finding becomes my chart event only when I tap Chart it, the starter fills Assessment and Plan in my voice and leaves the cursor for me, and the scaffold line comes from my paint, not from her. Which killer would I resent, which would I thank? I thank the money one; it fixed my sentence and dated the quote. I would resent the read-back on my own phone. What would make me trust the estimate the front desk quotes? The trace as it stands names Cigna, 50%, and the deductible; add remaining annual maximum and the eligibility pull time and I will say the number to the patient myself.
