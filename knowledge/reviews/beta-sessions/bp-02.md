# Checkout is faster than Dentrix ever was, but the Board says "Filed later" where I would say "checked out", and one row in the queue has no Checkout button at all

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Tomás Reyes, she/her, 30, seven years on the front desk and the schedule; the last office ran Dentrix.
I am at a desk monitor, light theme, no access adjustments, tech comfort about a 3 of 5.
I test at the speed of a busy morning: a patient at the window, a phone ringing, and no patience for a screen that makes me hunt.

## What I did (one short paragraph per task: what happened, taps vs budget, what confused me, quoting the screen)

**fo-1 Arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 taps, budget 2, completed).** The 9:00 card in Op 1 read "Confirmed · Hygiene · Verify · Re-verify · 1 alert" with one Arrive button. I clicked Arrive; the card flipped to "Arrived" and focus landed on Seat, which is exactly where my hand wanted to go. I clicked Seat and the chair strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH", the card "In chart". What confused me (seq 5-9): after Arrive and Seat the card still said "Verify" with a Re-verify button, even though Details said "re-run started on arrival". If the re-run started, I expect the chip to say so or to turn green. The Re-verify control itself is fine: one chip, one button, on one line.

**fo-2 Check out Ines Okoro, $44 by card (4 taps, budget 4, completed with a caveat).** Checkout opened from the card in one click. "Collect" was already pressed and the Amount box already said 44.00; the estimate column was shaded and labelled "Patient portion (estimate)" with "$44.00 est." in the footer, clearly apart from the fee column. I clicked Card, clicked into the card box, typed the number, clicked Post. "Posted in one transaction · Payment $44.00 by card · held as credit until the note is filed". Two things confused me. First (seq 12-14), the three numbers at the top said "$0.00 Patient due" right above a table telling me to collect $44; in Dentrix the patient portion and the balance were the same number, and I had to read the footnote "Estimate is separate from the balance above" to trust it. Second (seq 40-42), back on the Board the card did not read Done; it read "Filed later · Paid at the window · charges and claim release when Dr. Kim files the note". The money posted correctly, so I am calling the task done, but the word I was told to look for was "checked out" and it is not on the card. I also noticed afterwards that focus had jumped into the card box by itself when I picked Card, so the click into the field was a wasted tap: three would have done it.

**fo-3 Check out Ruth Adler, $0 (2 taps, budget 2, completed).** Checkout, Post. "Nothing due today" was already pressed with the line "Patient portion is $0. Post still writes the collection decision (reason zero_due) so the day's 'Not collected at window' line stays honest." No refusal, no dead end, and the two charges posted because Ruth's note was filed. This is the one I would show a temp first.

**fo-4 Samir Haddad pays in full, off insurance (5 taps, budget 5, completed).** The row toggles read "Paid in full — don't send to insurance" and were already showing because the Amount was prefilled at 168.00, which covers both fees; when I typed 50 in Amount they disappeared, and came back at 168. That is plain language with no policy paragraph, which is what I wanted. Cash, two toggles, Post: "Self-pay restriction on pr-421: claim assembly refuses it". What confused me (seq 54-57): the task and the Board Details both said $180 ("Balance $180.00 · patient portion, estimate separate") but the checkout totals said "$168.00 est." I would have quoted Samir $180 from the Board and then collected $168 at the window, and he would have asked why.

**fo-5 The patient whose note is not filed (3 taps, budget 3, NOT completed).** The queue row read "7:30 am · Devon Price · Filed later · Note ◆ Open · BL · Claim ▬ Held · Note not filed — Bree L. · Ping chair". I clicked Ping chair and got a stamp "Pinged chair 1 · 8:40 am · one-to-one, not broadcast"; the message write is there. I did not feel like the note cop: the row names Bree and sends the nudge to her chair, not to me. Then the script broke down. I clicked Ping again to see the limit and got a box reading "Already pinged this chair in the last 15 minutes" with no button in it (seq 84-85). And the row has no Checkout button: it says "Paid at the window · in the Filed-later lane until Bree L. files", because this patient was already checked out before I sat down (seq 85-86). So `board.queue.row.a-1050.checkout` does not exist and the scripted path cannot run. I tried the checkout page by its address as a probe (seq 86-89): it showed "Already checked out", "No completed procedures on this encounter", $0 due, and it still let me Post a second collection decision (cd-4) on a visit that had already been posted. No allocation intent was written because there was nothing due. I also noticed after the first Ping that keyboard focus went nowhere at all (seq 81-83): the screen tried to move it to a Checkout button the row does not have.

**fo-6 Find Lena Fischer with three letters (5 taps, budget 3, completed; 3 taps on the clean path).** Search, typed "fis", three rows: "Lena Fischer · DOB 7/8/1969 · …0126", Beth, Cole. Clicked Lena; the dialog said "Confirm date of birth · Lena Fischer · …0126 · MRN-306" and put me in the box. The task asked me to check the wrong date first, so I typed 07/08/1968 and Enter: "Date of birth does not match" with one "Try again" button and Open chart switched to a locked "Held". That is the right shape. Try again cleared the box; the right date and Enter opened the Rail: "Lena Fischer · DOB 7/8/1969 · phone …0126". My two extra taps were the deliberate wrong-DOB detour, so the clean path is exactly three. What bothered me (seq 95-99): the result row had just shown me "DOB 7/8/1969" and then the next screen asked me to confirm the date of birth. I could answer the second identifier without the patient saying a word.

## Interview focus (in my words)

**Would you choose this Board over your last system's schedule?** Yes: arrive and seat are one click each on the card and I never opened an appointment window to do either, which is the whole morning in Dentrix.

**Which gate felt like a dead end?** The second Ping. "Already pinged this chair in the last 15 minutes" and nothing to press; every other gate handed me one button. The $0 checkout, which I expected to be the dead end, was the smoothest thing I did today.

**What would a temp need on day one that is missing?** The words. "Filed later", "Pre-flight", "allocation intent", "Held" on a claim chip: none of these are Dentrix words, and nothing on the Board explains that "Filed later" means the patient is paid and gone. One line of plain language on that card, and a Checkout button that does not vanish from the queue row, or at least a note saying why it is gone.
