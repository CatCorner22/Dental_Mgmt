I am Kenji, 60, twenty-six years assisting and running sterilization; the last office was on Dentrix, so I think in Appointment Book, Family File, Perio Chart, and Office Journal, and I read English words literally because Vietnamese is my first language.
Today I am on the shared operatory tablet, light theme, 1024 by 768, and I work gloved with wet hands, so I measured every control I touched: anything under 44 px, or closer than 8 px to its neighbour, I would hit wrong.
