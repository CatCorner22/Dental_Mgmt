# The keys chart a mouth faster than Dentrix ever let me; the screen stops following me at tooth 31, and the glove pad is missing two keys I use

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Elijah, 33, four years a hygienist, most of my days with kids and teenagers; the last office ran Dentrix, so I think in its words: perio chart, auto-advance, bleeding on the site, recall.
Tech comfort 4 of 5; no access adjustments. I test at the speed of a busy morning, and I judge a screen by whether I can run it without taking my eyes off the mouth.
Shared tablet in the operatory, light theme, motion on auto. I work gloved and often wet, so any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a target I will hit by mistake.

## What I did

Event numbers are the `gseq` field in events.json. `reset()` wipes `window.__events`, and I had to reset twice (hy-3 and hy-6 cannot run on a saved exam, explained below) plus once for probes, so I kept one global count: A = 1-200, B = 201-451, C = 452-479, probes D = 480-895. Taps follow the contract rule with one correction: a keyboard Enter on a button logs a `key` and a synthesized `click` for one press, which I count once (the literal count is `raw_taps_contract` in results.json), and the two spaces I typed inside the tag text box are characters, not taps. Author chip on the shared tablet read "BL BL · RDH" (Bree Lawson, RDH); nothing asked me for a PIN, because the hygienist persona already carries the licence.

**hy-1, open Perio from my first card (1 tap, budget 1, done).** My first card was not Marisol. Seat order put 7:30 Devon Price, "Filed later", with a stop chip "Minor: guardian Alicia Price", ahead of 9:00 Marisol Vega (gseq 1-3). I like that the guardian is on the card for a minor; I do not like a finished chair sitting in front of the live one. Marisol's card answered the three questions without a tap: what changed, "Med hx changed · New anticoagulant (apixaban), intake 2 days ago"; what is due, "Perio 14 mo ago" and "BWX due, practice rule: 12 mo"; what helped, "stop signal, sunglasses". Enter on Perio (gseq 2-4) opened `#/hygienist/perio/enc-9001` at "Tooth 2 · site 1 · prior 3" because 1 is missing, with "Prior exam 7/1/2025 ghosted" and 168 grey prior depths in the cell corners (10 px, rgb(86, 97, 122)). Keyboard focus was left on the body, not on a cell (gseq 4); the grammar still worked because it listens on the document. The line under the title is 37 words and half of it is a key legend I stop reading after the first chart.

**hy-2, full mouth, keyboard only, one pass (1 tap, budget 1; 186 keystrokes, budget 200; done).** 168 depths with 17 Spaces for bleeding (gseq 6-190). I checked the cursor at 13 points against the path and it never jumped. The orientation line ("Tooth 15 · site 3 · prior 4 · Sites recorded: 41/168") kept me on track. Then the problem: when the cursor crossed into the lower arch at site 85 (gseq 99), the highlighted cell sat at y = 820 in a 768 px viewport and nothing scrolled; at site 127 it was at y = 976. From tooth 31 to tooth 17, half the mouth, I charted with the active cell and its ghosted prior off-screen, and the canvas never moved (scrollTop 0, focus on body). The text line saved me; the grid did not. Save was one Enter (gseq 191-193), no licence asked; the note came back "Perio: 168 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 17 sites." with "4-month perio maintenance with BWX", the stamp said "Saved to tooth #31 (draft, this session)", and focus went to "Tag for dentist". That is Dentrix auto-advance without touching the mouse. The saved card is 122 words before the buttons.

**hy-3, undo a mistyped depth and skip a site without recording zero (4 taps, budget 0; done only after a fresh start).** As written, straight after hy-2, nothing can happen: "Exam saved · grid is read-only", the Save button is a chip, and 9, Backspace, 3, Right landed on the focused Tag button and did nothing (gseq 197-200). No Held, no refusal, no "amend". So I reset and opened the chart again (setup gseq 202-205). Fresh: 9 put a 9 in tooth 2 site 1; Backspace put the cell back to "—" with its ghost 3 and flashed "Undo: removed 9 at tooth 2 site 1" in 15 px, gone after 2.0 s (gseq 206-207). A skipped cell and a never-entered cell both read "—"; the pale blue fill is the only difference. I finished the other 166 sites (gseq 210-375) and pressed Save: "Name why 1 sites were not probed" with one 176 x 44 button "Choose a reason", Save switched to a locked "Held" (gseq 376-380). The underscore code is in Marisol's hygiene note; the card two lines below says it properly, "Patient could not tolerate".

A PSR that writes zero sites probed is a note I would have to fix by hand. Three presses on the sheet against a budget of two.

**Probes (gseq 480-895).** The glove pad: 13 keys at 44 x 44 with 8 px between them and "Next tooth" 252 x 56 (gseq 485-488). A pad tap recorded a depth and the pad stayed open; Bleed, skip, undo and Next tooth all worked from the pad (gseq 856-870); the first keyboard key closed it (gseq 871). With focus parked on Save, 3 still charted and the screen re-focused Save; the Space that should have marked bleeding activated Save, "Name why 164 sites were not probed", Held (gseq 888-895). With a complete chart that Space saves the exam. Tab from the pad toggle took 171 stops to reach the first pad key (gseq 490-831), through every grid cell; the ring there is 3 px solid rgb(43, 108, 184) with a 2 px offset, same as on the Chairs button, the grid cell and the sextant box. On every screen: no control under 44 x 44, no two live controls closer than 8 px, no sideways scroll at 1024 (document 1024/1024, grid wrap 992/992). The sticky top bar does overlay live cells to within 3.7 px once the canvas scrolls ("Main St" over tooth 4 sites 5 and 6).
