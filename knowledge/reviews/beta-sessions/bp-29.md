# The glove pad I can hit with a wet glove; the tablet I cannot get to say my name

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Linh, 53, nineteen years assisting, new to this office this month; the last place ran Denticon, so "op", "the chart", and "who's signed in on the op computer" are my words.
No access adjustments; I test at the speed of a busy morning on the operatory tablet at 1024 px, light theme, reduced motion.
I work gloved, often with wet hands: any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a defect I will hit, so I measured every one I touched.

## What I did

**da-1, switch author by PIN on the shared tablet (6 taps, budget 6, NOT completed).** The top-bar chip read "PR PR" (Priya Raman, the front desk). I tapped it and a "Who is charting?" dialog opened with the cursor on key 1 and a hint: "Enter the other person's PIN. Their session opens on this page; yours is revoked and local drafts are wiped after autosave." The keys measured 72 × 56 px with 8.0 px gaps, digits 20 px bold, easy for a gloved thumb; the dots filled to "••••" as I tapped 3-3-3-3 (seq 5-8). I tapped Go (seq 10). A `sessions` row was written (`sess-u-da-1`, seq 11), the dialog closed, my focus went back to the chip, and the chip still said "PR PR" with the label "Who is charting: Priya Raman" (seq 10-12, da-1.png). No "Now charting as Jo Ramirez", nothing announced, `currentUser()` still Priya, persona still frontdesk. In a fresh-page probe I typed Bree's 1111 the same way and the chip flipped to "BL BL · RDH" with "Now charting as Bree Lawson"; a hygienist's PIN switches everything and an RDA's PIN is swallowed. In Denticon that is exactly how the hygienist's name ends up on my chart, and it is the first thing I was told to check here. Two smaller things: the chip prints the initials twice ("PR PR", "BL BL · RDH", seq 1-2), and the hint's "local drafts are wiped" is a frightening sentence for a new hire to read before her first PIN.

**da-2, arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 taps, budget 2, done).** The 9:00 card's Arrive button sat at 1062 px down a 768 px screen, under the "Before open" panel and the chair strip, so I scrolled to it (seq 13-14). Arrive: "Marisol Vega arrived. Seat is the next step on the same card." and the cursor was already on Seat. Seat: "Seated in chair 1. The chair strip now shows BL." The card flipped to "In chart", the strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH", status seated, two `appointmentEvents` writes (seq 15, 19). All four card controls 44 px tall with 8.0 px gaps, no horizontal overflow, no control under 44 px anywhere on the Board. What moved under my hand: after Seat my focus jumped from the card to the chair strip at the top of the page (seq 18-21). And I noticed, without understanding it yet, two `firstRunState` writes riding along with my arrive and seat (seq 16, 20); they mattered in da-5. Privacy probe on a fresh page with `?privacy=1`: the card read "9:00 am · MV", the strip "Chair 1 · —", and none of the patient names appeared anywhere on the canvas (probe-privacy.png).

**da-3, open Perio with the glove pad and record 12 sites (13 taps, budget 15, done).** The route put me in Perio as "BL BL · RDH", Bree, because the URL carries the hygienist persona; after da-1 that means whatever I chart here is hers. The heading read "Perio · Marisol Vega · Chair 1 · Prior exam 7/1/2025 ghosted · 28 teeth (x = missing: 1, 16, 17, 32)". I tapped "Glove pad" (seq 24): the button changed to "Hide glove pad" and the canvas scroll reset from 177 to 0, but the pad had been placed at 1135 px, below the whole grid, in a 655 px canvas (da-3-padopen.png). I scrolled it under my thumb; then the "Tooth 2 · site 1 · prior 3 · Sites recorded: 0/168" line was 367 px above the viewport and cell t2-s1 was 238 px above it (seq 24-27, da-3-padinview.png). The pad itself is right: every key 44 × 44 px, gaps exactly 8.0 px, Next tooth 252 × 56 px, grid cells 44 × 44 with 8 px gaps, focus ring 3 px solid blue at 2 px offset when reached by keyboard. Twelve taps on 3 (seq 27-49) walked Tooth 2 through Tooth 5 on the facial sites, the line read "Tooth 6 · site 1 · prior 4 · Sites recorded: 12/168", 12 cells showed 3, and my focus stayed on key 3 after every rerender, so I never mis-tapped a neighbour. Two things about the pad: the "Bleed" label is 54 px of text in a 44 px key, so it spills 5 px past each edge and its left edge sits 3 px from key 9, which reads to a gloved eye as one wide double key (measured in a probe); and when I tapped Bleed in a probe the only confirmation was the cell's description changing to "3 mm, bleeding" 238 px above the viewport, with the flash line empty.

**da-4, chair strip shows initials and no patient data (0 taps, budget 0, done).** Chairs read "Chair 1 · BL RDH", "Chair 2 · HK DDS", "Chair 3 · HK DDS"; labels "Chair 1, author Bree Lawson, RDH. Show device author"; each 44 px tall with 8.0 px gaps; the only digits in the strip were the chair numbers; no patient names. Good. The trouble is where I landed: the Board opened from Perio with the canvas already scrolled 651 px, the heading "Board · Main Street · Thursday 9/3" 522 px above the viewport and the chair strip itself 55 px above it (seq 51, da-4.png). The thing I was asked to read was off the top of the screen when I arrived.

**da-5, day pass for Sam Lee, then the first-shift rail (10 taps by the contract formula, budget 8, NOT completed).** Roles opened from the nav (seq 53-54); "Add day pass" put the cursor in Name (seq 56-57); I tapped the field anyway, as I would with gloves, and typed Sam Lee (seq 58-65; the space in the name is counted as a tap by the seq formula, so 9 real taps). Front desk, Main Street; the preview said "Will grant: Schedule · Post payments until 5:30 pm + 30 min grace · Nonclinical role · no licence needed · No SoD conflicts". I noticed the role list offers "RDA (assistant)" and "DA (assistant)", which is my job. "Issue day pass" wrote dp-1 (seq 73-75) and the card read "Day pass issued to Sam Lee · Front desk · expires 5:30 pm + 30 min grace · magic link sent to their phone". "Sign in as this temp" landed on #/temp/board as "SL SL" (seq 77-78). The rail read "Your first shift: Arrive ✓ Seat ✓ Checkout Take payment Find a patient" before Sam had done anything: the chips were retired by Priya's arrive and seat in da-2 (railState byEvent 15 and 19; seq 16, 20). I tapped "Arrive ✓" to see where it would point (seq 80): the pulse class landed on Hank Haddad's Arrive with animation-duration 0s under reduced motion, and "Tap Arrive on the first card" went into a 1 px sr-only div, so nothing visible pointed anywhere (da-5-pulse.png). a-1043 had no Arrive, as the script warned, so I arrived a-1061 (seq 82-83): the chip stayed "Arrive ✓" and no `firstRunState` was written. In a fresh-store probe the mechanism works: as the temp, Arrive flipped "Arrive" to "Arrive ✓" and wrote `frs-arrive`. So the rail retires on the practice's events, not the temp's own, which is the opposite of what a first-shift rail is for.
