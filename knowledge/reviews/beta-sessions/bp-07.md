# Three lines to read back instead of forty-one, but the write-off gate named the wrong second approver

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Fatima, 35, ten years running the back office; the last ten were on Open Dental, so "Account", "Audit Trail", "walkout" and "claim procedures" are the words in my head.
I would call my tech comfort 2 of 5: I learn a screen by posting on it for a week, and I do not read policy prose while a payer is on hold.
My back does not tolerate long mouse sessions, so I stand at the desk and work from the keyboard in short bursts; every extra click and every deep menu costs me, and I count them.

## What I did

**bo-1, post the Delta ERA and read back the differing lines (4 taps, budget 4, done).** The desk opened on the ERA tab with one sentence I could trust: "Delta 835 · 41 lines · 37 posted before you sat down · EFT $4,812.33 · TRN 20260903-90112 · matched to bank line". I put focus on Post matched and pressed Enter; the screen said "Read back: 3 lines differ from the claim" and gave me exactly three rows, not forty-one (seq 2-4). Each row was one line I could read in a breath: "expected $590.00, ERA says $540.00 · CARC 45 (contract underpayment)", and the Confirm button's own label spelled out what it would do: "post $540.00 and a contractual write-off of $50.00". The cursor was already on the first Confirm, and after each decision it moved to the next line by itself: Confirm 14 ("Confirmed line 14. 2 left."), Hold 22, Dispute 31 ("Disputed line 31. Batch complete.") (seq 5-19). The batch line then read "38 posted, 1 held, 1 disputed, 1 denial moved to Denials". In Open Dental I would have scrolled the whole 835 in the Insurance Payment window looking for the three odd lines. Nothing confused me here; the only word I had to think about was "Downcoded", and the row told me "downcoded to D2391" right after it.

**bo-2, appeal denial c-88 from the record (3 taps, budget 2, done but over budget).** Denials tab, then Appeal on Ines Aziz (seq 20-27). The row already said in plain words "Delta says the claim is missing information: the perio chart was not attached" and "Next: Appeal with the perio chart and the SRP narrative from the note"; the "Why this code" line underneath decoded CARC 16 / RARC N4 for anyone who wants it, and I did not need it. The packet opened under the row with four "Clear" slots (perio chart pe-1, narrative, radiograph, letter) and a line "What the patient reads: Delta asked for your gum chart; we are sending it. You owe nothing while they review." Send was one press (seq 28-32); the tab then read "No open denials · Appeal for c-88 sent · disclosure recorded". Three presses against a budget of two, because the tab itself is a tap. The page header advertises "A appeal", and in a fresh probe the A key opened the packet with no tap at all and Send made it one tap total (events-probe.json seq 2-9), so the budget is reachable, just not by the path the script wrote down.

**bo-3, try to bill the patient for the denied claim (0 taps, budget 1, NOT completed).** The step was `money.denial.c-88.bill`, but after bo-2 the Denials tab reads "No open denials" and the count is "Denials 0"; the row with Bill patient on it was gone because the claim is now "appealed" (seq 32). There was nothing to press, so I could not raise the denial_suppression gate in this session as written. On a fresh store (events-probe.json seq 2-13) the gate itself is right: Bill patient answers "Appeal first — denial with no appeal" with one 141 by 44 px "Build appeal" button, nothing posts, and Build appeal opens the packet, so the refusal is a next step and not a wall. But run in the order the sheet gives, task three cannot happen after task two.

**bo-4, request a $410 courtesy write-off on Lena Fischer and see it held (3 taps plus the W key, budget 4, NOT completed on one criterion).** The first step, `money.writeoff.p-306`, was not on the screen: the Open balances card only lives on the ERA and Aging tabs and bo-2 had left me on Denials (seq 32-33). The header says "W write-off", so I pressed W and the card opened with $410.00 already in the amount field and the cursor in it (seq 33-34). Courtesy, Post (seq 36-41). The gate came up as one line with one button: "Needs a second approver — Dr. Reagan or Dr. Kim" · "Request approval", and Post had already turned into an outlined, locked "Held" that still works (seq 42-44). That is what I wanted from a hold. The problem is the names. The contract and the seed say the second approver is Dana or Dr. Reagan; the small print under the same button says "Dana or Dr. Reagan will see it on their phone", the announcement said "Dana or Dr. Reagan", and the gate line, the one line I am supposed to read, says "Dr. Reagan or Dr. Kim" (seq 43-47). Dr. Kim is a dentist. If I am the office manager and my own name is missing from the gate while it is in the footnote, I stop trusting the gate. The approvals row was written pending ("Write-off $410.00 on Lena Fischer (courtesy) requested by Sam Dawson at 08:40") and shows on the Approvals tab, nothing hit the ledger, and pressing Held once more just repeated who it is waiting on (seq 48-49). Everything held except the sentence.

**bo-5, find "Office Journal" and "walkout statement" through the palette (2 taps, budget 4, done).** Ctrl+K put me in the box, "office journal" returned one row, "Audit log (sentences) · office journal — called that in Dentrix", Enter took me to Practice risk (seq 50-69); Ctrl+K again, "walkout" gave "Statement · walkout statement — called that in Dentrix", Enter took me to Money Desk (seq 70-81). Both words worked first try and both rows named the system the word came from, so the task passed. Two things for a standing keyboard user: the synonym line credits Dentrix, and I am coming from Open Dental, whose words ("Audit Trail", "Account") are not in the list; and after each jump my focus was on the page body, so the next Tab landed on the skip link and it took 32 Tabs from there to reach the Statements tab (probe after seq 81). I did the next task by putting focus on the tab directly; a person cannot do that.

**bo-6, send a held statement with its reason visible (3 taps, budget 3, done).** Statements tab: two rows, each with its hold reason on the row, "held: window deferral · deferred at the window 9/2 so insurance could settle first" (seq 83-85). Preview on Hana Lindqvist opened under the row with the patient's sentence first: "Your share is $84.00 after insurance. We held this statement so your plan could settle first; nothing here is an estimate." (seq 87-89). The two buttons look like two different things: Send is the filled light button with the bar glyph, Preview is the outlined teal one and stayed pressed while the preview was open. Send: "Statement sent · disclosure recorded", the row left the list, sd-1 shows sent with a mail disclosure row (seq 91-94). That is the walkout I know, with the reason for the delay written where the patient's name is.

**Interview probes.** Ines Okoro's ledger (`#/biller/ledger/p-303`, seq 95 and results-probe2.json): Explain gave me the one sentence I would say to her, "SRP, 4+ teeth per quadrant on 7/14/2026: charge $217.00; Delta Dental paid $156.00 on 8/2; MetLife paid $61.00 on 8/20; paid in full", and Show patient turned it into "Nothing left to pay" with the poster names gone. I note that the front-office script for the same patient collects $44 at the window today; from this ledger she owes nothing, so someone at the window is saying a number I cannot see. Measurements at 1280 px, dark theme: every visible control on the ERA, Denials and Statements tabs (17 to 19 per tab) measured at least 44 px each way, no two adjacent controls closer than 8 px, no horizontal overflow (scrollWidth 1280 = clientWidth), focus ring 3 px solid rgb(143,193,245) with a 2 px surface halo, muted text rgb(198,207,221) on rgb(23,32,51).
