# The extraction charts in six taps and the queue reads in words, but the shared tablet took Dana's PIN, said nothing, and kept filing as Dr. Okafor

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Priya, 39, final-year oral surgery resident, nine years in the role; I have never run a practice-management system, so I read every screen literally and I do not know the product's words.
I have deuteranopia, so I switched grayscale on and ranked the queue I work from by shape and word; if two states collapse into one shape, that is a defect to me.
I work gloved with wet hands at a shared operatory tablet, 1024 by 768, light theme; I tap with a finger, and any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is one I will hit by mistake.

## What I did

**os-1, open the referred-in consult for Aiko Tanaka (1 tap, budget 1, done).** Exams to sign listed four rows "ordered by time in chair"; mine was last, "Aiko Tanaka · Chair 3 · Dr. Okafor · Arrives 10:00 am · Referred by Dr. Serrano: #17 extraction, sedation requested; records forwarded · Open". So the referral loop was on the row before I opened anything. Open (seq 2) took me to the encounter, which repeated it in a card "▬ Referral Referred by Dr. Serrano: #17 extraction, sedation requested; records forwarded" under the head "Aiko Tanaka · DOS 9/3/2026 · Dr. Okafor · Chair 3 · Surgery · DOB 12/5/1982". The first thing I did not understand was the empty gate at the bottom: "The killer strip appears after you leave a field: at most three rows, each with one fix." I do not know what a killer strip is, and "DOS" I had to work out from the date. Every control on both screens measured 44 px or more and nothing sat closer than 8 px (12 and 62 controls audited). The top bar wrapped to two rows at my width, with Dark and Sign out on the second line, and it stays on screen when I scroll.

**os-2, chart a surgical extraction #17 with IV sedation and file the sedation note (6 taps, budget 8, done).** Tooth 17 (seq 5) went blue-edged with "Selected: #17". The procedure strip put my two first: "Surgical extraction D7210", "IV sedation D9243", then the composite, crown, and exam. Extraction (seq 8) announced "Charted Extraction, surgical #17. Pending charge $360.00. One transaction: chart, procedure, plan, note scaffold." and sedation (seq 14) did the same for $320.00. Then the screen misled me: the extraction's own transaction card now read "Note scaffold IV sedation, each 15 min #17", and the note panel said "Procedure (from the chart): IV sedation, each 15 min #17" with the extraction gone (seq 15-18). The task sheet said the sedation starter was button 3; button 3 said "Fractured cusp", so I pressed the one that said "Sedation: ASA II, IV midazolam, monitored per protocol" (starter 0, seq 20), and it filled Assessment "Referred for surgical extraction #17; ASA II; airway and medical history reviewed; sedation consented." and Plan "Surgical extraction #17 under IV sedation (midazolam), monitored per protocol; postoperative instructions given to patient and escort." That read as a record, not a template; I would edit it but I would not be ashamed of it. File (seq 24) turned into the read-back, a gray-railed line "Filing as Dr. Chidi Okafor for Aiko Tanaka" with a filled "Confirm and file", and under it "Filing as Dr. Chidi Okafor for AT, DOB 12/1982, #17", a "Switch author" button, and a full-width outlined "Held — confirm the read-back" with a lock. Three buttons for one gate; two of them say confirm. Confirm and file (seq 28) wrote the note, two ledger rows and claim c-100, and the card read "Filed · ruleset 2.25.2 · byteaudit verified · Charges released: 2 · $360.00 #17, $320.00 #17 · Claim queued · c-100 to Cigna". The frozen text under it has three lines and the third is only "IV sedation, each 15 min #17"; the extraction survives only because my Plan sentence names it. "byteaudit" means nothing to me.

**os-3, switch author by PIN on the shared tablet (6 taps, budget 6, NOT completed).** The chip at the top right read "DC DC · DDS, OMS", the initials printed twice. Tapping it (seq 34) opened "Who is charting? Enter the other person's PIN. Their session opens on this page; yours is revoked and local drafts are wiped after autosave." The pad is twelve keys of 72 by 56 px with 8 px between them, Go filled dark, Cancel a plain outline underneath; gloved, I could not miss. Four presses of 4 (seq 37-40) drew four dots; Go (seq 42) wrote sessions:sess-u-om-1 (seq 43) and the dialog closed. Then nothing. The chip still said "DC DC · DDS, OMS", the aria-label still said Dr. Chidi Okafor, the live region still held the old "Filed. Ruleset 2.25.2..." sentence, no "Now charting as" anywhere, and the store's current user was still Dr. Okafor. The system recorded a switch and did not perform one, and did not tell me. Later I did the same from the read-back's own Switch author (seq 73-83): a second sessions write for Dana, and the line above it still said "Filing as Dr. Chidi Okafor for Ines Okoro". Bree's PIN (seq 104-112) did work: chip "BL BL · RDH", "Now charting as Bree Lawson", same encounter; so it is Dana's, the office manager's, that silently goes nowhere. A wrong PIN (seq 117-123) at least said "No match for that PIN. Six digits at most; three misses lock this device for 5 minutes."

**os-4, check that the Board card reads Surgery by shape and word (1 tap, budget 1, done).** Board (seq 46) in grayscale. My card was the last in Op 3, below the fold at 1023 px on a 768 px screen, "10:00 am · Aiko Tanaka ● Note filed ■ Surgery ● Eligible ▲ 1 alert ▬ Referred in", with the darkest rail on the page (luminance 61 against 85 for Restorative and 116 for Hygiene). The square and the word carry it; in colour the red rail is one I would confuse with Hygiene's green, but I never had to. What did collapse was the pair beside mine: Hygiene is "● Hygiene" and Restorative is "● Restorative", the same round glyph, with ink luminance 74 against 56 and near-white fills; and the Hygiene, Exam and Emergency rails are 116, 114 and 114, the same gray. In grayscale I ranked Surgery, then Restorative by its slightly darker rail, then everything else as one class, and the word did the rest. Ninety-nine controls, none under 44 px, none closer than 8 px.

**os-5, try to file with an empty assessment (2 taps, budget 2, done).** Back on Exams to sign the queue had three rows; mine was gone, "Rows leave only by filing" as the empty state promises. I opened Ines Okoro (seq 50), Dr. Kim's exam, and pressed File under a blank note (seq 53). One orange-railed row, "Assessment is empty", one button "Add assessment" (174 by 44 px), a "Why" underneath, the chip "▲ 1 to fix", and File turned into an outlined "Held". The live region said "Assessment is empty. Add assessment". Nothing filed. Focus stayed on Held rather than on the fix; when I pressed Add assessment (seq 57) the cursor landed in the Assessment field (seq 58), which is what I wanted.

**My checks (Priya-specific).** Privacy on (seq 61) and File (seq 68): the read-back line still said "Filing as Dr. Chidi Okafor for Ines Okoro" and the page title still said "Ines Okoro" while the small line under them said "IO, DOB 06/1985"; on operatory glass the biggest text on the gate is the one the mode was meant to hide. Focus ring (seq 87-98): "solid 3px rgb(43, 108, 184)" with a 2 px white halo on File and on tooth 17. Exams to sign in grayscale: the wait column is plain text ("Waiting 26 min", "Waiting 14 min", "Out of chair", "Arrives 10:00 am") in one ink, no colour to lose; the only chip is "◆ 2 BWX, no interpretation". Size audits on seven screens (12, 62, 68, 10, 23, 99, 64 controls): no control under 44 by 44 px, no two controls on the same layer under 8 px apart, no horizontal overflow at 1024.
