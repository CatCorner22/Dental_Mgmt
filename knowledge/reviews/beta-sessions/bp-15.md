# Eight clicks from the queue to a filed note; but the one button that files it sat off the bottom of my screen, and in gray the read-back wears the same stripe as a killer

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Caleb, 34, a prosthodontist working in a general practice for five years; the last office ran Eaglesoft, so my words are the tooth chart, the treatment plan, the clinical note, the walkout and the estimated insurance column.
Tech comfort 3 of 5: I use the mouse at the desk and my gloved, often wet hands at the chair; any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a target I will miss or hit twice.
I am deuteranopic, so I switched grayscale on and ranked every queue and every state by what was left: if two states collapse into one gray, that is a defect, not a taste.

## What I did

The driver ran three fresh page loads with the mouse (click, not Enter, so each press is one `click` event) plus one probe load; all at 1280 x 900, light theme, reduced motion, grayscale on. Load A carried dn-1 into dn-2. Load B redid the six setup clicks (Open, Chart it, D, O, Composite, starter; seq 1002-1021, not counted) because dn-3 has to happen before a note is filed, and dn-4 read the card in that state. Load C redid the setup with `?device=shared` for dn-5. In `events.json` load A keeps its seqs, B is offset by +1000, C by +2000, the probe load (P) by +3000. Every load ran with no `error` events and no missing selector.

**dn-1, open Theo Brandt from Exams to sign (1 click, budget 1, done).** The queue read "Exams to sign · 3 waiting · ordered by time in chair": Ines Okoro "Waiting 26 min · Note ready for signature", Theo Brandt "Waiting 14 min · Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L." with a chip "◆ 2 BWX, no interpretation", Devon Price "Out of chair · 7:30 am visit · Checked out, note unfiled — filing releases the held payment". In gray the ranking still held, because nothing in it was colour: the wait is a number, the reason is a sentence, and the one chip carries a diamond. In Eaglesoft I read the hygienist's finding from the chart after I opened it; here it is on the row. Open (seq 2-3) took me to `#/dentist/encounter/enc-9002`, and the first thing under the patient head was "Hygienist tags · Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L. · ▲ Needs disposition · Chart it · Dismiss", at y = 160 under a 61 px single-row top bar. Tooth 30 on the chart carried a dashed border; in gray, dashed against thirty-one solid borders reads without any hue.

**dn-2, chart #30 DO from the tag, plan, note, and File (7 clicks here, 8 from the queue, budget 10, done).** Chart it (seq 5) selected #30 and wrote "Selected: #30 · D O" with D and O dashed: "Distal, suggested by the tag, tap to confirm". D (seq 9) and O (seq 12) went solid. Composite 2-surf D2392 (seq 15) wrote three rows at once, chartEvents ce-1, procedures pr-500, planItems pl-1 (seq 16-18), and the card said so: "One paint, one transaction · Chart event Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO · ce-1 · Dr. Kim · Procedure D2392 · $260.00 pending charge, released at File · pr-500 · Note scaffold Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO · Plan card · pl-1 · $260.00 Fee · $130.00 Your plan pays about · $130.00 You'd owe about · Why · Undo last paint". Bree's tag flipped to "● Charted". The first starter (seq 21) filled Assessment "Caries #30 DO confirmed clinically and on BWX; asymptomatic; vitality normal." and Plan "Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given." and left my cursor in Assessment. File (seq 25) stopped at the read-back (refusal `readback`, seq 26): "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt · Confirm and file · Why", then "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for TB, DOB 11/1990, #30 DO · Switch author", then a 350 px outlined "Held — confirm the read-back". Two things about that moment. This is my own desk, `device=desk`, and it still asked me to read my own name back. And the verb line was on screen (y 840-883) but its one control was not: "Confirm and file" measured top 891, bottom 935, in a 900 px window whose canvas ends at 900; focus was put on it with `preventScroll`, so nothing moved to show it to me. I saw a sentence and no button. The driver scrolled for me; a person would hunt. Confirm and file (seq 29) wrote filedNotes nf-1, ledger le-5000 for $260.00 by the File event, claims c-100 to Cigna (seq 30-32), and the screen became "● Filed · ruleset 2.25.2 · byteaudit verified · By Dr. Hana Kim at 2026-09-03 08:40 · nf-1 · Charges released: 1 · $260.00 #30 · Claim queued · c-100 to Cigna · Queued to clearinghouse". Eight clicks from the queue to a filed note with the charge out; in Eaglesoft that was the chart, the treatment plan, "complete", the clinical note, the signature, and the walkout.

**dn-3, put money in the note and fix it with one control (3 clicks, budget 3, done; the contract's tap formula says 7 because the four spaces I typed into the Plan field count as taps).** Fresh chart, same six setup clicks (seq 1002-1021). I clicked into Plan (seq 1025), pressed End, typed " Patient portion $130 discussed" (seq 1026-1058), and pressed Tab to leave the field (seq 1059). Focus landed on File, the button still read "File", and no strip appeared; the screen let me carry a dollar sign to the gate without a word. File (seq 1060) then drew the strip (refusal `money_in_note`, seq 1061): "Before File · ▲ 1 to fix · Money stays out of the note · Move to plan card · Why", six words, one control, and File became the outlined "🔒 Held". Move to plan card (seq 1064) rewrote Plan to "Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given. See plan card for the quoted amount.", left Assessment alone, put "▬ Quoted to patient 9/3" on the plan card, and the gate went back to "● Nothing outstanding · File". One line, one button, no sermon. But after the click my focus was on BODY (seq 1065): the row I had just pressed was gone and nothing took its place. And in gray, the strip's stripe measured 114 of 255, the same 114 as the read-back's stripe two minutes earlier: a killer and a confirmation wear one stripe.

**dn-4, the plan card estimate and its rule trace (1 click, budget 1, done).** The card read "Plan card · pl-1 · ▬ Quoted to patient 9/3 · $260.00 Fee · $130.00 Your plan pays about · $130.00 You'd owe about · Why". Why (seq 1066, a 49 x 44 disclosure) opened "Cigna PPO: 50% after deductible (met) → patient est. $130.00. This is an estimate until the plan pays; it never joins the balance." Theo's carrier in the record is Cigna, so the payer, the percentage and the deductible are right, and "about" is the right word. Could I defend it chairside? For a two-surface composite, yes. For what I actually do all day, crowns and bridges, no: the card shows my fee, and does not say whether Cigna's allowed amount is my fee, what the annual maximum has left, or whether a frequency limit applies. In Eaglesoft the treatment plan showed estimated insurance beside the fee schedule; here I get a percentage and a promise. Labels measured 8.5:1 on the card, the numbers 20 px; I could read it to the patient.

**dn-5, the read-back line before File on a shared device (2 clicks, budget 2, done).** Fresh load with `?device=shared`, same six setup clicks (seq 2002-2021). The author chip read "DH DH · DDS": my initials twice and then the licence; the aria label said "Who is charting: Dr. Hana Kim, DDS", which is what the chip should have said. File (seq 2025) stopped at the read-back (refusal `readback`, seq 2026): "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt", eight words, "Confirm and file" 182.6 x 44 with focus on it, the Why, then "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for TB, DOB 11/1990, #30 DO" beside "Switch author" 154.1 x 44, and under that "Held — confirm the read-back" at 350 x 44. filedNotes had no enc-9002 row before I confirmed. Confirm and file (seq 2029) wrote nf-1, le-5000, c-100 (seq 2030-2032), author Dr. Hana Kim. Would it catch a stale author on a passed tablet? Yes: the author's name is the second thing in the line I must confirm, and Switch author is one click away. I would want it here and not on my desk.

**My measurements (gloved, deuteranopic, gray, 1280 x 900).** Across eleven screen states with 12 to 69 visible controls each, every button, field and Why disclosure measured at least 44 x 44 px: teeth 44 x 44 with 8.00 px between them, surfaces 44 x 44 and 8.00 px apart, Chart it and Dismiss 44 tall, the three gate buttons 182.6, 154.1 and 350 wide by 44, the Why folds 49 x 44. No two controls on the same layer sat closer than 8 px (the only pairs under 8 were the sticky top bar over scrolled teeth, different layers). No horizontal overflow: scrollWidth 1280 at 1280, two columns 856 + 376. Focus is a 3 px solid rgb(43, 108, 184) ring with a 2 px offset when I Tab (seq 3004, on Rail). Verb lines: "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt" eight words, "Money stays out of the note" six, each with exactly one control in its row, verb text 14.9:1 at 15 px; small muted text 8.9:1. Gray ranking of the chips I met, as gray of 255 (bg / rail / ink): required "Needs disposition" and "1 to fix" 248 / 114 / 71 with ▲; review "2 BWX" 251 / 132 / 25 with ◆; info "Quoted to patient" 250 / 114 / 63 with ▬; style "Today" 244 / 85 / 56 with ●; clear "Charted", "Nothing outstanding", "Filed" 249 / 116 / 74 with ●. The glyphs do the work the hues cannot do for me: required and info share a 114 rail and are told apart by ▲ against ▬, which is right. Two pairs are not told apart. Style and clear both use ●, and "● Today" sits on the same card as "● Charted" with rails 85 against 116 and inks 56 against 74. And the refusal rows have no glyph at all: the info read-back stripe and the required killer stripe both measure 114, so in gray a gate that says "confirm" and a gate that says "fix" are one stripe with different words. Tooth states survive gray by shape: tagged is dashed at 114, charted is solid at 85, selected is solid at 100 on a 237 fill. Surface states too: suggested is dashed, confirmed is solid on the 237 fill.

**The wet-glove probe (P, seq 3000s).** With #30 D charted, I pressed Composite 2-surf D2392 a second time (seq 3019). The screen painted #30 D again, ce-2, pr-501, pl-2 (seq 3020-3022), drew a second card, and announced "Charted Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO. Pending charge $260.00" as if it were the first. Nothing said #30 was already charted today. A wet glove lands twice; this is the one button in the room where twice costs the patient $260.

**Interview answers.** Would I sign hygiene-drafted notes here rather than bypass to a template? Yes, and the reason is that they are not hygiene-drafted: Bree's finding stays a tag until I press Chart it, the tooth and surfaces in the note come from what I painted, and the starter fills Assessment in words I would have typed. In Eaglesoft the autonote gave me blanks and the hygienist's finding lived in a different window. Which killer would I resent, which would I thank? I thank the money one; it took my sentence out, dated the quote on the plan card, and asked me nothing else. I resent the read-back on my own desk, where I am the only author from eight to five; on a passed tablet I want it, and I want its button where I can see it. What would make me trust the estimate the front desk quotes? The trace already names Cigna, the percentage and the deductible. Put the allowed amount and the remaining maximum on the same card, and say when a frequency limit applies, and I would let the desk read it to a crown patient without calling me over.
