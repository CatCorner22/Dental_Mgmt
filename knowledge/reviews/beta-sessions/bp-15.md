# Eight clicks from the queue to a filed note; but the one button that files it sat off the bottom of my screen, and in gray the read-back wears the same stripe as a killer

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Caleb, 34, a prosthodontist working in a general practice for five years; the last office ran Eaglesoft, so my words are the tooth chart, the treatment plan, the clinical note, the walkout and the estimated insurance column.
Tech comfort 3 of 5: I use the mouse at the desk and my gloved, often wet hands at the chair; any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a target I will miss or hit twice.
I am deuteranopic, so I switched grayscale on and ranked every queue and every state by what was left: if two states collapse into one gray, that is a defect, not a taste.

## What I did

**Interview answers.** Would I sign hygiene-drafted notes here rather than bypass to a template? Yes, and the reason is that they are not hygiene-drafted: Bree's finding stays a tag until I press Chart it, the tooth and surfaces in the note come from what I painted, and the starter fills Assessment in words I would have typed. In Eaglesoft the autonote gave me blanks and the hygienist's finding lived in a different window. Which killer would I resent, which would I thank? I thank the money one; it took my sentence out, dated the quote on the plan card, and asked me nothing else. I resent the read-back on my own desk, where I am the only author from eight to five; on a passed tablet I want it, and I want its button where I can see it. What would make me trust the estimate the front desk quotes? The trace already names Cigna, the percentage and the deductible. Put the allowed amount and the remaining maximum on the same card, and say when a frequency limit applies, and I would let the desk read it to a crown patient without calling me over.
