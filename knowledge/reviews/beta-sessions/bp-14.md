# Eight taps from the queue to a filed note is the best chairside flow I have used; at my 150% zoom the fix button I need sits off the right edge of the screen

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Wei, 19, one year in as a pediatric dentist; this is the first practice system I have worked in, so I have no old words for anything and I read what the screen says.
I have low vision: I run the browser at 150% zoom with high contrast, so I checked every screen at the 683 px width that zoom gives me, plus the 1024 px width the panel asked for, and I checked that every status carries a glyph and a word, not just a colour.
At the chair I work gloved and often wet: I tapped everything (no keyboard for the main flow), and I measured every control for 44 px and every neighbour for 8 px, because anything smaller I will hit by mistake.

## What I did

**dn-1, open Theo Brandt's chart from Exams to sign (1 tap, budget 1, done).** Exams to sign read "3 waiting · ordered by time in chair" and the rows came in that order: "Ines Okoro · Chair 3 · Dr. Kim · Waiting 26 min · Note ready for signature", then "Theo Brandt · Chair 2 · Dr. Kim · Waiting 14 min · Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L. · ◆ 2 BWX, no interpretation", then Devon Price "Out of chair · 7:30 am visit · Checked out, note unfiled — filing releases the held payment". So yes, the queue is chair wait first and each row tells me what the hygienist found before I open it. One tap on Open (events-part1.json seq 2) and the route was dentist/encounter/enc-9002 (seq 3). Under the header "Theo Brandt · DOS 9/3/2026 · Dr. Kim · Chair 2 · Restorative · DOB 11/2/1990" the first card was "Hygienist tags: Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L. · ▲ Needs disposition · Chart it · Dismiss", and tooth 30 on the chart below already had a dashed orange border. The one thing that was not right: after the route change my focus was on the page body, so a keyboard user starts again from the top bar.

**dn-2, chart #30 DO from the tag, plan, note, File (7 taps here, 8 with the Open, budget 10, done).** "Chart it" (seq 5) selected tooth 30, moved my focus onto it, and drew D and O as dashed "suggested" surfaces with the line "Selected: #30 · D O". I tapped D and O to confirm them (seq 9, 12); the dashes went solid. One tap on "Composite 2-surf D2392" (seq 15) wrote chartEvents ce-1, procedures pr-500, and planItems pl-1 in the same instant (seq 16–18) and the card said so in words: "One paint, one transaction · Chart event Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO · ce-1 · Dr. Kim · Procedure D2392 · $260.00 pending charge, released at File · pr-500 · Note scaffold Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO", with the plan card under it and "Undo last paint". The tag chip flipped to "● Charted" without my touching it. The starter "Caries confirmed, composite today" (seq 21) filled Assessment ("Caries #30 DO confirmed clinically and on BWX; asymptomatic; vitality normal.") and Plan ("Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given.") and put my cursor at the end of Assessment so I could edit. File (seq 25) gave the read-back "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt" (8 words, refusal code readback at seq 26) with one filled "Confirm and file" control already focused, a Why fold, a "Switch author" button, and the File button turned into the outlined lock "Held — confirm the read-back". Confirm (seq 29) wrote filedNotes nf-1, the $260.00 ledger charge le-5000, and claim c-100 to Cigna (seq 30–32); the card read "Filed · ruleset 2.25.2 · byteaudit verified · Charges released: 1 · $260.00 #30 · Claim queued · c-100 to Cigna". The killer strip never had to appear: the gate said "● Nothing outstanding". Eight taps, budget ten. Two things I noticed: the gate at read-back carries three buttons for one gate (Confirm and file 183×44, Switch author 154×44, Held 966×44), and the author chip in the top bar reads "DH DH · DDS", initials twice.

**dn-3, put money in the note and fix it with one control (6 taps by the panel's formula, 2 control presses, budget 3; done).** The script says "before filing", but dn-2 had just filed the note and the encounter now shows only the filed card, so I had to reset the prototype and redo the six setup taps (events.json seq 1–24; not counted). I put my cursor in Plan, typed " Patient portion $130 discussed" and left the field with Tab (seq 25–58). Nothing changed: the gate still showed the green "● Nothing outstanding · Ruleset 2.25.2 · File runs the same audit server-side." and a filled navy File button while "$130" sat in the plan a few lines above (screenshot dn-3-killer.png). Only when I pressed File (seq 60) did the strip appear: "Before File · ▲ 1 to fix · Money stays out of the note · Move to plan card · Why · Held" (refusal money_in_note, seq 61). One row, six words, one control. "Move to plan card" (seq 64) rewrote the plan to "Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given. See plan card for the quoted amount.", the plan card grew a chip "▬ Quoted to patient 9/3", the gate went back to "Nothing outstanding" and File came back filled. I later checked why the strip had not come up on blur: when I leave the field by tapping anywhere else on the page it appears after a moment, but when focus moves straight into the gate (Tab from Plan lands on File) the swap is skipped, and even a further Tab away does not trigger it. The four taps the formula counts beyond my two presses are the spaces I typed (seq 27, 35, 43, 48); the contract counts a space on any element with a testid.

**dn-4, read the plan card estimate and its rule trace (1 tap, budget 1, done).** The plan card read "Plan card · pl-1 · $260.00 Fee · $130.00 Your plan pays about · $130.00 You'd owe about" with a Why fold. One tap on Why (seq 66) opened "Cigna PPO: 50% after deductible (met) → patient est. $130.00. This is an estimate until the plan pays; it never joins the balance." The carrier on the trace matches the patient's carrier in the store (Cigna), and the claim in dn-2 went to Cigna too. Could I defend $130 chairside? Mostly: carrier, percentage, deductible met, and the fee are all on one card in plain words. What is missing is when that was verified; if a parent asks "as of when?", the card does not say.

**dn-5, confirm the read-back before File on the shared device (2 taps, budget 2, done).** File (seq 68) gave "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt" (refusal readback, seq 69), focus already on "Confirm and file", and beneath it the detail line "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for TB, DOB 11/1990, #30 DO" next to "Switch author". Confirm (seq 72) wrote nf-1, le-5000, c-100 (seq 73–75). Would it catch a stale author on a passed tablet? The name is there, the licence is on the author chip, and Switch author is one tap away at the gate, so yes, if the person reads. But the verb line says the full patient name and the detail line under it says "TB": two spellings of the same fact an inch apart, and with Privacy on (privacy-events.json) the full name is still there in both the page header and the verb while the Exams list had gone to initials.

**My zoom, contrast, size and keyboard checks (Wei-specific).** At 1024 px: 68 controls on the encounter, none under 44×44, no two controls on the same layer closer than 8 px, no clipped labels, no horizontal scroll (document 1024/1024, canvas 1024/1024). At 683 px (my 150% zoom): the Exams list reflows to two columns and nothing clips, but the encounter does not fit. The odontogram grid is a fixed 16 × 44 px plus 15 × 8 px = 824 px, the grid column stretches to hold it, and the whole page becomes 866 px wide inside a 683 px canvas (canvas scrollWidth 866, clientWidth 683). Teeth 13–20 are off the right edge, the note fields and the File button are 824 px wide, and with the killer strip up the "Move to plan card" control (left 652, right 840) and the "▲ 1 to fix" chip (765–853) are entirely off-screen; I have to drag the canvas sideways to find the fix (zoom-killer-left.png, zoom-killer-right.png). The sticky top bar wraps to 113 px at 1024 and 165 px (three rows) at 683, which is a third of a 512 px viewport that never scrolls away. Contrast in the light theme is generous: body and read-back verb 14.9:1, muted lines 8.9:1, chips 7.3–8.2:1, reversible buttons 7.8:1, the Why link 6.7:1 at the low end. Every status I met carried a glyph and a word (▲ Needs disposition, ● Charted, ● Today, ● Nothing outstanding, ▲ 1 to fix, ▬ Quoted to patient 9/3). In forced-colours mode the tagged tooth stays dashed, the suggested surface stays dashed, and the selected surface gets the Highlight border, with "Selected: #30 · D O" in words beside it. Keyboard: 52 Tab stops on the encounter, every one with a solid 3 px outline; arrow keys walk the arch (30 → 29) and the letter B toggled the buccal surface. And when I mis-tapped a procedure before a tooth on a fresh copy (mistap-events.json seq 4–10) the screen said "Pick a tooth before the procedure · Go to teeth" and put my focus on tooth 30, the tagged one.
