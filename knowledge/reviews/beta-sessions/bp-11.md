# One paint did the chart, the plan, and the note in eight taps; but the plan card says Cigna to a Delta patient, and Switch author filed my partner's words under my name

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Quinn, 36, nine years a general dentist and the owner of the practice; the last office ran Dentrix, so my words are Chart module, Treatment Planner, Clinical Notes, Ledger, and "walkout".
Tech comfort 3 of 5, no access adjustments; I work gloved and often wet at the chair, so I test at the pace of a busy morning, and any control under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a defect I will hit.
Device today: the operatory glass at 1024 x 768, light theme, motion auto; the prototype had me charting as Dr. Hana Kim, DDS.

## What I did

**dn-1, open Theo Brandt's seated chart from Exams to sign (1 tap, budget 1, done).** The queue read "Exams to sign · 3 waiting · ordered by time in chair": Ines Okoro "Waiting 26 min · Note ready for signature", Theo Brandt "Waiting 14 min · Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L." with a chip "2 BWX, no interpretation", Devon Price "Out of chair · 7:30 am visit · Checked out, note unfiled — filing releases the held payment". Longest in the chair on top, the hygiene finding on the row, the practice line underneath "median ready → filed 11 min · 3 notes filed (practice level, nobody ranked)": no one is scored, which is how I want my associates to see it. Open (seq 2) landed on `/dentist/encounter/enc-9002` (seq 3) with "Theo Brandt · DOS 9/3/2026 · Dr. Kim · Chair 2 · Restorative · DOB 11/2/1990" and, first card under the name at 212 px, "Hygienist tags: Suspected caries #30 DO — Bree L. · Needs disposition · Chart it · Dismiss". Tooth 30 in the odontogram already wore a dashed orange border. One thing on that screen was wrong before I touched anything: the who-is-charting chip in the top bar read "DH  DH · DDS". I am Dr. Hana Kim today; DH is not me, and it is printed twice (seq 1-3, exams.png).

**dn-2, chart #30 DO composite from the tag, plan, note, File (7 taps, 8 with dn-1, budget 10, done).** Chart it (seq 5) selected tooth 30 and put D and O on the surface row as dashed "suggested by the tag, tap to confirm"; the line read "Selected: #30 · D O". I confirmed D and O (seq 9, 12): they went solid. Composite 2-surf D2392 (seq 15) wrote three rows at once (seq 16-18): "Chart event Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO · ce-1 · Dr. Kim", "Procedure D2392 · $260.00 pending charge, released at File · pr-500", "Note scaffold Composite, 2 surf posterior #30 DO", plus the plan card, plus "Undo last paint". The tag flipped to "Charted" on its own. In Dentrix that is the Chart module, then a procedure code, then the Treatment Planner, then a Clinical Notes template; here it was one press. The starter "Caries confirmed, composite today" (seq 23) filled Assessment "Caries #30 DO confirmed clinically and on BWX; asymptomatic; vitality normal." and Plan "Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given." with the cursor in Assessment, ready to edit. File (seq 70) did not file: the gate became "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt" (8 words) with one filled "Confirm and file", a Why, a detail line "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for TB, DOB 11/1990, #30 DO", "Switch author", and File itself turned into the outlined padlock "Held — confirm the read-back" (966 x 44 px, not disabled). Confirm (seq 74) wrote filedNotes nf-1, ledger le-5000 ($260.00, actorKind file_event, released by nf-1) and claim c-100 to Cigna "Queued to clearinghouse" (seq 75-77); the screen became "Filed · ruleset 2.25.2 · byteaudit verified · By Dr. Hana Kim at 2026-09-03 08:40 · nf-1 · text and version frozen; corrections are addenda, never edits · Charges released: 1 · $260.00 #30 · Claim queued · c-100 to Cigna". Clean path from Open through Confirm: 8 presses. My run also carried the dn-4 Why press and the dn-3 detour (2 presses), 11 presses in total; by the contract's formula, which counts the four spaces I typed into the Plan field as taps, it is 15. I noticed afterwards that the two surface confirmations were optional: the paint takes the dashed surfaces as they are, so the true floor is six presses.

**dn-4, read the plan card and its rule trace (1 tap, budget 1, done, with a caveat I found later).** Straight after the paint the plan card read "Plan card · pl-1 · $260.00 Fee · $130.00 Your plan pays about · $130.00 You'd owe about · Why". The carrier and the rule are not on the card: I had to press Why (seq 21) to read "Cigna PPO: 50% after deductible (met) → patient est. $130.00. This is an estimate until the plan pays; it never joins the balance." For Theo that is right, he is Cigna. I went back afterwards on a fresh copy and painted the same D2392 on Ines Okoro, who is Delta Dental primary and MetLife secondary (events-probe-carrier.json seq 10-15): her card read the same "Cigna PPO: 50% after deductible (met) → patient est. $130.00". The trace is one sentence for everybody. I could defend Theo's number chairside; I could not defend Ines's, and neither could my front desk.

**dn-3, put money in the note and fix it with one control (2 presses, 6 by the formula, budget 3, done).** With the caret at the end of Plan I typed " Patient portion $130 discussed." (seq 27-58) and left the field with Shift+Tab (seq 59-60). The strip fired at once (refusal money_in_note, seq 61), but not where I was looking: the glass in front of me still showed the Plan field with the dollar sentence and nothing else changed (dn-3-blur.png); "Before File · 1 to fix" was a screen further down. Scrolling to it: one row, "Money stays out of the note" (6 words), one 188 x 44 control "Move to plan card", a Why ("Clinical text is exported on records requests; quoted amounts live on the plan card with the date, never in the note."), and File already turned into the padlock "Held". Pressing Held (seq 64) re-ran the audit and refused again (seq 65), no lecture. Move to plan card (seq 68) replaced my sentence with "See plan card for the quoted amount.", kept "Composite #30 DO today under local; postoperative instructions given." untouched, put a chip "Quoted to patient 9/3" on the plan card, and the gate flipped to "Nothing outstanding · Ruleset 2.25.2 · File runs the same audit server-side." That is the fix I wanted. Two things about the moment after it: the plan card it told me about was 105 px above the top of the glass, and the button I had pressed was gone with focus dropped on the page body (seq 67-68). Of the six taps the formula charges, four are the spaces in the sentence the script told me to type.

**dn-5, confirm the read-back before File on a shared device (2 taps, budget 2, done).** Fresh load with `?device=shared` (events-shared.json): Open, Chart it, D, O, D2392, starter, then File (seq 25) gave the same info-railed line as on my operatory glass, "Filing as Dr. Hana Kim for Theo Brandt" with "Confirm and file" (refusal readback, seq 26); filedNotes was empty until Confirm (seq 29) wrote nf-1, le-5000, c-100 (seq 30-32). File completed only after Confirm, as promised. The top-bar chip still read "DH  DH · DDS". Would it catch a stale author on a passed tablet? I tried the case (events-probe-switchfile.json seq 29-51). With Dr. Kim's Assessment and Plan drafted and the read-back showing, Switch author opened "Who is charting? Enter the other person's PIN. Their session opens on this page; yours is revoked and local drafts are wiped after autosave." I entered Dr. Reagan's PIN. The route became `/owner/encounter/enc-9002`, the chip "DB  DB · DDS", the line "Filing as Dr. Blake Reagan for Theo Brandt", and Dr. Kim's Assessment and Plan were still in the fields, word for word. Confirm and file wrote nf-1 "By Dr. Blake Reagan" carrying Dr. Kim's text, over chart event ce-1 still authored "Dr. Kim", and the $260 charge with actor Dr. Blake Reagan. The line caught the author; the words did not follow the author. That is the stale-author problem the gate exists for, turned inside out.

**My gloved-hand checks.** On six screens (Exams 13 controls, encounter open 65, painted 65, killer gate 67, read-back gate 68, filed 12) every button, textarea, and summary with a data-testid measured at least 44 x 44 px by getBoundingClientRect: teeth 44 x 44 with an 8.0 px gap in a 966 px row, surface keys, the procedure strip, the fix control 188 x 44, Confirm and file, the Held bar 966 x 44, PIN keys 72 x 56. No two controls on the same layer were closer than 8 px; the only sub-8 px pairs the audit reported are between the sticky top bar and content that had scrolled under it (topbar.theme and topbar.signout over enc.surface.30.o, enc.undo, enc.plan.pl-1.why), a different layer. No horizontal overflow: document scrollWidth 1024 at 1024 wide; the encounter grid collapses to one 992 px column. Keyboard focus is visible, a 3 px solid rgb(43,108,184) outline with a 2 px white shadow on enc.tooth.1, enc.tooth.30, and enc.tag.tag-1.chart; the script-focused Confirm and file after a mouse press shows no ring (`:focus-visible` false), which a gloved finger does not need. Verb lines: money_in_note 6 words, readback 8, at the contract's limit. Killer strip 1 row of a maximum 3. What the one column costs at 768 px tall: tags at 212, teeth at 384, procedure strip at 720, Note at 1044, the gate at 1359 and File at 1403, under a sticky top bar that wraps to two rows and holds 113 px (15 percent) of the glass the whole visit.
