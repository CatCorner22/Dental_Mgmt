# Two taps to seat and three to check out, but do not make me explain an $88 credit on a $44 payment

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Yara, 24, one year on the front desk at a three-chair office; before this we ran tab32, so "schedule", "walkout", and "patient portion" are the words in my head.
I would put my tech comfort at 2 of 5: I learn a screen by doing it forty times on a Monday, not by reading about it.
No access adjustments today; a desk monitor, dark theme, reduced motion, and I test at the speed of a busy morning with a line at the window.

## What I did

**fo-1, arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 taps, budget 2, done).** I clicked Arrive on the 9:00 card and the screen read "Marisol Vega arrived. Seat is the next step on the same card" and put my cursor on Seat for me, so the second click was already lined up. After Seat, the chair strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH" and the card flipped to "In chart". Before arrival the card carried a "Verify" chip and a 44 px "Re-verify" button next to it, and the strip above had a second place, "2 insured patients came back amber at 6 am — first at 9:00 am / Re-verify all". Two places for the same thing is one more than I need, but each one was one line and one button, which is what the card promised. This is the tab32 schedule with the extra clicks taken out.

**fo-2, Ines Okoro, $44 by card (3 taps, budget 4, done).** Checkout opened from the card, "Collect" was already pressed, the estimate column had its own shaded header "Patient portion (estimate)" and the total read "$44.00 est." with "Estimate is separate from the balance above; it never enters the ledger", so yes, I could tell the estimate from the balance. Card, sixteen digits, Post. What confused me: the three tiles at the top said "$0.00 Patient due · $0.00 Waiting on insurance · $0.00 Credit" while the table said she owed $44 (seq 11-14), and after Post the Credit tile jumped to "$88.00" for a $44 payment (seq 34-40). If Ines had been standing there she would have asked me why she has twice her payment sitting as credit, and I would not have had an answer. The chip "Note unfiled — Filed-later lane" told me Dr. Kim had not filed yet, and the Post summary said "held as credit until the note is filed", which I understood. Back on the Board her card did not say "checked out"; it moved to a "Filed later" lane and read "Paid at the window · charges and claim release when Dr. Kim files the note" (seq 41-42). Fair enough, but the task sheet said the card would read checked out.

**fo-3, Ruth Adler, $0 portion (2 taps, budget 2, done).** Checkout, Post. "Nothing due today" was already pressed with nothing for me to pick, and the text under it said "Patient portion is $0. Post still writes the collection decision (reason zero_due) so the day's 'Not collected at window' line stays honest." No refusal, no dead end. In tab32 a zero walkout meant a fake $0 payment line; this is cleaner.

**fo-4, Samir Haddad pays in full, off insurance (5 taps, budget 5, done).** The toggles "Paid in full — don't send to insurance" were already showing on both lines when Checkout opened, before I picked Cash (seq 55-57), because the prefilled $168.00 already covered each fee. The task sheet said they would appear once the tender covered the fee, so I was watching for something that never needed to happen. The wording is plain and there is no policy paragraph, no word "HIPAA" anywhere on the screen; I could read it to the patient as is. What did throw me: the Board's Details said "Balance $180.00 · patient portion, estimate separate" for Samir, and Checkout said fee total $168.00, estimate $168.00, Patient due $0.00 (seq 55-57; Board reading from a separate probe run, probe-samir-balance.png). Three numbers for one exam. After Post both lines read "Self-pay restriction on pr-421: claim assembly refuses it", which is a sentence I would trust.

**fo-5, patient at the window whose note is not filed (1 tap of 3, NOT completed).** Devon Price's queue row already read "Filed later · Note ◆ Open · BL · Claim ▬ Held · Note not filed — Bree L." before I touched anything, with one button, "Ping chair". I pinged (seq 80-81), the row stamped "Pinged chair 1 · 8:40 am · one-to-one, not broadcast", and the pressure went to Bree's chair, not to me; nobody made me the note cop. Then the second step, `board.queue.row.a-1050.checkout`, did not exist: the row ends with "Paid at the window · in the Filed-later lane until Bree L. files" and offers no Checkout because the seed already checked Devon out (status `checked_out_unfiled`). I could not run the script's checkout or Post, so the task failed as written. Two things I noticed while stuck: after Ping my keyboard focus went nowhere (`document.activeElement` was the page body, seq 80-81), and when I typed the checkout address for Devon by hand as a probe (seq 82-86, outside the script), the screen showed an "Already checked out" chip and still let me Post, writing a second collection decision "cd-4: Nothing due today" for an encounter that was already done.

**fo-6, find Lena Fischer with three letters (2 taps, budget 3, done).** Ctrl+K put me in the search box, "fis" gave me three Fischers, and each row already showed "DOB 7/8/1969 · …0126" (seq 91-96). I clicked Lena, the box said "Confirm date of birth" with the hint "Second identifier. Ask the patient, or read it from the appointment card", I typed 07/08/1969, Enter, and the Rail opened on the left reading "Lena Fischer / DOB 7/8/1969 · phone …0126". It worked, but the gate asked me to type the date it had just shown me on the row above; I could pass it without Lena in the room. Probe with a wrong date (01/01/1970, seq 118-131): "Date of birth does not match" with one "Try again" button, the Open chart button turned into an outlined "Held" with a lock, and the screen reader line said the same. That refusal is exactly what I want a temp to hit. One more thing the Rail did: with it open, the Op 3 column slid under the Checkout queue and Lena's own Checkout button was half hidden (seq 108-111).
