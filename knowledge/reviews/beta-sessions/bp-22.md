# The digits walked the whole mouth without one wrong jump, but on my operatory screen the lower arch was below the fold the entire time, and Space on the wrong button tried to save the chart

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Malik, 20, one year in as a hygienist with a perio focus; the last office ran Eaglesoft, so I think in the perio chart's number pad and the Tab key.
I work gloved with wet hands at the chair, on the operatory monitor at 1024 by 768, dark theme, reduced motion; anything under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is a target I will hit by mistake.
Tech comfort 5 of 5 and no access adjustments: I test at the speed of a busy morning, I measure every control I press (getBoundingClientRect, computed outline), and I count my own presses against the log.

## What I did

**hy-1, open Perio for Marisol Vega from my first card (1 press, budget 1, done).** The 9:00 card read "Marisol Vega · Confirmed · Chair 1 · Hygiene · Recall due", then a stop chip "New anticoagulant (apixaban), intake 2 days ago", then the strip "Since last visit · Med hx changed · New anticoagulant (apixaban), intake 2 days ago · Perio 14 mo ago · BWX due practice rule: 12 mo · What helped last time stop signal, sunglasses · More" (seq 1-3). So yes: what changed, what is due, what helped, in one line, zero taps. The anticoagulant is printed twice on the same card, once as an alert chip and once in the strip; I read it twice before I understood it was one fact. The author chip read "BL · RDH", initials only on the operatory glass, which is right. All 24 controls on Chairs were at least 44 x 44 and none closer than 8 px. I focused Perio (99.2 x 44) and pressed Enter: route #/hygienist/perio/enc-9001, the cursor at tooth 2 site 1 (tooth 1 is missing, so the first present tooth), 168 ghost values from the 7/1/2025 exam in the cells, and the subtitle "Prior exam 7/1/2025 ghosted · 28 teeth (x = missing: 1, 16, 17, 32) · Keys: 1–9 depth · Space bleed · S suppuration · ⌫ undo · → skip · PgDn next tooth" (seq 3-4). One thing for the record: after landing, document.activeElement was BODY. The grid took my keys anyway, but nothing on the screen had focus.

**hy-2, full-mouth six-point perio, keyboard only, one pass (1 press, budget 1, 173 keystrokes of 200, done).** I typed 168 digits, mostly 2 to 4 with six 5s and 6s, and pressed Space after five of them for bleeding; then Save exam (139.8 x 44). Store: pe-2 with probed 168, skipped 0, bleeding 5, deepest 6, and notes[enc-9001].perioSummary "Perio: 168 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 5 sites." plus "SRP evidence: 6 sites at or above 5 mm." (seq 5-183). I sampled the cursor every 24 sites and it never once disagreed with where I expected to be (jumps: none); the path goes 2 to 15 facial, back 15 to 2 lingual, then 31 to 18 facial and 18 to 31 lingual, which is how I probe anyway. The active-site line "Tooth 10 · site 2 · prior 2 · Sites recorded: 25/168" kept me oriented, and the stamp "Saved to tooth #15 (draft, this session)" ticked along under the grid. Now the part that matters at the chair: from site 85 onward (tooth 27 site 2, seq around 98) the active cell sat at y 820 to 864 in a 768 px window, and by tooth 24 site 5 it was at y 976 to 1020; the canvas (clientHeight 655) stayed at scrollTop 0 for all 168 keys because focus was on BODY and nothing scrolls a cell into view. For the entire lower arch I could not see the cell I was filling, the value I had just typed, or the ghosted prior depth I was supposed to compare against; the only thing on screen was the top line. On an operatory monitor that is half the mouth charted blind. Save was one press; afterwards focus landed on "Tag for dentist" in the saved card (canvas scrolled to 814) and the live region read "Exam saved. Perio: 168 sites probed, deepest 6 mm, bleeding at 5 sites. 4-month perio maintenance with BWX". Ghost values are 10 px at 5.83:1 against the cell; legible up close, not from a probe's length away.

**hy-3, undo a mistyped depth and skip a site without recording zero (0 presses, budget 0, NOT completed as scripted; done on a fresh chart as a probe, 4 presses).** The script runs this after hy-2 with state carried, and hy-2 ends in an irreversible Save. So I typed 9, Backspace, 3, ArrowRight and the grid ignored all four: keystrokes stayed at 173, the line read "Exam saved · grid is read-only", the flash stayed empty, and the Save control was gone, replaced by a chip "Exam saved" (seq 184-187). No message told me the keys were being dropped; the line said read-only and that was all. I then rebuilt the store (Proto.store.reset, not __proto.reset, so the event log stays continuous) and walked the real path Chairs → Perio (seq 188-192). On the fresh chart: ten digits, then 9 at tooth 5 site 2 (cell shaded, "9 mm, prior 2"), Backspace: the cell went back to "—" with its ghost 2, the cursor stepped back to tooth 5 site 2, and the flash line read "Undo: removed 9 at tooth 5 site 2" at 15 px, 101 px above the active cell, for two seconds, with the same sentence in the live region (seq 203-205). Then 3, then nine more digits, then ArrowRight at tooth 8 site 3: the cell showed "—" in the skipped shade with aria-description "not probed, prior 3" and the count line grew "· 1 not probed" (seq 217-227). I filled the rest and pressed Save (seq 365): "Name why 1 sites were not probed" (seven words), one 44 px control "Choose a reason" (175.6 x 44), a Why summary (49.2 x 44), refusal code omission_licence logged, and Save flipped to an outlined lock "Held" (97.6 x 44) with aria-label "Save exam is held: Name why 1 sites were not probed". Focus was already on the control. Enter: the chooser "Why were 1 sites not probed?" opened with four 44 px reasons and focus on Implant; I chose "Patient could not tolerate", focus moved to the confirm, which had changed from "Choose a reason above" to "Save exam with this reason" (281.8 x 44); Enter saved pe-2 with probed 167, skipped 1, licence not_tolerated, the undone site stored as depth 3 and the skipped site as depth null, skipped true, never 0 (seq 366-381). The note, though, reads "1 not probed (not_tolerated)" with the raw code where the chooser said "Patient could not tolerate", and the card says "1 sites not probed". The undo feedback is honest and specific, but it is a line of text at the top of the screen and a two-second timer; gloved, with my eyes on the probe, I would hear it only if a screen reader were running.

**hy-4, tag #30 for the dentist as a finding, not a diagnosis (2 presses, budget 3, done).** "Tag for dentist" (159.8 x 44, aria-label "Tag a tooth for the dentist: a finding, not a diagnosis") opened a card "Tag for Dr. Kim" with the hint "A finding, not a diagnosis. The tag lands as a checklist row on the dentist's exam; sign refuses until it has a disposition." and six 44 px observation words (seq 384-386). Focus went into the Tooth field, which was already filled with 14, the deepest pocket on my chart; I had come to tag 30, so I selected the field and typed over it, then typed "suspected caries DO" in Observation and pressed Save tag (122.2 x 44). Store: tag-2, encounterId enc-9001, tooth 30, text "suspected caries DO", disposition null; chip "Tagged #30 · waiting for dentist"; live region "Tagged tooth 30 for the dentist. A finding, not a diagnosis."; focus back on Tag for dentist (seq 412-415). The scope felt respected: nothing asked me to pick a diagnosis code, and the vocabulary was observation words. Two quibbles: the prefilled 14 is a guess I did not make, and Marisol's chair has no dentist on it in the record, so "Dr. Kim" is a name the screen made up.

**hy-5, mark ready for exam (2 presses, budget 2, done).** Chairs in the top bar (89.3 x 44), then the card now carried "Perio charted today · 167 sites probed, deepest 5 mm" in its strip and a "Ready for exam" button (289.7 x 44, aria-label "Ready for exam: Marisol Vega joins the dentist's queue") that had not been there before the chart existed (seq 418-421). Enter: appointmentEvents ae-1 encounter.exam_requested, status ready_for_exam, chips "Exam requested" and "Exam: 1st in queue", the live region "Marisol Vega ready for exam: 1st in queue. The Board chair strip shows Exam requested.", and focus handed to Note on the same card (seq 422-425). Clean.

**hy-6, run the screening lane (2 presses, budget 2, NOT completed as scripted; done on a fresh chart as a probe, 2 presses and 6 keystrokes of 12).** Perio from the card again: the screen landed with the canvas at scrollTop 414, so "Full chart · Screening · Glove pad · Settings" and the Exam saved chip were 202 px above the top edge, under the sticky bar (seq 428-429). I focused Screening anyway and pressed Enter: click logged (seq 432), aria-pressed stayed false, mode stayed full, no announcement; I typed 212321 and the keystroke count did not move; there was no Save (seq 426-438). Same wall as hy-3: once the exam is saved, this encounter's Perio screen is done, and it says so only by ignoring me. On a fresh store via Chairs → Perio (seq 439-443): Screening pressed, focus on sextant UR, the grid replaced by six boxes 325 x 88 "— UR · 1–5" and so on, the line "Sextant UR (teeth 1–5) · keys 0–4 or * · Codes: 0/6"; six keys gave "All six sextants coded · Save exam · Codes: 6/6 · Full chart due"; Save wrote pe-2 with mode screening and the card "Screening codes: UR 2 · UA 1 · UL 2 · LL 3 · LA 2 · LR 1 · Full chart due" (seq 446-466). Obviously different from the full chart: no grid, six big boxes, its own count line. But the derived note said "Perio: 0 sites probed, deepest 0 mm, bleeding at 0 sites." for a screening, which a dentist would read as a chart with nothing in it.

**Probes outside the script (fresh charts, seq 467-521).** (a) Six digits in, I put focus on Save exam and pressed Space, the bleeding key: Save fired, the gate "Name why 162 sites were not probed" came up and Save turned into Held (seq 472-483). Had the chart been complete, that Space would have committed the exam. (b) 0 then 7: "Depth 17 mm is above the 15 mm limit" (eight words), control "Re-enter the depth" (197.5 x 44), Save became Held, the cursor stayed on tooth 2 site 2; focus stayed on BODY rather than moving to the control, but typing a digit still records, and the control returned focus to the cell (seq 493-500). (c) With a grid cell focused, the cell follows the cursor as I type (t2-s1 → t3-s1 after three digits), so the fold problem in hy-2 goes away if focus is inside the grid; but then a Space for bleeding is logged as a key on a data-testid and counts as a tap by the contract formula (seq 506-514). (d) Settings: "Last key pressed: Space → Bleeding on at tooth 2 site 3" and "Keystrokes this exam: 4 (counted, never scored per person)". (e) Glove pad: nine 44 x 44 keys, Bleed, skip, undo, and a 252 x 56 Next tooth, gaps 8 px. Across all seven measurements (174 to 193 controls each) no control was under 44 x 44, no two were closer than 8 px, the focus ring was "solid 3px rgb(143, 193, 245)" plus a 2 px shadow on all 23 controls I pressed, and the document never scrolled sideways (scrollWidth 1024, perio-wrap 992 of 992). Every Enter I pressed on a button was logged twice, as "key Enter" and as "click" on the same testid (seq 2-3), so the contract's tap formula scores my 12 presses as 24.
