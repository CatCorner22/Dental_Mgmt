# The EOB read itself back to me in three lines, but the write-off screen told me two different names to go find

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Grace, 51, eighteen years posting insurance; the last office ran Eaglesoft, so my words are "day sheet", "EOB", "walkout", "account", "adjustment".
Tech comfort 2 of 5: I learn one path and repeat it; I do not read policy paragraphs at 8:40 in the morning with a payer on hold.
No access adjustments; I tested at the speed of a busy morning on the desk monitor, light theme, motion reduced, mouse in hand.

## What I did

**bo-1, post the matched Delta lines and read back only the differing ones (4 taps, budget 4, done).** The ERA tab was already open: "Delta 835 · 41 lines · 37 posted before you sat down · EFT $4,812.33 · TRN 20260903-90112 · matched to bank line" with chips "37 posted · 3 deltas · 1 denial" and one button, Post matched, with the sentence "Posts the 37 matched lines; only lines that differ from the claim will ask you." I pressed it and the card became "Read back: 3 lines differ from the claim" with exactly three rows, not forty-one (seq 2-3). Each row is one line I used to build by hand off the paper EOB: "Cole Fischer · Line 14 · D2740 Crown, porcelain/ceramic · #19 · Contract variance / expected $590.00, ERA says $540.00 · CARC 45 (contract underpayment)". Ten words on the money line, about thirty per row; I read all three in the time it takes to say them. Confirm on 14 wrote the insurance payment and the write-off (seq 5-6), Hold on 22, Dispute contract variance on 31 (seq 7-14); each press took its row away and moved the chips ("38 posted · 2 deltas", then "1 held · 1 disputed"). End card: "Batch complete: 38 posted, 1 held, 1 disputed, 1 denial moved to Denials." One thing I would want on the screen: the button says only Confirm; the $50.00 I am agreeing to write off is in a hidden label ("Confirm line 14: post $540.00 and a contractual write-off of $50.00"), not on the button or the row.

**bo-2, appeal c-88 from the record (3 taps, budget 2, done, one over).** After the batch finished my cursor was sitting on the Denials tab but the ERA card was still showing, so the tab was my first press (seq 15-16). Denials read "Ines Aziz $285.00 · c-88 · D4341 SRP, 4+ teeth per quadrant · Delta Dental · Appeal by 11/2", then "Delta says the claim is missing information: the perio chart was not attached." and "Next: Appeal with the perio chart and the SRP narrative from the note." That is CARC 16 / N4 in English; the codes are under a "Why this code" fold I did not need. Appeal opened "Appeal packet · ap-1 · built from the record" with four slots all Clear: perio chart pe-1 dated 7/1/2025, narrative from the filed note, bitewings, letter (seq 18-21), and "What the patient reads: Delta asked for your gum chart; we are sending it. You owe nothing while they review." Send put my cursor on Send, I pressed it, and the tab flipped to "No open denials · Appeal for c-88 sent · disclosure recorded." (seq 22-25). In Eaglesoft that appeal was forty minutes of pulling the perio chart and writing a narrative. Here it was two presses.

**bo-3, try to bill the patient for the denied claim (0 taps, budget 1, NOT completed as scripted).** There was nothing to press. After I sent the appeal the Denials tab showed "No open denials" and c-88 was gone; I looked on Aging and Approvals too and it was on neither (seq 26-34, three tab presses of my own looking around, not counted against the task). An appealed claim has no home on this desk, and the scripted Bill patient control does not exist once the appeal is sent. To see the gate at all I opened a fresh copy (probe-bill-events.json, seq 4-8): pressing Bill patient before appealing gave "Appeal first — denial with no appeal", seven words, one 44 px button "Build appeal", a Why fold, and nothing posted (ledger stayed at 114 rows). I understood it without reading the Why. Build appeal opened the same packet and put me on Send. That gate is good. The problem is the order: the task as written cannot be run, and in real life I would want to see c-88 sitting somewhere with "appealed 9/3, Delta owes an answer by" on it.

**bo-4, $410 courtesy write-off on Lena Fischer, held not dead (5 taps, budget 4, done, one over).** I was on Denials and there was no write-off button; it only lives under ERA and Aging, so the first press was the ERA tab (seq 35-36). Then "Lena Fischer $410.00 open · Crown #19 · MetLife paid; patient portion outstanding since 9/1", Write-off or adjustment, amount prefilled 410.00 with "At or above $150.00 a second approver is needed; the posting is held, never silently allowed.", Courtesy, Post (seq 38-45). The moment I pressed Post two things happened at once: an approval request ar-1 was written (seq 46) and a red-railed line came up reading "Needs a second approver — Dr. Reagan or Dr. Kim" with one button, "Request approval" (seq 47). Post had already turned into an outlined padlock "Held". So the request was in before I pressed Request approval; pressing it (seq 49) wrote nothing new and only added a chip, "Request ar-1 waiting", and the sentence "Dana or Dr. Reagan will see it on their phone; this flips to Posted when they approve." Now the same card told me two different pairs of people, an inch apart. Dana is the office manager; Dr. Kim is a dentist with a patient in the chair. If I walked down the hall on the red line I would have interrupted the wrong doctor. The red line also stayed on the screen with its live Request approval button next to Held, so I had two buttons for one waiting state; pressing Held (seq 51) changed nothing I could see. The Approvals tab did show "Waiting $410.00 Write-off $410.00 on Lena Fischer (courtesy) requested by Sam Dawson at 08:40 ar-1" (seq 53). Held, not dead: yes. But the verb line is ten words and names the wrong people.

**bo-5, "Office Journal" and "walkout statement" through the palette (3 taps by the contract's count, budget 4, done).** Ctrl+K opened a box whose placeholder already said "Type three letters… try walkout statement, day sheet, Office Journal". "office journal" gave one row, "Audit log (sentences) · office journal — called that in Dentrix", and pressing it opened Practice risk (seq 55-75). "walkout" gave "Statement · walkout statement — called that in Dentrix" and took me back to the Money Desk (seq 76-88), but the Money Desk opened on the Approvals tab, where I had last been, not on Statements; I asked for a walkout statement and got the approvals list. Both Dentrix words worked first try, though we said "walkout" in Eaglesoft too and the row credits Dentrix. Then my own words, not counted against the task (seq 89-227): "day sheet" and "deposit slip" came back "Daily Close · called that in Eaglesoft"; "eob" came back "ERA batch"; "adjustment" found "Write-off or adjustment (needs a reason code)"; "ledger" found the Ledger. But "write off" with a space found nothing, and neither did "account", "insurance", "aging", or "claim". The two Eaglesoft words I say most, "account" and "write off", came up empty. The third tap the formula counts is the space between "office" and "journal" (seq 64).

**bo-6, send a held statement with its reason visible (3 taps, budget 3, done).** Statements tab: "Hana Lindqvist $84.00 · held: window deferral · deferred at the window 9/2 so insurance could settle first" (seq 229-230). Send is a filled navy button with the bar glyph; Preview is outlined teal; I could tell which one comes back before I touched either. Preview pressed in and the first line under the buttons was the patient's sentence: "Your share is $84.00 after insurance. We held this statement so your plan could settle first; nothing here is an estimate." (seq 232-234). Send removed Hana's row and the tab count dropped from 2 to 1 (seq 235-238). That is all that changed on the page. "Statement sent · disclosure recorded" went only to a 1 px screen-reader region. On a busy morning a row that disappears looks the same whether I sent it or lost it; I want the word Sent where the row was, the way the appeal left a sentence behind.

**Interview probe, Ines Okoro from the Ledger (seq 239).** Balance $0.00, Patient due $0.00, Waiting on insurance $0.00, three rows: SRP charge $217.00 on 7/14, Delta paid $156.00 on 8/2, MetLife paid $61.00 on 8/20. In one sentence: "Ines, Delta paid $156 and MetLife paid $61 on your $217 deep cleaning, so you owe nothing." I could say that from this screen without a calculator, which I could not do from the Eaglesoft account window.
