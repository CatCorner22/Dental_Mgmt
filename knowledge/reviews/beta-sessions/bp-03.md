# Arrive, seat, and a zero-dollar checkout are faster than Curve, but the shared desk asked me for a PIN it never checked, and the one row in the queue I was sent to had no Checkout button

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Ravi Menon, he/him, 40, twelve years as a patient coordinator; the last office ran Curve.
I am on the shared front-desk machine today, light theme, no access adjustments, tech comfort about a 3 of 5.
I test at the speed of a busy morning: a patient at the window, the phone ringing, no time to read a paragraph before I press something.

## What I did (one short paragraph per task: what happened, taps vs budget, what confused me, quoting the screen)

**fo-1 Arrive and seat Marisol Vega (2 taps, budget 2, completed).** The 9:00 card in Op 1 read "Confirmed · Hygiene · Verify · Re-verify · 1 alert" with one Arrive button. Arrive, and the card flipped to "Arrived" with focus already sitting on Seat; Seat, and the chair strip read "Chair 1 · BL RDH", the card "In chart". Two taps, no appointment window, which is the part of Curve I will not miss. The Re-verify control is what the watch item asked about: the amber "Verify" chip (28 px tall) and the Re-verify button (44 px) sit on one line at 1024 wide, one chip and one control, with "1 alert" wrapping under them. What confused me (seq 3-10): after Arrive and Seat the card still said "Verify · Re-verify" even though the store had the re-run marked as running. If the re-run started when I arrived her, the chip should say so; otherwise I press Re-verify a second time for nothing.

**fo-2 Check out Ines Okoro, $44 by card (5 taps, budget 4, completed, over budget).** Checkout opened from the card in one tap. The money is right. Two more things bothered me. The strip at the top said "$0.00 Patient due · $0.00 Waiting on insurance · $0.00 Credit" over a table telling me to collect $44 (seq 12-14); in Curve the patient portion and the balance were one number, and I had to trust the footnote. And back on the Board (seq 51-52) her card did not say checked out; it said "Filed later · Paid at the window · charges and claim release when Dr. Kim files the note", and she now shows up twice: in a "Filed later" lane and in the Checkout queue with a Ping chair button. In Curve a zero walkout still meant printing a statement nobody wanted; this is the screen I would show a temp first.

### Interview focus (in my words)

**Would you choose this Board over your last system's schedule?** Yes: Arrive and Seat are one tap on the card with the cursor already on the next button, and I never opened an appointment window, which is most of a Curve morning.

**Which gate felt like a dead end?** Two. The second Ping: "Already pinged this chair in the last 15 minutes" with a Why and nothing to press. And the PIN gate on Post, not because it stopped me but because it did not: it took a PIN nobody owns and posted under someone else's name, so I do not know what it was protecting.

**What would a temp need on day one that is missing?** The words and the PIN. "Filed later" has to say "paid, waiting on the doctor's note, nothing for you to do"; the Checkout queue has to hold only people who can be checked out; and the shared desk has to tell the temp who they are posting as before they press Post, because on this machine everything I did today says Priya Raman.
