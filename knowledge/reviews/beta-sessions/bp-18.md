# The surgery codes come first and the referral is one tap away, but on my phone the chart is twice as wide as the screen and the filed note forgot the extraction

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Beatriz, 23, two years out, an oral surgeon working with a hospital and taking referred-in consults; my last office ran Dentrix, so the words in my head are Progress Notes, Treatment Planner, Appointment Book and Ledger, and Spanish is my first language, so I read a screen's words literally.
My tech comfort is 4 of 5; I am not afraid of a new system, I am afraid of a system that makes me pan and hunt while a sedated patient is in the chair.
Today I test on a phone, 420 px wide, light theme, and at the chair I am gloved and often wet: anything under 44 px or closer than 8 px to its neighbour is something I will hit by mistake, so I tapped everything, I did not use the keyboard.

## What I did

**os-1, open the referred-in consult for Aiko Tanaka (1 tap, budget 1, done).** The Exams to sign row already said everything before I opened it: "Aiko Tanaka · Chair 3 · Dr. Okafor · Arrives 10:00 am · Referred by Dr. Serrano: #17 extraction, sedation requested; records forwarded · Open" (seq 1-2). One tap on Open and the encounter landed on `#/surgeon/encounter/enc-9020` with a "Referral" chip and the same sentence at the top: "Referred by Dr. Serrano: #17 extraction, sedation requested; records forwarded" (seq 3). So yes, the referral loop is visible without opening a document, in the list and in the chart. What I noticed at the same moment: the top bar on my phone had wrapped into four rows of buttons, 217 px tall out of 860, and it stays stuck to the top while I scroll, so a quarter of my screen is always Riverbend, Main St, Search, Exams to sign, Board, DC Dr. Okafor, Privacy, Dark, Sign out.

**os-2, chart the extraction #17 with IV sedation and file the sedation note (7 taps, budget 8, done).** The odontogram is 16 teeth across at 44 px each with 8 px gaps, 824 px wide, and my phone is 420 px: untouched, I could see teeth 1-7 and 32-26 only; #17 sat at x=809, off the right edge. When I tapped it the whole canvas panned sideways, not just the tooth strip: the referral card, the "Hygienist tags" heading, the "Chart" heading all slid half off the left edge, and the File button measured x=-77 and 824 px wide (seq 4-6, os-2-charted.png, os-2-readback.png). From then on I was working in a page wider than my hand. The procedure strip was ranked for me: "Surgical extraction D7210" and "IV sedation D9243" first, then the composite, crown and exam (seq 7-8, 13-14), and the announcements were plain: "Charted Extraction, surgical #17. Pending charge $360.00. One transaction: chart, procedure, plan, note scaffold." The task sheet said starter 3 was the sedation starter; on my screen starter 3 is "Fractured cusp" and it wrote "Fractured cusp #17, no pulpal exposure" and "Crown #17; core build-up as needed" into my note (seq 19-22). The sedation starter is first, `enc.note.starter.0`, so I tapped that and it overwrote the text (seq 23-26). File brought the read-back: "Filing as Dr. Chidi Okafor for Aiko Tanaka" (8 words), one "Confirm and file" button, focus already on it, and the File button turned into "Held — confirm the read-back" (seq 27-31). Confirm wrote nf-1, released two charges ($360 #17 and $320 #17), and queued claim c-100 to Cigna (seq 32-36). The driver's own check first flagged this task because it counted all six of Aiko's charges; against the pre-registered criterion it is done. Then I read the filed record. The "Note scaffold" line and the frozen text end with "IV sedation, each 15 min #17" and nowhere name the surgical extraction I charted first; the D7210 transaction card also read "Note scaffold IV sedation, each 15 min #17" (seq 15-17). The second paint overwrote the first in the note. The extraction lives only in the Plan sentence the starter wrote for me. And the sedation "record" is a template sentence: "ASA II; airway and medical history reviewed; sedation consented" and "monitored per protocol", no dose, no start and stop time, no vitals, no escort name, for a code that bills "each 15 min".

**os-3, switch author by PIN on a shared operatory tablet (6 taps, budget 6, NOT completed).** I reopened the encounter with `?device=shared` and the author chip now read "DC DC · DDS, OMS", the initials twice (seq 38). Tapping it opened "Who is charting?" with twelve keys of 72 by 56 px and 8 px between them, focus on 1, and the hint "Enter the other person's PIN. Their session opens on this page; yours is revoked and local drafts are wiped after autosave." Wiped or saved? I read that twice. I entered Dana's 4444 and Go (seq 41-47). The pad closed, the log wrote `sessions:sess-u-om-1` (seq 48), and nothing else happened: the chip still said "DC DC · DDS, OMS", the aria label still said "Who is charting: Dr. Chidi Okafor", the current user was still Dr. Okafor, and the live region still held the old "Filed." sentence (seq 48-49, os-3.png). A session row says Dana took this tablet; the tablet says I did. As a probe afterwards I entered Bree's 1111 the same way: chip "BL BL · RDH", "Now charting as Bree Lawson", and the route became `#/hygienist/encounter/enc-9020` (seq 151-162). So the pad works for a person who has a persona and silently fails for one who does not. A wrong PIN said "No match for that PIN. Six digits at most; three misses lock this device for 5 minutes" (seq 163-173), which is clear.

**os-4, does the Board card read Surgery by shape and word (1 tap, budget 1, done).** Board is in the top bar, one tap (seq 50-52). Aiko's card carries "■ Surgery" with an 8 px red left rail, plus "● Note filed", "● Eligible", "▲ 1 alert", "▬ Referred in". I set grayscale on and the root filter became `grayscale(1)`. Ranking the type chips by darkness: Surgery ink 50, rail 61; Restorative ink 56, rail 85; Exam ink 64, rail 114; Hygiene ink 74, rail 116; all four chip fills sit at 244 to 250, the same pale grey. Surgery is the only square glyph, so my card is unmistakable; but Hygiene and Restorative both use "●", so with the colour gone only the word and a small difference in ink separate them (os-4-grayscale.png). One more thing about a phone Board: 25 appointments in one column, and my 10:00 card was at y=3948, more than four screens down, under a "Before open" strip about eligibility, a shared tablet and a lab case that are not my job.

**os-5, try to file with an empty assessment (2 taps, budget 2, done).** I went back to Exams to sign, opened the first row, Ines Okoro, Dr. Kim's exam (seq 54-56), and tapped File with nothing written (seq 57-58). One row appeared: "Before File · ▲ 1 to fix · Assessment is empty · Add assessment · Why", verb of three words, one button of 174 by 44 px, and File became "Held" with the aria label "Held: fix the rows above, then File" (seq 59-60). The live region said "Assessment is empty. Add assessment". Nothing was filed. Tapping "Add assessment" put my cursor in the Assessment field (seq 61-63). This is what a gate should look like. I only note that the screen let me, the surgeon, stand at Dr. Kim's exam with "Filing as Dr. Chidi Okafor for IO, DOB 06/1985" under the File button; the read-back would have been the only thing between me and signing his note.

**Probes.** Every control on every screen measured at least 44 px (12 controls on Exams, 62 to 68 on the encounter, 99 on the Board, 23 with the pad open) and on a freshly loaded screen no two controls sat closer than 8 px; the focus ring on File is 3 px solid rgb(43,108,184) and matches `:focus-visible` (seq 64-69). The document itself never scrolled sideways, but the canvas inside it did, which is the same thing for my thumb. The search promised "Words from Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and Curve are translated"; "ledger" found "Ledger (three numbers and Explain) · called that in all", but "progress note", "treatment plan" and "appointment book" each got "Nothing matches" (seq 70-133). "extraccion" got nothing either, which I expected.
