# Six taps from the referral to a filed sedation note, but the office manager's PIN was swallowed without a word and my sedation got charted on a tooth

## Who I am today (3 lines, from the card, in first person)
I am Devin, 50, eighteen years an oral and maxillofacial surgeon; my last office ran Open Dental, so I still say "procedure code" and "note" and I do not have patience for a screen that redraws itself while I am reading it.
I have ADHD: every time the screen changes under me, a modal grabs my focus, or the page drops me back at the top, I lose the object I was working on, so I recorded where my focus landed after every tap.
At the chair I work gloved and often wet, so I tapped everything (no keyboard for the flows) and I measured every control for 44 px and every neighbour for 8 px; anything smaller I will hit by mistake. Dark theme, reduced motion, operatory profile at 1024 px. The prototype's seed knows me as "Dr. Chidi Okafor, DDS, OMS" (u-dr-3); I answered to it.

## What I did

**os-1, open Aiko Tanaka's referred-in consult (1 tap, budget 1, done).** Exams to sign read "4 waiting · ordered by time in chair" and my consult was the last row, correctly, because she is not in a chair yet: "Aiko Tanaka · Chair 3 · Dr. Okafor · Arrives 10:00 am · Referred by Dr. Serrano: #17 extraction, sedation requested; records forwarded · Open". So the referral loop was visible before I opened anything. One tap on Open (seq 2) and the route was surgeon/encounter/enc-9020 (seq 3). Under the header "Aiko Tanaka · DOS 9/3/2026 · Dr. Okafor · Chair 3 · Surgery · DOB 12/5/1982" the first card was "▬ Referral · Referred by Dr. Serrano: #17 extraction, sedation requested; records forwarded" (os-1.png). No documents to open, which is what I wanted. The one thing I did not like: after the route change my focus sat on the page body, not on the referral card or the first tooth.

**os-2, chart the surgical extraction #17 with IV sedation and File (6 taps, budget 8, done).** The procedure strip came ranked for me: "Surgical extraction D7210 · IV sedation D9243 · Composite 2-surf D2392 · Crown D2740 · Periodic exam D0120", and the first starter was "Sedation: ASA II, IV midazolam, monitored per protocol". Tooth 17 (seq 9) gave "Selected: #17". D7210 (seq 12) wrote ce-1, pr-500, pl-1 in the same instant (seq 13-15) and the card said "One paint, one transaction · Chart event Extraction, surgical #17 · ce-1 · Dr. Okafor · Procedure D7210 · $360.00 pending charge, released at File · Note scaffold Extraction, surgical #17". D9243 (seq 18) wrote ce-2, pr-501, pl-2 (seq 19-21) as "IV sedation, each 15 min #17". That is my first problem: sedation is not a tooth procedure, and when I later tried D9243 on a fresh copy before picking a tooth the screen refused me with "Pick a tooth before the procedure · Go to teeth" (probe seq 2-5, probe-sedation-no-tooth.png). My second problem: after the second paint, the extraction's own card now read "Note scaffold IV sedation, each 15 min #17"; the scaffold line remembers only the last paint. The sedation starter (seq 24) filled Assessment with "Referred for surgical extraction #17; ASA II; airway and medical history reviewed; sedation consented." and Plan with "Surgical extraction #17 under IV sedation (midazolam), monitored per protocol; postoperative instructions given to patient and escort." and put my cursor at the end of Assessment. It reads as a record I would edit, not a template with blanks, and it named the tooth from my chart. File (seq 28) gave the read-back "Filing as Dr. Chidi Okafor for Aiko Tanaka" (8 words, refusal readback, seq 29) with "Confirm and file" already focused; Confirm (seq 32) wrote nf-1, le-5000 ($360), le-5001 ($320) and claim c-100 to Cigna (seq 33-36). The filed card read "Filed · ruleset 2.25.2 · byteaudit verified · By Dr. Chidi Okafor at 2026-09-03 08:40 · nf-1 · text and version frozen; corrections are addenda, never edits · Charges released: 2 · $360.00 #17, $320.00 #17 · Claim queued · c-100 to Cigna" (os-2.png). But the frozen text's procedure line is only "IV sedation, each 15 min #17": the extraction I did is in the chart events and the ledger, and in my plan sentence, but not in the chart-derived line of the note. My own check script first marked this task failed because it counted the seed's older ledger rows for the patient too; the two released charges are le-5000 and le-5001 and the criterion is met.

**os-3, switch author by PIN on the shared tablet (12 taps across two attempts, budget 6; done on the second attempt).** I set the device to shared (seq 37) and tapped the author chip, which reads "DC DC · DDS, OMS", initials twice (seq 39). The pad said "Who is charting? · Enter the other person's PIN. Their session opens on this page; yours is revoked and local drafts are wiped after autosave." Focus opened on key 1. Keys are 72 × 56 with exactly 8 px between them, Cancel is 608 × 44 (os-3-pad.png). I entered Dana's 4444 (seq 42-45, dots "••••") and Go (seq 47). The prototype wrote sessions sess-u-om-1 (seq 48), closed the pad, put my focus back on the author chip (seq 49), and nothing else happened: the chip still said "DC · DDS, OMS", the route stayed put, the live region was empty (os-3-after-dana.png). A session row says Dana signed in on this tablet; the screen says I am still charting. No "no match", no "switched", nothing. I tried again with Bree's 1111 (seq 50-57): sessions sess-u-hy-1 (seq 58), chip "BL · RDH", route hygienist/encounter/enc-9020, same encounter (seq 60), and my focus dropped to the page body (os-3-after-bree.png). The read-back line was the last gate in os-2, so a stale author would have been caught there; but the PIN pad itself accepted a PIN and told me nothing.

**os-4, the Board card reads Surgery by shape and word (1 tap, budget 1, done).** Back on my own Exams route I tapped Board (seq 63, route seq 64). The 10:00 card "10:00 am · Aiko Tanaka · ● Note filed · ■ Surgery · ● Eligible · ▲ 1 alert · ▬ Referred in", so the card carries the square glyph and the word. In grayscale (data-grayscale=1, filter grayscale(1); os-4-gray-row.png, os-4-card-gray.png) I ranked the 10:00 row: Hank Haddad "● Restorative", Kwame Serrano "● Restorative", Aiko Tanaka "■ Surgery". Surgery is the only square, so I can pick it out. What I cannot do in grayscale is tell Hygiene from Restorative by shape: both are "●", and in the dark theme all four type fills are within a hair of each other (background luminance Hygiene .022, Restorative .017, Exam .022, Surgery .015), so the word does all the work for those two.

**os-5, try to file with an empty assessment (2 taps, budget 2, done; 2 setup taps not counted).** Ines Okoro was next on Exams to sign, so I opened her (seq 66, 69; route seq 70). The gate under the empty note read "The killer strip appears after you leave a field: at most three rows, each with one fix. · File · Filing as Dr. Chidi Okafor for IO, DOB 06/1985". File (seq 72) gave "Before File · ▲ 1 to fix · Assessment is empty · Add assessment · Why" (refusal assessment_required, seq 73) and File became the outlined lock "Held" (os-5.png). One verb line of three words, one control. My focus stayed on the Held button, which is where my thumb was. "Add assessment" (seq 76) put my cursor in the Assessment field (seq 77). Nothing was filed for enc-9003.

**My gloved, dark-theme, lose-my-place checks (Devin-specific).** Sizes: 63 controls on the fresh encounter, 69 at read-back, 65 with the killer strip up, 100 on the Board, 13 on Exams; none under 44 × 44 by getBoundingClientRect; PIN keys 72 × 56. Gaps: my first pass flagged 7.5-7.8 px between the temporality buttons, between Arrive and Details on every Board card, and between Search and Exams to sign, but that was rounding in my own probe; measured precisely, Arrive right edge 135.56 to Details left edge 143.56 is 8.00 px and the flex gap is 8px throughout, so no gap defect. The only sub-8 px pairs left are content scrolled under the sticky top bar, a different layer. No horizontal overflow (document 1024/1024, canvas 1024/1024). The sticky top bar wraps to two rows, 113 px, and never scrolls away. Dark-theme contrast: body and refusal verb 13.7:1, refusal control 14.3:1, Held 14.5:1, reversible buttons 11.2:1, teeth 14.5:1, chips 9.0-11.4:1 at 13 px, small muted text 10.4:1, the Why link 8.1:1 at the low end; focus ring #8FC1F5 on #172033, 3 px solid with a 2 px surface halo, and :focus-visible true on every Tab stop I walked (surfaces M O D B L, then D7210). Motion: --motion resolves to 0ms and button transitions read 0s with the reduced flag. Where my focus went: after Open it was BODY (seq 3); after each paint it stayed on the button I pressed (seq 10, 16, 22), which I appreciated; after the starter it moved into Assessment on purpose (seq 26); after File it moved to Confirm (seq 31); after Confirm it was BODY (seq 36); after the Bree switch, BODY (seq 60); after Board, Exams and Open, BODY (seq 64, 67, 70). Five times in one visit I had to find my place again from the top bar.
