## Patterns

- PATTERN: Vendor complaints converge on the same five themes in both dental PMS and GRC software — quote-only pricing, renewal creep (8–12%/yr Dentrix/Eaglesoft; 40–100% Vanta; 20–40%+ Drata), support degradation after growth or a strategic pivot, lock-in via data formats or integrations, and add-on or per-use fees that erode the headline price. (seen: dental PMS across 10 platforms; GRC across Vanta/Drata; sources/revup-dental-pms-reviews-2026.md, compliance-automation-complaints-2026.md)
- PATTERN: "Same headline, different bill" — the billing unit (per location, per user, per operatory), the number of included providers, and the year-two rate change the total more than the monthly figure does. (seen: Archy $899 unlimited vs Oryx $899 for two; Open Dental $149 base -> $389–$446 with modules; sources/medix-dental-pms-cost-2026.md)
- PATTERN: Cloud-only platforms convert reliability into a business-continuity risk with no local fallback; 2025 Curve outages (6+ hours), Denticon downtime, and Open Dental Cloud freezing all surfaced as top complaints. (seen: 3 platforms; sources/revup-dental-pms-reviews-2026.md)
- PATTERN: Ledger design is an internal-control variable — allocation logic (Open Dental), auto-generated transfer adjustments (CareStack), invoice-based billing (Curve), and AR that includes estimated write-offs (Oryx) all confuse billing staff and CPAs, which weakens owner oversight. (seen: 4 platforms; sources/revup-dental-pms-reviews-2026.md)
- PATTERN: "Early cloud love, then reliability and support decline after growth or a strategic pivot" — Curve (2025 outages after PE-era growth) and tab32 (support collapse after AI pivot with per-use fees) follow the same arc, so 2022-23 five-star reviews are stale evidence for a 2026 decision. (seen: 2 platforms; sources/grok-03-well-vs-poorly-2026.md, revup-dental-pms-reviews-2026.md)
- PATTERN: The interactive risk-advisor product shape (question the owner → score → tailored policies/controls → track remediation → produce evidence for a third party) recurs in three adjacent SMB markets with a reference price band of $39–$115/month product-led, $499–$1,200/year self-service-plus-advisor, $3,000+/year coach-led, and $7,500–$10,000+/year for multi-framework cyber GRC. (seen: 3 markets; sources/hipaa-compliance-platforms-small-practice-2026.md, smarterrisk-and-insurable-risk-platforms.md, compliance-automation-pricing-2026.md)

## Lessons

- LESSON: Always identify the unit behind a vendor count — dentists, practices, locations, users, or professionals — before ranking; converting between them silently is the most common error in this category. (from: Medix's four tests; the Eaglesoft "users" vs "practices" and Curve "professionals" ambiguities)
- LESSON: Read the vendor's live fee page rather than a comparison site when a number matters; Medix (published one day earlier) already differed from Open Dental's page on two line items. (from: open-dental-fee-schedule vs medix-dental-pms-cost-2026)
- LESSON: Reddit is blocked from direct retrieval in this research environment; use aggregators that quote threads (RevUp Dental, PracticeSignal) and label everything from them as anecdotal. (from: SITE_BLOCKED error on reddit.com, 2026-09-02)
- LESSON: Paid analyst reports (FMI, SNS, MRFR, Coherent) publish confident percentages with no methodology and list defunct vendors; treat them as models or marketing, never as measurements. (from: future-market-insights-and-analyst-reports)
- LESSON: A PMS audit trail is a necessary but insufficient control; the independent control is daily reconciliation of ledger to bank performed outside the PMS. (from: dental-embezzlement-ada-prosperident 17%/83% finding; zeldent-dental-fraud-detection)
- LESSON: When a source reports a "conflict" about what a pricing page includes, read the page's column structure yourself; Grok's Curve-imaging conflict came from reading a two-column (Hero / SuperHero) table as one list. (from: grok-01 vs my 2026-09-02 fetch of curvedental.com/pricing)
- LESSON: When two independent readers disagree with my own fetch on a vendor's published number, treat the page as possibly serving variants and mark the line unresolved rather than picking a winner. (from: Open Dental training/AI/ODMobile lines, 2026-09-02)
- LESSON: A second research agent working from the same public web finds the same anchors (Medix, RevUp, PracticeSignal); the marginal value comes from the sources it reaches that I could not (Dentaltown vote, ADA PDFs, Software Advice primary reviews, Patterson page) — so reconcile at the claim level, not the conclusion level. (from: Grok package reconciliation, 2026-09-02)

## Methods

- METHOD: Current working method for pricing any quote-only software vendor is: (1) fetch the vendor's own pricing and FAQ pages; (2) capture third-party ranges with dates and sources; (3) record the billing unit, included seats, year-two rate, conversion/implementation fees, and exit/export terms as separate fields; (4) label each figure Verified / Vendor-reported / Third-party / Anecdotal. (updated 2026-09-02)
- METHOD: Current working method for ranking installed base with no measured data is: rank by vendor-reported counts adjusted for scope, corroborate with independent IT-firm consensus, assign confidence tiers (ranks 1–3 high; 4–6 moderate; 7+ low and unordered), and state the inference used for any unit conversion. (updated 2026-09-02)
