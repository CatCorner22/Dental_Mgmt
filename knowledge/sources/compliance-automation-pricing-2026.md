## Summary

Pricing evidence for the cyber-compliance GRC category. No vendor publishes a rate; procurement datasets and partner estimates are the only benchmarks.

## Key concepts

- None of Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Sprinto, Thoropass publishes a price (checked Aug 2026); all route to demo/quote.
- Vendr through Aug 2026: Drata Advanced median $20,300 (range $16,600-$39,700, N=94); Sprinto Advanced $19,600 (N=6); Anecdotes $33,000 (N=17); Vanta Professional $65,600 at 500-employee baseline (range $6,900-$109,600, N=106); Secureframe similar purchases $8,000-$34,000.
- Entry tiers (partner estimates): Drata Foundation $7,500-$15,000; Secureframe Fundamentals $7,500-$20,000; Vanta Core ~$10,000; Sprinto/Scrut/ComplyJet often <$7,500 for <50 employees; Thoropass $8,700-$80,000.
- Median annual spend: Vanta $19,800 (315 purchases); Drata average contract ~$13,500; per-additional-framework ~$5,000 (Vanta) vs ~$1,500 (Drata).
- Four separate line items: platform, auditor ($15K-$50K, separate CPA firm), pen test, implementation; only Thoropass bundles the auditor.
- Levers: multi-year 10-20%, quarter-end, framework bundling, certified-partner pricing 15-25% off list.
- Vanta scale: $300M+ ARR, 16,000+ organizations (Apr 2026 release).
