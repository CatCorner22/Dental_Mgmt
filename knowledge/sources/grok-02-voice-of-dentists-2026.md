## Summary

Grok's demand-side synthesis: ADA HPI economic surveys, the Dental Economics-Levin survey, the Dentaltown Townie Choice 2025 vote, KLAS and Forrester on CareStack, an Alta Voice hygiene survey, Software Advice primary reviews for Open Dental, and aggregator-sourced Reddit quotes. Ends with ten must-haves, persona preferences, a switching table, a 12-item gap list, and an honest list of what could not be found.

## Key concepts

- ADA HPI Q1 2025 (791 responses): 18.1% of owners invested in new software; solo 16.8% vs 2-9 dentists 20.5%; age 35-44 highest at 28.0%. Q4 2025 (751 respondents): insurance 55.3%, staffing 54.2%, overhead 41.5% top challenges; 16.9% likely to invest in software in 2026 vs 42.3% adding staff; DSO 29.0% vs non-DSO 16.3%; 23.3% invested in 2025 vs 20.3% planned.
- Levin 2025 (n not published; 87% owners; 52% solo; avg age 55): reimbursements 56%, overhead 55%, clinical hiring 53%, admin hiring 22%; 43% using AI; 23% adding technology for productivity; production per doctor ~$1.0M flat.
- Townie Choice 2025 (358 votes): Dentrix 25%, Open Dental 20%, Eaglesoft 15%, Ascend 5%, Curve 4%, SoftDent 2%, ABELDent 2%, Oryx/PracticeWorks/DentiMax/Denticon/XLDent 1% each, tab32 0%, 'do not use' 10%. Independent-GP electorate; on-prem big three = 60% of named votes.
- KLAS CareStack First Look (Mar 2024): n=8 from 7 orgs; overall 93.0 (limited); 100% would buy again (n=7); portal tickets slow; doctors want imaging AI.
- Forrester TEI CareStack (Oct 2023, Straumann-commissioned): 52 multi-site decision-makers; 10-location composite; 307% ROI; payback <6 months.
- Alta Voice (Jul 2026, vendor, n undisclosed): dentists 95% vs hygienists 52% 'enough time'; 72% vs 29% 'very confident'; 60% skip full-mouth perio when behind; 11% chart it every visit.
- Ten recurring themes (insurance is the daily test; support windows; cost opacity and add-ons; data exit; staff familiarity; cloud love/hate; ledger readability; perio bottleneck; DSO consolidation; AI adopted but bolted on).
- Ten must-haves ranked by evidence weight; persona splits (solo GP, multi-location, DSO, specialists, OM/biller vs dentist vs hygienist); documented switching paths (Dentrix->OD, Dentrix->CareStack, Dentrix Enterprise->Denticon, Eaglesoft->Curve, Curve->Denticon, Curve->back, DOX->Denticon, OD->tab32, Ascend->Dentrix, tab32->out).
- Switching-cost figures disagree by an order of magnitude ($800-$2,400 PracticeSignal vs $5k-$50k Dental Practice Insider); imaging and in-flight claims are what break.
- Could not find: a national brand-usage survey; G2 grid; live Reddit; Alta Voice n; the '61% price and integration' and 'more than half now cloud per ADA' claims — do not cite those two.
