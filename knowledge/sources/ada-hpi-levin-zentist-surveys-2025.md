## Summary

The only probability-style demand-side data found for dental practices. Insurance and staffing lead every list; software is a minority capital decision; eligibility verification is the named daily burden. None of these surveys asks which PMS brand a practice uses.

## Key concepts

- ADA HPI Q4 2025 (751 respondents, per Becker's): top-3 challenges insurance/reimbursement 55.3%, staffing 54.2%, overhead 41.5%, patient volume 31.8%; new technology and equipment updates only 4.5%; administrative time 0.8%.
- ADA HPI Q4 2025 plans (via Grok): add staff 42.3%; drop insurance networks 35.0%; major equipment 24.4%; invest in new software 16.9%; DSO-affiliated 29.0% vs non-DSO 16.3% on software.
- ADA HPI Q1 2025 (791 responses; 738 private practice; via Grok): 18.1% of owners invested in new software early 2025; solo 16.8% vs 2-9 dentists 20.5%; age 35-44 at 28.0%.
- Dental Economics-Levin 2025 (n unpublished; published May 5, 2026): 55% cite rising overhead as the biggest challenge (verified); Grok adds reimbursements 56%, clinical hiring 53%, 43% using AI, 92% expect same or better in 2026.
- Zentist 2026 RCM Trends (vendor survey, n not in release): 71% call real-time insurance verification the primary daily challenge; 78% report rising denials; 63% net collection >=90% sustained by manual labor; 58% committing to automation.

## Notable quotes and data

- 'More than half of respondents said low insurance reimbursements and staffing… were among their top challenges' — Becker's Dental on ADA HPI Q4 2025.
- ADA HPI earlier poll (1,000+ dentists, six-month horizon): staffing 66.3%, overhead 40.2%, insurance 39.1% — a different question and horizon; do not mix with Q4 2025 figures.
