## Summary

Vendor-by-vendor check of published dental PMS rate cards as of August 2026, with the cost lines that sit outside the subscription. Argues the billing unit, year-13 rate, conversion fees, and infrastructure decide the real bill, not the headline monthly price.

## Key concepts

- Published headline rates run $129-$1,399/month; Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend, Eaglesoft, and Curve publish nothing; Denticon's 'starts at $795' and Oryx's conversion fees hide in FAQs.
- Same headline, different bill: Archy $899 covers unlimited providers; Oryx Automate $899 covers two, then $100 each.
- Open Dental publishes the most detail: $199 first 12 months then $149; $1,450 typical conversion; $400 per additional clinic; $850 document and $900 x-ray conversion; insurance benefits do not convert.
- Practice-Web's pricing page and homepage disagree ($179->$129 vs $149->$99).
- Server platforms carry Windows Server ($1,176 list), CALs, hardware, UPS, and a 5-7-year refresh; cloud removes hardware but not backup, export, and vendor-risk governance.
- Open Dental volume rate $169 (4-9 offices) -> $149; prepay 5/10/15%; lapsed support forfeits earned discounts (bites in acquisitions).
- Enterprise agreements (Oryx Enterprise, CareStack per locations/chairs/providers) replace published rates for groups.
- Open Dental changed license from GPL to proprietary in v24.4 (per its license page); 'free PMS' claims are outdated.

## Notable quotes and data

- Open Dental raised US rate from $179 to $199 on 2026-02-02; offices that started at $179 keep it for their first 12 months.
- Medix lists Open Dental on-site training $4,325/$1,325 and AI Image Analysis $149 — the live fee page shows $3,650/$1,200 and $199 (see open-dental-fee-schedule).
- PracticeSignal-style '$500-$900' band for enterprise platforms rests partly on vendors that never confirmed a number.
