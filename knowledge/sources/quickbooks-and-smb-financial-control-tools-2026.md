## Summary

Second targeted search round on financial internal-control tooling for small businesses. Confirms the pattern: accounting systems supply audit trails, permissions, exception reports, and now AI anomaly flags; CPA firms supply the same checklist and sell the tailoring as a service; no interactive SMB product recommends and tailors financial controls.

## Key concepts

- Intuit (verified): Finance AI continuously monitors KPIs and flags anomalies with a suggested next step; Intuit Intelligence links accounting, payments, payroll, bill pay, tax; KPI scorecards on Essentials/Plus. Positive Pay is not native to QuickBooks Online (Intuit community).
- QuickBooks as a fraud tool (415 Group / SD Mayer): audit trail with date, user, change type; AR/AP aging for anomalies; exception reports (duplicates, voids, unusual vendors); bank reconciliation; predefined and custom user roles.
- CPA Journal (Aug 2026): FRM Guide 2023's five principles align to COSO's five components; management-override controls (top-side adjustments, biased estimates) are required; data analytics enhance fraud risk assessment.
- Every 2026 SMB guide retrieved repeats the same controls — segregation of duties, dual approval thresholds, owner review of bank notifications weekly and reconciliations monthly, mandatory vacations, vendor-change verification, MFA — then offers a paid controls assessment.
- Price bands (secondary, Beancount citing market): software-only anomaly detection and reconciliation $20-$70/month for very small businesses; AI-enhanced bookkeeping $200-$550/month; B2B vendor-fraud platforms higher with entry plans emerging; Trustpair 2026: 71% of US companies saw more AI-driven fraud attempts.
- One anecdotal data point: a 2026 QuickBooks AI review cites a 1.1/5 Trustpilot score for support and steep post-promo renewals.
