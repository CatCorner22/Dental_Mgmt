## Summary

The authoritative, vendor-neutral resources for designing a tailored fraud-risk and internal-control program for an entity of any size. They are documents and templates, not interactive software.

## Key concepts

- COSO ICIF 1992, revised 2013 with 17 principles; Principle 8 requires considering fraud risk when assessing risks to objectives.
- COSO/ACFE Fraud Risk Management Guide, 2nd edition (2023): explains how ICIF 2013, ERM 2017, and fraud risk management fit together; expanded data-analytics guidance; states there is no one-size-fits-all program and any organization can build a custom-fitted one.
- ACFE Fraud Risk Tools site: anti-fraud program self-assessment, expanded fraud-exposure and scheme library with hyperlinked descriptions, data-analytics tests, documentation templates; executive summary for boards.
- Feb 2026: COSO published audit-ready guidance for governing generative AI — eight capability types, minimum control expectations per COSO component, starter templates (risk matrices, control tests, metric dashboards).
- MindBridge's COSO-aligned checklist maps the five components: control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information and communication, monitoring.
