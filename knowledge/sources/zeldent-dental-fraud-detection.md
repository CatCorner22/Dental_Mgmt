## Summary

A dental-specific control platform: automated bank-to-PMS reconciliation plus anomaly alerts. The clearest example of vertical internal-control software for small practices; pricing not published.

## Key concepts

- Thesis: the PMS is a system of record that anyone with access can manipulate; the bank is the only independent ground truth; daily ledger-to-deposit comparison outside the PMS is what catches skimming, unposted payments, and covering adjustments.
- Features claimed: continuous scanning of payments, deposits, entries; alerts for refunds above threshold, after-hours transactions, retroactive edits; automatic deposit matching; HIPAA-oriented audit logs and least privilege; integrations with all major PMS and custom ones.
- ROI calculator assumptions per $1M revenue: ~$30K (dialed-in), ~$75K (some gaps), ~$135K (needs overhaul) recoverable.
- Cited stats: 60-70% of practices affected; ~$100K average loss; median scheme 18 months; ACFE discovery mix ~40% tips, ~15% internal audit, ~12% management review; average embezzler tenure 8 years (Hiscox).
- Blog covers PMS-specific reconciliation walkthroughs (Curve, Dentrix Ascend) and payer-specific (Delta, MetLife).

## Notable quotes and data
