## Summary

Third-party accounts of Patterson moving Eaglesoft from perpetual licenses to subscription, plus ecosystem lock-in details. Patterson has not published a timeline.

## Key concepts

- New Eaglesoft customers subscription-only since 2024; installed base migrating at renewal through 2026 (Ainora); Patterson reported 27% subscription growth and 11% perpetual decline in fiscal 2024 (Molar Report citing Mordor).
- Legacy model: one-time license + monthly Service Club (support, updates); on-premise only; pricing quote-based via Patterson branch.
- Global Payments is the only natively integrated processor; DentalTown threads report 0.80-0.90% above interchange (~$400-$500/month on $50K card volume).
- Eaglesoft stores imaging in proprietary formats; migration off Eaglesoft is hardest on imaging.
- PracticeSignal (220 practitioner reviews): neither Dentrix nor Eaglesoft is recommended for a practice starting fresh in 2026; choose either only if already invested in the hardware ecosystem.

## Notable quotes and data

- 'Starting in 2026, new customers are subscription-only; existing perpetual license holders face migration during their renewal cycles.' — Ainora.
