## Summary

Two Curve releases that supply the first practice-location count for Curve and the CEO's market framing. The Aug 27 release resolves the earlier inference from '80,000+ professionals' to an actual location count.

## Key concepts

- Aug 27, 2026: Curve 'supports more than 6,000 dental practice locations across North America' and is approaching $100M ARR, almost entirely organic; Luke Anderson promoted to Chief Product Officer.
- Aug 20, 2026: further $200M R&D commitment to Curve Hero and ecosystem; 'around 20% of the cloud dental practice market'; profitable; CEO Dave Cormack (ex-Brightree leadership) predicts a major shift and, per Grok's read, >40% of PMS vendors gone in 36-48 months; 85% of ~160,000 NA practices still on client-server systems.
- All figures are vendor-reported and unaudited; the 20% figure has an undefined denominator; the 6,000+ figure counts locations, not organizations.
