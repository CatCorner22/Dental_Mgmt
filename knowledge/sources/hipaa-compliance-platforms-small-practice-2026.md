## Summary

The risk-management category a dental practice actually buys: guided questionnaires that generate practice-specific policies and remediation, training tracking, BAA management, and audit defense. This is the closest existing match to an interactive tool that recommends and tailors controls — for regulatory controls rather than financial ones.

## Key concepts

- Three product kinds (Patient Protect taxonomy): healthcare compliance platforms (Abyde, AccountableHQ, Patient Protect, Medcurity), service-led providers with coaching (Compliancy Group, Total HIPAA), multi-framework GRC (Vanta, Drata, Sprinto) that over-serves 1-25-person practices.
- Mechanics (CDA on Abyde; Venn): guided risk analysis with tailored questions; one-click policy generation kept current with federal and state rules; training with certificates; document storage; expert support during complaints, breaches, audits; 'customized policy generation based on your practice's specific circumstances'.
- Two distinctions that matter: recorded vs enforced controls (BAA-gated messaging); documentation-first vs active prevention (breach simulation, audit logging).
- Pricing: Patient Protect $39/mo Basic, $99/mo Pro, no contracts (verified on vendor page); Abyde starting $115/mo (Capterra, 1 review) with in-browser computed quote; AccountableHQ 'starts at $99/mo' (own page) vs $149-$249/mo (Medcurity); Compliancy Group 'typically $3,000+/yr, custom quotes' (competitor-reported); Medcurity $499-$1,200/yr (own page); HIPAA One $4,000+/yr (competitor-reported).
- Typical dental office has 8-15 business associates; a missing BAA is a §164.502(e) violation before any breach (Patient Protect); IBM 2026: average healthcare breach $6.64M.
- CDA endorsed Abyde as a member benefit (Jan 2026); CDA cites $114M in HIPAA fines since 2016 and 98% of OCR-audited providers failing Notice of Privacy Practices content.
- Complaints are mostly competitor-authored: consultant-dependent onboarding and annual contracts (aimed at Compliancy Group); per-employee pricing and enterprise breadth (AccountableHQ); templates are not a program (Total HIPAA); AI assistants routing PHI to third-party LLMs (raised by Patient Protect).
