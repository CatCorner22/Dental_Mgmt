## Summary

The one general-purpose small-business 'risk advisor' found: SmarterRisk questions the owner for ~15 minutes, returns a risk score and prioritized improvement plan, builds safety policies, and produces an insurance-ready report, at $500/year. Also the insurer-linked cyber pattern (Coalition Control) and the inspection-app pattern (SafetyCulture/iAuditor, Safesite, KPA).

## Key concepts

- SmarterRisk: RISK-B AI assistant; 10-15 minute assessment (20-30 for multi-location); outputs risk score, risk improvement plan (why, how, resources per item), comprehensive report for agent/carrier; policy builder with 20+ programs aligned to OSHA/NFPA/ANSI/ISO; 50+ courses; 150+ forms; photo-verified tracking updates the score; $500/yr Intelligent Plan; one-year term if paying monthly; industries listed do not name dental.
- SmarterRisk's market framing: enterprise RMIS/GRC (Origami, LogicManager, NAVEX, Riskonnect) $50K-$200K+ with 6-12-month implementations; inspection apps document hazards but do not assess organizational risk; carrier risk-control visits about every five years with no implementation help; safety consultants $2,500-$5,000 for policies alone.
- Insurance economics used as the value case: workers' comp $20K-$100K+/yr; EMR effects persist three years; a $50K claim can add $15K-$25K/yr in premium.
- Coalition: free external cyber risk assessment; Coalition Control AI-powered risk-management platform tied to active cyber insurance; Allianz global partnership.
- Mitti (SafetyCulture): risk register, inspections, corrective actions — an operations platform, not an advisor.
