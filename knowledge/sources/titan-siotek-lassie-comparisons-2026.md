## Summary

Supporting third-party views used to corroborate the big-three consensus, infrastructure demands, and integration ecosystems.

## Key concepts

- Siotek (supports Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Ascend in 30+ states): Dentrix most widely used; Eaglesoft closest competitor; Open Dental fastest-growing, runs on MySQL across Windows/Mac/Linux; Eaglesoft licensing estimate $400-$900/mo; Dentrix stores images in a separate image path outside the database.
- Lassie: Dentrix 35,000+ and Eaglesoft 30,000 practices (unsourced); Open Dental $199 with anytime export; Lassie's own posting add-on costs $3,000/location setup + 2% posting fee.
- Titan: quotes Reddit/DentalTown endorsements for Open Dental, CareStack (Mac imaging acquisition limitation), tab32 for specialties.
- Mordor: Jarvis Analytics acquisition (Mar 2024) embeds predictive models in Dentrix; Patterson-Weave partnership Jan 2025 bundles comms into Eaglesoft; Open Dental marketplace doubled integrations in eight months; SaaS 60.53% of 2025 revenue (modeled).
- Mentera/First Stop Dental: practices moving Dentrix->Open Dental report 40-60% software savings (anecdotal); Eaglesoft integrations via Patterson Innovation Connection.
