## Summary

The largest vendor-side security event affecting the dental PMS ecosystem, relevant to vendor-concentration risk for practices that depend on HS1 for software, eServices, and supplies.

## Key concepts

- Incident discovered 2023-10-14; systems including e-commerce taken offline; BlackCat claimed 35 TB exfiltrated; re-encryption after failed negotiations; US e-commerce restored after six days in late November.
- Possible exposure of customer bank account and card data; supplier bank details misused; notification to 29,112 people in one state filing.
- Business-interruption estimate $0.55-$0.75 per share; FY2023 sales guided 1-3% below 2022.
- Historical: FTC settled with Henry Schein for $250,000 over misrepresenting Dentrix G5 encryption (Wikipedia).

## Notable quotes and data
