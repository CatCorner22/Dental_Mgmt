## Summary

Planet DDS's self-reported footprint across boilerplates and the February 2026 Synchrony partnership release. Anchors Denticon's rank near the top of the cloud tier.

## Key concepts

- Boilerplate progression: 10,000 practices / 60,000 users (Jan 2023) -> 13,000 / 118,000 (2024) -> 14,500 / 175,000 (end of 2025).
- Synchrony release (Feb 2026): 15,000+ Denticon dental practices and 2,500+ Cloud 9 orthodontic practices; CareCredit becomes preferred financing across Planet DDS.
- Planet DDS claims more 100+-location DSOs than any other cloud PMS; Coast Dental (88 locations) selected Denticon; Dental365/PCDM scaled from 2 to 110 locations on Denticon.
- Launchpad program: groups with 2+ locations, $3M-$25M revenue, not PE-backed.
- Planet DDS sunset Apteryx on-premise (per Open Dental forum thread), ending 'perpetual' imaging licenses.

## Notable quotes and data

- 'serving over 13,000 practices in the United States and having over 118,000 users' (2024 boilerplate); '14,500 practices and 175,000 users' (2025 close).
