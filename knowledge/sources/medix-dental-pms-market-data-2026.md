## Summary

A methodological audit of dental PMS market-share claims. Finds that no measured share data exists; the circulating Dentrix/Eaglesoft/Open Dental percentages trace to SEO content. Separates measured ADA data, vendor-reported counts, and invented figures, and verifies current ownership of every major platform.

## Key concepts

- No neutral body measures dental PMS adoption; ADA HPI tracks practice modality and DSO affiliation, not software.
- Circulating shares (Dentrix 18-22%, Eaglesoft 15-20%, Open Dental 14-18%) have no source; the 82% three-vendor claim is a decade old.
- Technographic vendors infer software from web signals and report Open Dental counts in the hundreds; unusable for server-based installs.
- Vendor counts are real claims but differently scoped: HS1 48,000 US practices (Dentrix+Ascend) vs 100,000+ global locations (all products).
- Overlapping 'top DSO' claims are not contradictory because large DSOs run several platforms.
- No credible cloud-vs-server split exists; '>80% cloud' traces to a self-contradicting vendor page.
- Ownership verified: HS1 (Dentrix family); Patterson private via Patient Square Capital (closed 2025-04-17); Planet DDS (Denticon, Cloud 9, Apteryx); Carestream Dental software recapitalized Sept 2024 by GA Credit-led group; CareStack independent with Straumann minority (2022); Open Dental independent; Oryx independent; tab32 independent (Series B 2021).
- Four tests for any dental statistic: who collected it and how; dentists vs practices vs locations; commercial interest; traceable to a primary document.

## Notable quotes and data

- ADA HPI 2024: 16.1% of US dentists DSO-affiliated; 26.5% of dentists <=10 years out; 9.1% >25 years out; database covers 198,117 of 212,459 dentists.
- HPI Survey of Dental Practice: 58,568 sampled, 1,208 responses, 2.1% response rate (separate dataset from the near-census office database).
- Envista's 2022 Carestream deal covered scanners (now DEXIS), not the PMS software.
