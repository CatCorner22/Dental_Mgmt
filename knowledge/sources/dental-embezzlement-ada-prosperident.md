## Summary

The evidence base linking dental PMS to internal-control failure: victimization rose from 35% to 48% across ADA surveys, and only 17% of thefts were caught by the practice's designed controls.

## Key concepts

- ADA surveys: 35% victimized (2008 survey) -> 48% (survey released Feb 2020); Dentistry Today reports 35% (2007) vs 47% (2019) for the same pair.
- Repeat victimization (2020): 26% once, 11% twice, 2% three times, 8% more than three.
- Prosperident's re-analysis: 17% discovered through planned controls (e.g., reviewing the software audit trail); 83% through chance events.
- Behavioral red flags: reluctance to cross-train (~50% of respondents), territorial about workspace (>25%), defensiveness, bullying.
- Loss size: ~$105,000 (Prosperident case data) / ~$109,000 (MDA); >$1B/year profession-wide (Harris, 'Healers Versus Stealers', 2023); 60-70% of dentists expected to be embezzled; some lecturers cite 90%.
- Services: Prosperident Owner Proactive Strategies, risk assessment questionnaire, PMS-report monitoring checklist, duty-separation rules; MDA-endorsed with discounts and free assessments.

## Notable quotes and data

- 'only 17% of embezzlement was discovered through the systems employed by practices, with a staggeringly high 83% revealed through unpredictable events.' — Prosperident.
