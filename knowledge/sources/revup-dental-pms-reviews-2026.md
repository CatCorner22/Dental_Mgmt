## Summary

A 10-platform review that aggregates Capterra, G2, GetApp, SoftwareAdvice, TrustRadius, Reddit r/Dentistry, and DentalTown feedback with named quotes. The richest single source of complaint themes; author is a marketing agency, and its price figures are estimates.

## Key concepts

- Dentrix: praised for insurance depth, hiring familiarity, 400+ integrations; complaints on support scheduling (updates Mon-Thu 5am-2pm), total cost, disruptive updates, click-heavy workflow, training model for multi-location.
- Eaglesoft: easiest to learn; complaints on decade-old UI, imaging resolution, non-Patterson devices, thin reporting (~$5,000 for custom reports), per-user pricing, Global Payments lock-in, proprietary imaging on exit.
- Open Dental: most-endorsed on r/Dentistry and DentalTown; complaints on ledger allocation logic, configuration burden, staff unfamiliarity, eServices add-ons, cloud version glitches, Canadian version; real cost with hosting and eServices ~$350-$500.
- Dentrix Ascend: remote access 100% positive sentiment; 77% of reporting mentions negative (Capterra, 234 reviews); fee-schedule setup, slow APIs, clicks, 'not ready for a busy practice'.
- Denticon: cloud/multi-location praised; complaints on fixed-percentage annual increases, ticket-only support, third-party integration failures, extra clicks, pediatric gaps, downtime.
- Curve: ease of use, CurveGRO, responsive support, single database; 2025 outages of 6+ hours and claims failures, x-ray problems, partial insurance payments, reporting, lost processor features, price increases; recent Reddit sentiment skews negative.
- CareStack: highest rating (4.8, 196 Capterra); 'operations loves it, accounting hates it'; transfer-adjustment ledger lines, dual-insurance glitches, separate imaging add-on, overwhelming reports, heavy owner setup, slipped features.
- DentiMax: value and 'real accounting' ledger; hold times, Windows-only, no mobile app, dated UI, thin features.
- tab32: all-in-one and fast development; support decline after 2026 AI pivot with per-use AI fees, x-ray limits, unreconciled reports, update bugs, data withheld until contract paid.
- Oryx: low latency, native imaging, built-in clearinghouse, Kois protocols; billing learning curve, AR includes estimated write-offs, paid training/conversion.
- Migration: 4-6 weeks without imaging, 6-8 with; conversion $800-$2,400; training $500-$2,000; ~$5,000 lost productivity; get data-exit terms in writing.
