## Summary

Where 'recommend and tailor internal controls' actually happens for small businesses today: inside accounting-system permission models and through CPA-led fraud risk assessments, not in a dedicated SMB product.

## Key concepts

- Core controls: separate authorization, recording, and custody; separate reconciliation from invoicing/payments; restrict who can change bank details; dual sign-off thresholds ($2,500-$5,000); mandatory vacations and duty rotation; least-privilege access.
- QuickBooks Enterprise: screen-level and bank-account-level user restrictions; audit-trail report that cannot be turned off; shared 'Administrator' logins defeat it; most small-business fraud is check tampering (Consero).
- Sage Intacct: named-user roles, prepare-vs-approve separation, full audit trail, AI anomaly detection; GAAP/IFRS/SOX-oriented.
- Red flags: refusal to take leave, missing/out-of-sequence documents, unexplained AR/AP adjustments, vendors with no footprint.
- ACFE 2024: over half of fraud cases linked to weak controls or management override; CPAs recommend forensic fraud risk assessments and outsourced accounting to achieve segregation where headcount is too small.
- Optro (vendor) and MindBridge (AI audit analytics) sell ML anomaly detection, aimed at audit teams and larger finance functions.
