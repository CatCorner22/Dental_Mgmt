## Summary

Pricing and positioning for the ERM/GRC tier, which mostly prices above small-business budgets; SimpleRisk is the exception with published tiers and a free core.

## Key concepts

- SimpleRisk (published): Core free and open source; Hosted $4,995/$9,995/$19,995 per year; On-Premise $9,995/$14,995/$19,995; discounts 10% logo, 10% two-year prepay, 20% three-year prepay; G2 'Starter Package' $5,000; users cite limited customization/integrations.
- LogicManager: fixed-fee unlimited users with advisory support; starts ~$10,000/yr (SelectHub) or $25K-$100K (trio.dev); vendor says ERM platforms run $20K-$40K; criticized as rigid, IT-governance-centric, limited API.
- Riskonnect: broadest ERM incl. insurable risk and claims; plans reported from ~$283,000/yr; enterprise only.
- Resolver, Archer, MetricStream: $50K-$200K+ custom; AuditBoard referred to as Optro in 2026 sources, audit/SOX-led; Workiva for SOX-grade reporting; LogicGate no-code workflows with AI questionnaire autofill.
- Diligent: board-reporting centric; AI Risk Essentials claims one-week setup with prebuilt templates and three-step workflows; reviewers cite learning curve and vendor-dependent report changes.
- IBM OpenPages SaaS Essentials indicative ~USD 3,300 (published); project-level tools $29-$49/user/month.
