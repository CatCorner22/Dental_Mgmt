## Summary

Open Dental's live fee schedule, the only complete published rate card among the top-tier dental PMS vendors. Captured because it anchors every cost comparison and because secondary sources drift from it.

## Key concepts

- US license and support: $199/month/location for 12 months, then the Current Reduced Rate published at month 13 (now $149); covers all computers per location for up to 3 providers; mobile units count as locations.
- Extra providers (excluding hygienists): $20/month/provider; volume 4-9 offices $169 -> $149; 10+ call management; prepay 5% (6 mo), 10% (1 yr), 15% (2-3 yr).
- Monthly fee includes telephone support and version updates; support required to buy most eServices.
- eServices: bundle $165; eClipboard $45 (+$20 OCR); eConfirmations $25/$40; Integrated Texting $20 access + per message; Secure Email $30; ODMobile $35; Web Sched Recall $75; Web Sched New/Existing $75; Web Sched ASAP $20 (free w/ Recall); ODTouch $85 (4 devices, +$20/device); Mass Email $8; AI Image Analysis $199/month/location.
- eRx: DoseSpot $57 w/ EPCS ($49 w/o) per prescriber regardless of locations; Ensora Basic $29 / Comprehensive $45 per prescriber per database + $45 identity proofing + $155/yr EPCS.
- Training: webinars free; online $80/hr; on-site $3,650 first day per trainer, $1,200 each additional day; post-conversion setup $160 if >30 days after conversion.
- Other: custom queries $110/hr; sheet design $55/hr; replication and after-hours support $100/hr; off-contract incident $150 first half-hour then $50.
- Free tiers: dental/hygiene/assisting schools; developing countries (limited support). Canada $164 -> $137; other countries $89.

## Notable quotes and data

- Fee increases occurred in 2008, 2015, 2018, 2022, 2026; 2003 prices were $139/$99; 2026 prices are ~19% and ~15% below 2003 prices adjusted for inflation (vendor statement).
- Any variation from published pricing must be in writing (per Medix quoting the site).
