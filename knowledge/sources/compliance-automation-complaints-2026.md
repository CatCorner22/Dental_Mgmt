## Summary

Recurring complaint themes for the leading compliance-automation platforms. Two of the sources are competitors, so weight SOC2Auditors and ComplianceRated more heavily.

## Key concepts

- Renewal shock is the dominant complaint: Vanta +40-100% after first-year discounts; Drata +20-40% at year two on headcount tiers; a shared example of $7,500 -> $20,000 to add two frameworks; a competitor cites >150%.
- Quote-only pricing blocks budgeting; SCIM gated behind unnamed add-ons (Vanta).
- 'Checkbox compliance': control tests 'often not comprehensive'; limited real security impact.
- Automation gap: ~50-60% coverage on on-prem/custom stacks vs 70-80% cloud-native; DevOps effort to keep integrations alive (Drata).
- Vanta: rigid policy templates, billing clarity, usage caps; Drata: longer setup; both: audit paid separately.
- Praise: Drata customer-success responsiveness (4.7/5, 1,331 G2 reviews); Vanta integration breadth (375-400+) and auditor familiarity.
- Shelfware risk if one person owns compliance.

## Notable quotes and data
